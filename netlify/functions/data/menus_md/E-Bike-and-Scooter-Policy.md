## Page 1

IMPORTANT

E BIKES & E SCOOTERS
   E-bikes and e-scooters are not
  permitted on the hotel premises.
Charging of e-bikes and e-scooters is
strictly prohibited due to fire safety
  risks from lithium-ion batteries.
The hotel is unable to provide storage
 facilities for e-bikes or e-scooters.
We thank you for your understanding
and cooperation in keeping our hotel
        safe for everyone.

         Hotel Management
