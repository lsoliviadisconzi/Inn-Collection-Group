Inn Collection Group pubs shortlisted for national awards
Fondly nicknamed “the Oscars of the Pub industry,” The Great British Pub Awards, run by leading trade magazine the
Morning Advertiser
, are the stand-out event for pub operators throughout the country.
The group’s popular seaside pub with rooms The Bamburgh Castle Inn, which overlooks the harbour in the seaside resort of Seahouses, Northumberland is up for both the Best Inn and Best Freehouse categories.
General manager of The Bamburgh Castle Inn Simon Lumsden said: “To be shortlisted for one award is amazing – but to be a finalist in two categories of The Great British Pub Awards is absolutely phenomenal!
“These are the pub awards for the people and are seen as the awards to win in the industry. Reaching the finals reflects an entire team effort here at The Bamburgh Castle Inn and our commitment to providing customers with a special place whether that’s to stay overnight, enjoy a pint or dine on quality home made pub fare – all in an environment and location that’s second to none.”
Cleveland pub with rooms The King’s Head Inn has also been shortlisted as a finalist in the Best for Families category in the awards, which drew in hundreds of entries from pubs across the UK.
The family-friendly inn has recently invested in a £100k playbarn and offers special walking breaks for families along with a raft of activities to keep little ones happy.
General manager of The King’s Head Inn Phil Harris said: “We are delighted to be named as finalists for the awards, which are known in the industry as the ones to win, especially for a category which is so close to our hearts.
“Happy children means happy parents, and as a dad myself I know how important it is to spend quality family time together, but it can be tricky finding somewhere to keep the kids entertained with space to play so everyone has a great time.
“We wanted to create a family-friendly offering for our customers, where parents know their children are happy and secure which is why we’ve invested in facilities including our new play barn which is priceless for letting the kids work off some energy and have fun while you relax with a drink and take your time over your meal.”
The Inn Collection Group is no stranger to awards success. It was named as best Pub Employer (up to 500 employees) in the 2018 Publican Awards.
The Great British Pub Awards take place in London on September 5.
