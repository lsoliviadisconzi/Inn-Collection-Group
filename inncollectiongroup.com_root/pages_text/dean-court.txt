Experience the perfect blend of historic charm and modern comfort at Dean Court Hotel York, a standout hotel in York located in the very heart of the city. Nestled within the ancient city walls and just steps from the magnificent York Minster, Dean Court offers a relaxed and welcoming stay for couples, families, and visitors looking to explore everything this beautiful city has to offer.
Step inside to enjoy cosy, characterful rooms and a warm pub atmosphere, where you can savour delicious pub fare, meet friends for drinks, or simply unwind after a day exploring the city. With its prime location a short walk from York Railway Station, Dean Court puts you close to iconic attractions including The Shambles, riverside walks, world-famous Betty’s Tea Rooms, and award-winning museums like the Jorvik Viking Centre and the National Railway Museum.
As a central York hotel, Dean Court is the ideal base for discovering the city’s rich history, vibrant culture, and scenic streets, all while enjoying the comfort and charm of an inn that truly makes you feel at home.

Dog Friendly Hotel in York
For dog friendly accommodation in York, discover Dean Court! We offer dog-friendly rooms across all room categories so your four-legged friend can join you for your city break. Just add your dog when booking and we’ll make sure you’re allocated a dog-friendly room.
When you book direct, up to two dogs can stay for £15 per night. They’re also welcome in our designated dog-friendly pub and restaurant areas, with water bowls and treats ready and waiting. Dogs can’t be left unattended in bedrooms during your stay, but can join you every step of the way.
With York city just waiting to be explored, you can discover all the dog friendly cafes and parks before settling in for a cosy night where none of the family has to get left behind.
Whether you’re looking for dog friendly places to stay in York or a dog friendly pub with rooms in York city, Dean Court is a great base for a getaway with your pooch.

Dean Court FAQs
Planning a stay at Dean Court in the heart of York and just steps away from York Mister? Our FAQs will provide you with essential details on location, parking, accessibility, check-in times and guest amenities.
Do you have on site parking available?
There is valet car parking available at Dean Court for £25 per night. All our drivers are fully insured.

What time is Check in and Check out?
Check in at Dean Court is from 3pm and check out is 10:30am.

Is there disabled access and accessible rooms at the inn?
There is an accessible lift from the pavement to the Dean Court reception area and level access to the bar and restaurant. There are two accessible rooms available at Dean Court.
Please note the total height of the bed and the mattress approximately 70cm.
For further accessibility information please call our reservations team.

Are you dog-friendly?
Your dogs are more than welcome at Dean Court! We have designated dog-friendly areas of the pub and restaurant, with water bowls and treats available – including our selection of Sir Woofchester’s dog food range.
We also offer a limited number of pet-friendly hotel rooms to stay over with your pet. Please let us know whether you will be bringing your dog at the time of booking to ensure availability and so we can allocate your room accordingly.
When you book direct, up to two of your four-legged friend can be added to your booking for £15 per night. Another perk of booking direct with us at The Inn Collection Group!
For more information on our pet-friendly accommodation, please contact our central reservations team on 0191 580 3610.

What is the cancellation policy?
Our standard cancellation policy is as follows:
You may cancel your booking without any payment to us at any point before 3pm on the day that is two days before the day your booking starts. After this deadline, the whole booking becomes fully non-refundable. If you try to cancel after this deadline (or if you don’t arrive for your booking), you will still be charged the full price for your booking (including any meals or extras).
Our Advance Purchase Rate cancellation policy is as follows: All bookings on an advance purchase rate are non-refundable and cannot be cancelled by you. If you try to cancel a booking on an advance purchase rate (or if you don’t arrive for your booking), you will still be charged the full price for your booking (including any meals or extras).
Our special offers/promotions cancellation policy is as follows: The cancellation rights for bookings offered on a special offer will be stated during the booking process and in your booking confirmation. Where we do not state any cancellation rights for a special offer, the advance purchase rate cancellation terms (above) will apply.
Close
Do you have any on site bike storage?
There is no on-site bike storage available at Dean Court.
Please note E-bikes and e-scooters are permitted on our premises provided the battery has been removed before arrival and is stored at least 10 metres away from the building. E-bikes and e-scooters must not be charged anywhere on our premises.
We are unable to provide storage facilities for e-bike or e-scooter batteries. The bike or scooter may be stored on site without its battery fitted.
