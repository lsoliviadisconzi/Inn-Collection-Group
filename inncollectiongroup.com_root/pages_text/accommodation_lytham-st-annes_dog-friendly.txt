Dog-friendly fun in Lytham St Annes
Wondering what to get up to if you choose to bring your beloved dog with you to Lytham St Annes? We hear you. Here’s our insider’s guide to the best activities to plan in for your stay.
Roam miles of beaches
Fylde beach and St Anne’s beach are both wonderful options for walking your dog. The sand dunes of Fylde beach are particularly fun to race over, however St Anne’s beach is more convenient, being right on the shoreline of Lytham. Conveniently, it’s also where our dog friendly Lytham St Annes hotel
Offshore
is based. The only thing to watch for is the main frontage of St Anne’s beach. Take a sunrise or sunset walk to enjoy the fantastic colours over the water.
Stroll the parks of Lytham
Lytham St Annes is well known for its beautifully kept parks. Wander through the lush scenery of Ashton Gardens and Lowther Gardens with your dog. Well behaved dogs are welcome to join you on a lead!
Promenade the pier together
Lytham St Anne’s pleasure pier
has been standing since the Victorian era. This uniquely British attraction cuts a striking figure on the waterline of St Anne’s beach. Dogs are welcome on the pier, so why not take a evening stroll together and enjoy the unparalleled views from on the water’s edge.
Lytham St Annes also makes a brilliant base for seeking out beautiful dog-friendly walking spots across the rest of Lancashire.
