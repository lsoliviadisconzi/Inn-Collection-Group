Located in the heart of Ambleside, The Ambleside Inn is a traditional Lakeland building with over 300 years of history, offering characterful Ambleside accommodation in one of the Lake District’s most popular towns. With 29 comfortable rooms, it’s the perfect Lake District inn for couples, families and walkers looking for a central base with everything close by.
A welcoming pub with rooms in Ambleside, the inn serves hearty food and drinks throughout the day, creating a relaxed space to unwind after exploring. Dog-friendly rooms make it ideal for those travelling with pets, while the surrounding area offers direct access to shops, Stock Ghyll Force, and a short walk to Lake Windermere.

Dog Friendly Hotel in Ambleside
The Ambleside Inn is a dog friendly hotel in Ambleside, with dog-friendly rooms where your four-legged friend can join you for a Lake District adventure. Simply add your dog when booking to make sure you’re allocated a dog-friendly room.
When you book direct, up to two dogs can stay with you for £15 per night. Dogs are also welcome in our designated dog-friendly areas of the pub and restaurant, with water bowls and treats ready and waiting. Please remember that dogs can’t be left unattended in bedrooms during your stay, but can join you every step of the way!
When it’s time to get those paws moving, you’re perfectly placed for exploring Ambleside and the surrounding Lake District, with scenic walks and countryside right on the doorstep.
Whether you’re looking for dog friendly places to stay in Ambleside or a dog friendly pub with rooms in Ambleside, the Ambleside Inn makes the perfect base for exploring the Lake District together.

What is the cancellation policy?
Our standard cancellation policy is as follows:
You may cancel your booking without any payment to us at any point before 3pm on the day that is two days before the day your booking starts. After this deadline, the whole booking becomes fully non-refundable. If you try to cancel after this deadline (or if you don’t arrive for your booking), you will still be charged the full price for your booking (including any meals or extras).
Our Advance Purchase Rate cancellation policy is as follows: All bookings on an advance purchase rate are non-refundable and cannot be cancelled by you. If you try to cancel a booking on an advance purchase rate (or if you don’t arrive for your booking), you will still be charged the full price for your booking (including any meals or extras).
Our special offers/promotions cancellation policy is as follows: The cancellation rights for bookings offered on a special offer will be stated during the booking process and in your booking confirmation. Where we do not state any cancellation rights for a special offer, the advance purchase rate cancellation terms (above) will apply.

Do you have any on site bike storage?
We have no official storage at The Ambleside Inn however there may be space to store in cupboards, subject to availability.

We are unable to provide storage facilities for e-bike or e-scooter batteries. The bike or scooter may be stored on site without its battery fitted.

Are you family friendly?
Yes, The Ambleside Inn is a welcoming choice for families looking for family-friendly accommodation in the Lake District. We offer comfortable family rooms and a relaxed pub atmosphere where guests of all ages can feel at home.
Our children’s menu includes a range of tasty favourites, making dining easy and enjoyable for younger guests. The inn is also perfectly located for family adventures, with nearby activities such as lakeside walks on Lake Windermere, gentle woodland trails, and exciting local attractions like Stock Ghyll Force waterfall. Ambleside itself is a great base for families, offering ice cream shops, boat trips, and safe walking routes all within easy reach.

What walks are near the inn?
The Ambleside Inn is perfectly placed for a wide range of walking holidays in the Lake District, with routes suitable for all abilities starting right from the town. One of the most popular local walks is Loughrigg Fell, offering rewarding panoramic views over Ambleside, Lake Windermere and Grasmere. For something shorter, the walk to Stock Ghyll Force waterfall takes you through woodland paths to one of the area’s most impressive cascades.
You can also enjoy peaceful lakeside walks along Lake Windermere, heading towards Waterhead or Borrans Park, both ideal for relaxed strolls. More experienced walkers can explore longer routes towards Rydal Water, Grasmere, and Wansfell Pike, each offering stunning scenery, varied terrain, and classic Lake District views. With so many options on your doorstep, The Ambleside Inn is an ideal base for walking breaks in Ambleside.
Close
How close are you to Lake Windermere?
The Ambleside Inn is just a short 10–15 minute walk from Lake Windermere, making it one of the most convenient places to stay in Ambleside for lakeside access. You can easily stroll down to the shoreline, enjoy boat trips, or explore the surrounding viewpoints. This central location makes us an ideal Lake District hotel near Windermere for both relaxing breaks and active getaways.
