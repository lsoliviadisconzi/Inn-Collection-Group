Pub Food & Drink Across Our Inns
At The Inn Collection Group, we believe good food should be easy to enjoy. That’s why we offer all-day dining, serving food every day from 7:30am to 9pm*. From relaxed pub lunches to comforting pub dinners, there is something to suit every appetite. There’s no need to book either, you can simply walk in, settle down, and enjoy great pub food whenever it suits you.
With 30+ welcoming pubs with food across the North East, Yorkshire, the Lake District, Lancashire and North Wales, our menus are all about hearty favourites, seasonal dishes and comforting classics.
All our dishes are freshly prepared by our talented chefs using quality ingredients from trusted suppliers, all served in a warm, friendly setting. From family-friendly pubs to dog-friendly inns, you’ll always find a relaxed atmosphere, good food, and a genuine welcome waiting for you.
