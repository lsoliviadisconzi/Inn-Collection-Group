Our Story
At The Inn Collection Group, we embrace our pub roots with pride, creating warm and welcoming inns where everyone feels at home. Whether you're here to eat, drink, sleep, or set off on your next adventure, you'll fit right inn. Where we began…
The Inn Collection Group was first formed in 2013, but can trace its roots back to 2006 and The Lindisfarne Inn on the Northumberland coast.
Under the watch of founder Keith Liddell, the Beal venue was joined by The Bamburgh Castle Inn at Seahouses, The Hog’s Head Inn at Alnwick and The King’s Head Inn at Newton-under-Roseberry to launch what has gone on to become a multi-award-winning hospitality group.
Now comprising of 32 establishments across Northumberland, Tyne & Wear, County Durham, North Yorkshire, Lancashire, the Lake District and North Wales, the company has grown remarkably since 2018 as it has acquired stunning properties in welcoming locations.
Originally rooted in Northumberland, The Inn Collection Group moved its central support hub to Newcastle Upon Tyne in 2022, remaining proudly northern at heart.
