Things to do in North Wales
Planning a visit to North Wales? From the rugged peaks of Snowdonia (Eryri National Park) to its sweeping coastline and historic market towns, it's is one of the UK’s most breathtaking holiday destinations, packed with outdoor adventures, iconic castles, scenic walks and unforgettable attractions.
Snowdonia
Famous for it's mountains, natural beauty, wildlife and cultural heritage. Snowdonia National Park is the largest in Wales.
