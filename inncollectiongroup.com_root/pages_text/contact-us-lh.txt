Our standard cancellation policy is as follows: You may cancel your booking without any payment to us at any point before 3pm on the day that is two days before the day your booking starts. If you try to cancel after this deadline (or if you don’t arrive for your booking), you will still be charged the full price for your booking (including any meals or extras).
Our Advance Purchase Rate cancellation policy is as follows: All bookings on an advance purchase rate are non-refundable and cannot be cancelled by you. If you try to cancel a booking on an advance purchase rate (or if you don’t arrive for your booking), you will still be charged the full price for your booking (including any meals or extras). Our special offers/promotions cancellation policy is as follows: The cancellation rights for bookings offered on a special offer will be stated during the booking process and in your booking confirmation. Where we do not state any cancellation rights for a special offer, the advance purchase rate cancellation terms (above) will apply.

Do dogs need to be on leads around livestock?
It’s good practice to keep your dog on a lead around livestock, and there are situations where it is a legal requirement. On open access land, dogs must be kept on a lead no more than two metres long around livestock, while between 1 March and 31 July they must also be on a lead on open access land to protect ground-nesting birds.

Can dogs swim in lakes and rivers in the Lake District?
Some dogs can safely enjoy a paddle or swim in the Lake District’s lakes and rivers, but always check the conditions first. Avoid fast-flowing water, steep banks, areas with strong currents and water where blue-green algae is present for the health of your dog. Keep an eye on your dog around deep water and remember that not every stretch of lakeshore is suitable for swimming. A doggy towel is also a good idea if your pup can’t resist a splash!

What used to be The Northallerton Inn?
The Northallerton Inn is a Grade II listed Georgian building with a fascinating history. Over the years, it has served as a private mansion, library, local authority office and, for 30 years, Northallerton Police Station. The Inn Collection Group purchased the building in November 2020 and, following an 18-month, multi-million-pound restoration, opened it as The Northallerton Inn in 2022.
Many of the building’s original features, including the impressive front entrance and main staircase, have been carefully preserved, while the former police custody suite has been transformed into our welcoming restaurant and bar. Today, it’s a place where guests can eat, drink, stay and experience a unique piece of Northallerton’s history.

When is the best time to visit Northumberland?
Northumberland is beautiful at any time of year, and each season brings its own charm. Spring and summer are ideal for exploring the county’s dramatic coastline and historic castles, with longer days and milder weather perfect for beach walks in places like Seahouses or Bamburgh. You’ll find coastal towns full of life, colourful harbours, and great spots to enjoy fresh seafood by the sea.
Autumn is a wonderful time to visit if you prefer quieter surroundings. The landscapes around Alnwick and inland villages glow with golden colours, and it’s still mild enough for long walks and exploring hidden trails. Winter has its own magic too – think cosy fires, hearty pub food, and peaceful beaches almost entirely to yourself.
Whenever you choose to visit, Northumberland’s beauty never fades. Staying at one of our inns means you’re perfectly placed to make the most of every season – from sunny days on the coast to crisp countryside mornings, with a warm welcome waiting at the end of the day.

What are the top things to do in North Wales?
North Wales offers a wealth of experiences, from thrilling adventures to historic sites and stunning natural beauty. Here are some top attractions to consider:
Adventure and Thrills
Zip World Penrhyn Quarry
: Home to the world’s fastest zip line, Velocity 2, and other exhilarating activities like Quarry Karts and the underground trampoline park at Zip World Llechwedd in Blaenau Ffestiniog.
Snowdon (Yr Wyddfa)
: Climb Wales’ highest peak or take the Snowdon Mountain Railway for breathtaking views.
Historic Sites
Conwy Castle
: A UNESCO World Heritage Site offering stunning views and rich history.
Beaumaris Castle
: A beautifully preserved fortress located on Anglesey.
Natural Beauty
Swallow Falls
: A picturesque waterfall near Betws-y-Coed, ideal for a peaceful walk.
Newborough Sands
: A serene beach on Anglesey, perfect for relaxation and coastal walks.
Seaside Charm
Llandudno Pier
: The longest pier in Wales, offering traditional seaside amusements and stunning views.
Portmeirion Village
: A unique Italianate village set in beautiful gardens, offering a blend of architecture and nature.
Whether you’re seeking adventure, history, or relaxation, North Wales has something for everyone.

What is the cancellation policy?
Our standard cancellation policy is as follows: You may cancel your booking without any payment to us at any point before 3pm on the day that is two days before the day your booking starts. After this deadline, the whole booking becomes fully non-refundable. If you try to cancel after this deadline (or if you don’t arrive for your booking), you will still be charged the full price for your booking (including any meals or extras).
Our Advance Purchase Rate cancellation policy is as follows: All bookings on an advance purchase rate are non-refundable and cannot be cancelled by you. If you try to cancel a booking on an advance purchase rate (or if you don’t arrive for your booking), you will still be charged the full price for your booking (including any meals or extras). Our special offers/promotions cancellation policy is as follows: The cancellation rights for bookings offered on a special offer will be stated during the booking process and in your booking confirmation. Where we do not state any cancellation rights for a special offer, the advance purchase rate cancellation terms (above) will apply.

We offer pet-friendly hotel rooms, so your dogs can join you on overnight stays. Please let us know whether you will be bringing a pet at the time of booking to ensure availability, and so we can make sure you are allocated to out pet-friendly rooms.
When you book direct, up to two of your four-legged friend can be added to your booking for £15 per night. Another perk of booking direct with us at The Inn Collection Group!
For more information on our pet-friendly accommodation, please contact our central reservations team on 0191 580 3610.

Do you have any on site bike storage?
There is no bike storage available on site at The Ripon Inn.
Please note E-bikes and e-scooters are permitted on our premises provided the battery has been removed before arrival and is stored at least 10 metres away from the building. E-bikes and e-scooters must not be charged anywhere on our premises.

Do you have on site parking available?
We offer complimentary parking at The Ripon Inn car park to the rear of the inn for guests only.  Space is limited on a first come, first served basis and we do not under any circumstances reserve or guarantee parking spaces.  Complimentary parking in our car park is only valid either when you are staying at our inn and checked in to a room or are a customer in the bar, restaurant or attending a function. Overnight parking is available to guests staying at the hotel in one of our bedrooms only.
You
MUST
register your vehicle registration number into the tablet on reception if you are a bar, restaurant or function customer.  If you are an accommodation guest, then please register your car registration number with reception.
Diners – 2 hours FREE
Overnight Guests – 24 hours FREE for each night of your stay
Charges apply to ALL other car park users – please remember to pay for your parking at the car park pay and display machine, via phone or prebook with RingGo. No overnight camping allowed.

What is the cancellation policy?
Our standard cancellation policy is as follows:
You may cancel your booking without any payment to us at any point before 3pm on the day that is two days before the day your booking starts. After this deadline, the whole booking becomes fully non-refundable. If you try to cancel after this deadline (or if you don’t arrive for your booking), you will still be charged the full price for your booking (including any meals or extras).
Our Advance Purchase Rate cancellation policy is as follows: All bookings on an advance purchase rate are non-refundable and cannot be cancelled by you. If you try to cancel a booking on an advance purchase rate (or if you don’t arrive for your booking), you will still be charged the full price for your booking (including any meals or extras).
Our special offers/promotions cancellation policy is as follows: The cancellation rights for bookings offered on a special offer will be stated during the booking process and in your booking confirmation. Where we do not state any cancellation rights for a special offer, the advance purchase rate cancellation terms (above) will apply.
