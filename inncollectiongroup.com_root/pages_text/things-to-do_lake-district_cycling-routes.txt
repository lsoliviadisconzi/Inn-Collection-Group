Cycle Routes in the Lake District
If you’re on the hunt for Lake District Cycle Routes for a fun activity for the whole family, this is a region renowned for its scenic beauty and picturesque landscapes and is just asking to be explored.
So for avid cyclists and casual riders alike, here’s our list of memorable biking adventures all within easy reach of our
Lake District accommodations
for your convenience.
So let’s explore the best bike rides, cycle routes, and paths the Lake District has to offer, so you can make the most of your cycling experience in this mesmerising national park.
Discover these Lake District Cycle Routes
A UNESCO World Heritage Site, cycle paths offer a wealth of places to go and
things to do in the Lake District
. This region is a mash-up of terrain with a wide variety of sights, boasting a unique combination of natural beauty like tranquil lakes, towering cliffs and rugged mountains – with picturesque villages as welcome waypoints dotted in between.
Bikes allow you to take in more of the landscape in a shorter time, but if you find somewhere special, feel free to hop off and take it all in.
Windermere Cycle Route – Ideal for Family Bike Rides
Distance:
Approximately 15 miles
Difficulty:
Easy
Highlights:
A serene ride around Windermere, England’s largest natural lake. Perfect for a family outing, this route provides breathtaking views of the water and the surrounding fells. It’s an excellent choice for beginners and those looking for a leisurely experience as there’s not a big difference in elevation and allows for a relaxing experience.

Grizedale Forest Trail – Thrilling Mountain Biking
Distance:
Various trails for all levels
Difficulty:
Moderate
Highlights:
Grizedale Forest is great for mountain biking enthusiasts setting off specifically for exciting bike trails and less so for a relaxing time. Explore well-maintained trails that wind through the forest, offering a mix of terrains. It’s an ideal spot for both mountain biking and forest cycling. If you’re seeking woodland adventure with a bike ride through Grizedale Forest,
The Coniston Inn
is a great place to set out from.
Honister Pass Challenge – A Challenging Ascent and Views
Distance:
Approximately 11 miles
Difficulty:
Difficult
Highlights:
The Honister Pass Challenge is for experienced cyclists seeking a heart-pounding adventure. This mountainous route offers a thrilling ascent and rewards you with breathtaking panoramic views. It’s a must-try for seasoned mountain bikers with good fitness levels and stamina looking to test their mettle.
Keswick to Borrowdale – Stunning Valley Scenery
Distance:
Approximately 18 miles
Difficulty:
Moderate
Highlights:
The Keswick to Borrowdale Loop takes you through the dramatic Borrowdale Valley which offers gorgeous views and a pleasant experience in either direction. Along the way, you’ll pass through charming Keswick and have the opportunity to explore the famous Honister Slate Mine. Many puddles can appear when it rains so bear this in mind.
Coast to Coast Route – Epic Long-Distance Adventure
Distance:
Approximately 140 miles
Difficulty:
Challenging
Highlights:
The Coast to Coast (C2C) route is an epic long-distance journey that takes you through some of the Lake District’s most spectacular scenery and is a thrilling challenge to share with friends. While it covers a vast stretch of northern England, the Lake District section is a highlight.
Scenic Beauty and Diverse Terrain
The Lake District offers a variety of cycle paths and trails, each with its own distinctive charm. From serene lakeshores to challenging mountain ascents, the Lake District has something to offer the whole family regardless of your cycling experience.
Charming Villages and Cultural Heritage
In between thrilling bike rides, take time to explore the charming villages of the Lake District. Each village has its unique character and attractions. You can also immerse yourself in the rich cultural heritage of the area. The landscapes that inspired
renowned poets like William Wordsworth
and
storytellers like Beatrix Potter
are yours to discover.
Planning Your Lake District Cycling Adventure
With the information you’ve gathered on the recommended cycle routes, it’s time to start planning your Lake District cycling adventure:
Maps and Navigation
The Lake District can be extensive and complex, so it’s essential to have maps, GPS devices, or even consider guided cycling tours to keep you on the right path.
Arrange Your Accommodation

Local Cuisine
Speaking of hearty pub meals, the Inn Collection accommodations all use locally sourced fresh ingredients for their meals. You’ll be well-rewarded by our kitchens after a day of exploring the Lake District.
Enjoy traditional British dishes at local pubs, taste artisan cheeses, and savour freshly baked goods. A delightful way to refuel after a day of cycling.
Bike Rental
If you don’t have your own bike, there are plenty of options for bike hire in the Lake District. From traditional bikes to mountain bikes, e-bikes, and other specialised equipment, you can easily find the right set of wheels for your adventure.
Many rental shops are conveniently located near popular cycling routes, ensuring a hassle-free experience.
Safety and Equipment
Safety should always be a top priority. Ensure your bike is in good working condition, wear appropriate safety gear, and carry essentials like a puncture repair kit, first aid supplies, and enough water.
Exploring Lake District’s Hidden Gems
In addition to the recommended cycling routes, the Lake District holds many hidden gems waiting to be discovered:
Lake District Wildlife
The region is
home to diverse wildlife
, including red squirrels, roe deer, and a variety of bird species. Keep your eyes peeled as you cycle through the countryside, especially during the early morning and late evening.
Tarns and Waterfalls
While the major lakes are popular, don’t forget to explore the lesser-known tarns (small mountain lakes) and waterfalls. These serene spots provide perfect picnic locations and are often less crowded if you’re on the hunt for more tranquil exploration.
Historical Sites
Discover the Lake District’s rich history by visiting historical sites such as Castlerigg Stone Circle, a prehistoric monument with stunning mountain views.
Explore Wray Castle
and the Roman fortifications near Ambleside for a glimpse into the past.
Museums and Galleries
The Lake District boasts a selection of museums and galleries. Explore the Wordsworth Museum in Grasmere to learn about the poet William Wordsworth’s life and works. Visit
The Beatrix Potter Gallery in Hawkshead
to witness original personal items, illustrations, letters and other artefacts and to delve into the world of the beloved author and illustrator.
A Different Lake District with Every Season
The Lake District’s beauty and cycling experiences vary with the seasons:
Spring
Spring is a fantastic time for cycling as the landscapes burst into life with vibrant greenery and blooming wildflowers. The weather is generally milder, and there are fewer tourists, making it an excellent time for a peaceful ride.
Summer
Summer offers long daylight hours and pleasant temperatures, making it the most popular time for cycling. However, be prepared for more crowds and book accommodations well in advance.
Autumn
Autumn is a stunning time to visit, with the landscape transforming into a symphony of reds, oranges, and yellows. The crisp air and quieter trails make for a memorable cycling experience.
Winter
While the Lake District’s winter weather can be unpredictable, it’s a magical time for cycling if you’re prepared for the cold. The snow-dusted fells and quiet trails offer a visual reward and you can warm yourself through at one of our warm, cosy accommodations.
Stay Safe and Respect Nature
Before you embark on your cycling adventure, remember to check the weather forecast, and let someone know your plans. Ensure you have suitable clothing, spare inner tubes, and tools for minor bike repairs. Most importantly, always respect the local environment, wildlife, and fellow cyclists.
The Lake District’s cycling routes are a gateway to unforgettable adventures and breathtaking vistas. Whether you’re seeking an easy lakeshore ride, a challenging mountain ascent, or a long-distance cycling odyssey, the Lake District has it all.
So, dust off your bike, plan your journey, and get ready to immerse yourself in the natural beauty of this remarkable region.
