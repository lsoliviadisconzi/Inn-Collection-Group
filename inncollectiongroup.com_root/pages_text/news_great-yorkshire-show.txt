Great Yorkshire Showground
Where is the Great Yorkshire Show held?
The Great Yorkshire Show takes place every year in Harrogate at the Great Yorkshire Showground, home to the Great Yorkshire Events Centre. Spanning an impressive 250 acres, the venue provides plenty of space for the UK's largest agricultural show, with a mix of expansive outdoor areas and indoor event spaces.
The Great Yorkshire Showground has been purpose-built to host large-scale events and welcomes hundreds of thousands of visitors each year. Its spacious layout makes it easy to navigate between the different areas of the show, while the combination of indoor and outdoor facilities ensures the event can run smoothly whatever the weather. After all, you can never be too prepared when it comes to a British summer!
How to spend your day at The Great Yorkshire Show
The Great Yorkshire Show is packed with attractions, demonstrations and experiences, making it easy to spend the week exploring everything on offer. From world-class livestock competitions and impressive machinery displays, to delicious food and drink stalls, shopping opportunities and family-friendly entertainment, there’s something for every visitor to enjoy.

Do I need to book Great Yorkshire Show tickets in advance?
If you are looking to visit The Great Yorkshire Show for 2026, you must pre-book your tickets, as no tickets are available for purchase on the gate. Is parking available at The Great Yorkshire Show?
Yes! The Great Yorkshire Show offers plenty of free parking for visitors, making it easy and convenient if you’re travelling from further afield. With ample space available on-site, you can arrive stress-free and focus on enjoying your day at the event. With the event being as popular as ever, it is advised to arrive early to help avoid traffic.
Are dogs allowed at The Great Yorkshire Show?
Unfortunately, your four-legged friends are not allowed at The Great Yorkshire Show which enforces a strict no-dog policy, with the exceptions of assistance dogs. This is to help maintain the safety of the event, with livestock, horses, and the thousands of visitors attending making it an unsuitable place to be able to walk your dog. With so much happening across the Showground, it also helps prevent any disturbance to the animals, keeping the experience calm and enjoyable for both exhibitors and guests alike.
