New Customer Loyalty App Unveiled by The Inn Collection Group
29th July 2025
The Inn Collection Group is excited to announce the launch of a new customer loyalty programme and app where visitors can utilise exclusive perks and rewards ahead of summer.
You can now download The Inn Collection Group app and e
arn Inn Crowd points not just for every night you stay, but for every bite and sip too, as well as being rewarded with surprise treats, special offers, and perks.
Users will be able to earn points at any of their 30 warm and welcoming inns by scanning the QR code available in the app at the till point.
To celebrate the launch, and as an introductory offer, The Inn Collection Group will be giving all users 10% off their next stay for a limited time, redeemable in the app. The app is free to join, and users can immediately start scanning to collect points and receive access to their personalised Inn Crowd offers, discounts and perks.
Sean Donkin, Managing Director, The Inn Collection Group said:
“We’re excited to be launching our new app and rewarding our loyal customers with exclusive offers and points just for doing what they already love. The Inn Crowd is our way of saying thank you for their continued support, and we can’t wait for guests to start enjoying the benefits of joining The Inn Crowd just in time for summer.”
For the launch, the pubs with rooms operator has partnered with leading app developer Pepper, known for their work with Itsu, Rick Stein, and Tortilla, to deliver a platform built with customer data security and privacy at its core.
