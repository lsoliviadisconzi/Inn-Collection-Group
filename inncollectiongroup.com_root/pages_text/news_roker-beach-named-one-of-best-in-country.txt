Roker Beach Named One of UK’s Best
4th August 2025
Sunderland’s coastline is making waves, with Roker Beach officially named by The Sunday Times as one of the top 50 beaches in the UK. This well-deserved recognition puts a spotlight on one of the North East’s most beloved seaside destinations – known for its striking views, clean sands, and vibrant local character.
The sheltered waters created by the iconic northern pier make Roker Beach an ideal spot to experience the North Sea up close. Thanks to its natural protection from strong currents and waves, the bay offers a safer and more inviting setting for swimmers and paddlers.

The beach has been rated ‘Excellent’ for water quality too, so if you fancy a dip, you’re in good hands - even if the North Sea gives you a bit of a wake-up call!
After a stroll along the sand or a brisk dip in the sea, there’s nothing better than warming up with a good coffee or grabbing a bite with a view - and Roker doesn’t disappoint. The seafront is lined with a growing collection of independent cafes, cosy coffee shops, and relaxed eateries, all perfect for refuelling after some time by the water.
The charm of Roker Beach extends well beyond the shoreline. The surrounding area is packed with character, scenery, and plenty to explore at a leisurely pace. A stroll along the promenade offers more than just sea views – it’s a scenic route lined with golden sands, quirky beachfront cafés, traditional seaside pubs, and a welcoming atmosphere that reflects the spirit of the North East. Make sure to view the local landmarks including the peaceful Roker Park, perfect for a shaded walk or picnic, and the historic Roker Lighthouse stood proudly at the end of the pier.
There’s also a fantastic network of walking and cycling routes waiting to be explored, stretching along the stretch of North East coast. These trails link effortlessly with nearby Seaburn Beach, offering an easy way to extend your seaside adventure and take in even more of Sunderland’s stunning coastal scenery.
Seaburn Bay viewed from The Seaburn Inn
Want to make a stay of it and experience this iconic seaside town in style? The Seaburn Inn is just north of the top-ranked beach, with views stretching over golden beaches and refreshing sea-breeze straight at your doorstep.
So, if you needed an excuse to visit this North East gem, consider this your invitation! With Roker Beach up there with the UK’s coastal greats, get your bags packed and see what the fuss is about...
