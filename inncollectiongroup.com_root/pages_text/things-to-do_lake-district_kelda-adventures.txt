Fancy exploring the Lake District from the saddle? Kelda Adventures offers premium guided road cycling tours around some of the region's most spectacular climbs and passes, led by a qualified Ride Leader with Mountain First Aid training. Choose from signature multi-day routes taking in legendary climbs like the Fred Whitton, or a fully bespoke tour built around what you want to ride and see — every group is small and private, so the pace and route are shaped entirely around you.
Kelda Logo (White)_edited (1)
If you'd rather ride with company, Kelda also runs regular shared Group Rides for those wanting a flexible, sociable way to explore the Lakes on two wheels. Whether you're after a serious challenge or a relaxed day taking in the views, Kelda Adventures will get you there. If you fancy trying it out for yourself, head to their website below!
