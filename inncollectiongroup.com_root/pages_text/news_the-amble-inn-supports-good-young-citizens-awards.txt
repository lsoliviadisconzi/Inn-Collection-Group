The Amble Inn supports Good Young Citizens Awards
The Amble Inn donated prizes for youngsters nominated for their exceptional behaviour and outstanding attitude both in and out of school.
The main prize of a bike was donated by one of our favourite suppliers – Spurreli Boutique Ice Cream – won by Ollie Dryden from Amble Links Primary School.
Archie Chisholm of Warkworth Primary and Owen Gough of Amble First were also nominated. All the youngsters received trophies and goodie bags.
Community Support Officer Darin Fawcett has organised the awards for the last ten years. He said “I’m so pleased it has run this long, but it wouldn’t have done so without the community and the support of everyone else.”
The Amble Inn’s general manager Chris Taylor said: “What a great way to reward children for their successes. We were proud to support the awards and give a pat on the back to these youngsters who have gone above and beyond to make a difference in our community by being outstanding citizens.
“We look forward to welcoming them and their families into the inn for their celebratory meals.”
