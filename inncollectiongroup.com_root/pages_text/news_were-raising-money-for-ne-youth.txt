We’re raising money for NE Youth
The Inn Collection Group are using their free time during lockdown to help those truly in need. Our chosen charity for 2020 is NE Youth, the North East’s leading youth development charity, supporting young people and youth organisations. Their vision is for all young people in the North East of England to have the opportunities they deserve to get involved in their communities, enjoy their lives, and achieve their potential.
