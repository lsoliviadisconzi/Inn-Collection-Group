The Inn Collection Group raise over £4.8k for NE Youth
The Inn Collection Group team took on the Mad March Mare in order to raise money for their chosen charity, NE Youth.
The team battled wind, snow, water, mud, obstacles and a 10k course and raised a total of £4,890 for the local charity!
Managing Director, Sean Donkin, was part of the team that took on the Mad March Mare and said: “It is our absolute pleasure to take part in an event which raised so much money collectively for various incredibly worthwhile causes. Our charity of choice is NE Youth, and we hope that at such a difficult time, the small contribution we have been able to make will make a positive difference to those most in need.”
NE Youth is one of the North East’s leading youth development charity’s supporting young people in the region. They try to make sure all young people have the opportunities they deserve, and to get involved in their communities, enjoy their lives and achieve their potential.
