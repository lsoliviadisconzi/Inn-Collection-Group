Set beside its spectacular namesake waterfall on the Afon Llugwy, The Swallow Falls Inn offers a truly unforgettable stay in the heart of Snowdonia National Park. Surrounded by dramatic landscapes, forest trails and flowing water, it’s a standout choice among hotels near Snowdonia for those seeking both adventure and relaxation.
Less than two miles from the picturesque village of Betws-y-Coed, this traditional coaching inn blends historic charm with modern comfort. Known as one of the most characterful inns in Betws-y-Coed, The Swallow Falls Inn provides easy access to the area’s famous walking routes, waterfalls and outdoor attractions, while remaining a peaceful retreat away from the bustle of the village.
The inn features 39 comfortable bedrooms, alongside a scenic 30-pitch campsite and camping pods, making it one of the most versatile options for Betws-y-Coed accommodation. Whether you’re planning a cosy hotel stay, a camping break, or a group adventure, all styles of escape are catered for.

Dog Friendly Hotel in Betws-y-Coed
The Swallow Falls Inn is a dog friendly hotel in Betws-y-Coed, with dog-friendly rooms where your four-legged friend can join you for an adventure in Snowdonia. Simply add your dog when booking to make sure you’re allocated a dog-friendly room.
When you book direct, up to two dogs can stay with you for £15 per night. Dogs are also welcome in our designated dog-friendly areas of the pub and restaurant, with water bowls and treats ready and waiting. Please remember that dogs can’t be left unattended in bedrooms during your stay, but can join you for meals so they can be with you every step of the way.
When it’s time to stretch those four legs, you’re already in the heart of Eryri (Snowdonia), with woodland walks and mountain scenery just waiting to be sniffed out. After a day exploring, head back to the inn for some well-earned food and drinks.
Whether you’re looking for dog friendly places to stay in Betws-y-Coed or a dog friendly pub with rooms, The Swallow Falls Inn is the perfect base for exploring North Wales together.

Do you have onsite parking available?

Overnight parking is available to guests staying at the hotel in one of our bedrooms only.

Your dogs are more than welcome at The Swallow Falls Inn! We have designated dog-friendly areas of the pub and restaurant, with water bowls and treats available – including our selection of Sir Woofchester’s dog food range.

What is the cancellation policy?
Our standard cancellation policy is as follows:
You may cancel your booking without any payment to us at any point before 3pm on the day that is two days before the day your booking starts. After this deadline, the whole booking becomes fully non-refundable. If you try to cancel after this deadline (or if you don’t arrive for your booking), you will still be charged the full price for your booking (including any meals or extras).
Our Advance Purchase Rate cancellation policy is as follows: All bookings on an advance purchase rate are non-refundable and cannot be cancelled by you. If you try to cancel a booking on an advance purchase rate (or if you don’t arrive for your booking), you will still be charged the full price for your booking (including any meals or extras).
Our special offers/promotions cancellation policy is as follows: The cancellation rights for bookings offered on a special offer will be stated during the booking process and in your booking confirmation. Where we do not state any cancellation rights for a special offer, the advance purchase rate cancellation terms (above) will apply.

What are the best things to do near The Swallow Falls Inn?
Guests staying at The Swallow Falls Inn are perfectly placed to explore the natural beauty and adventure activities of Snowdonia National Park. Popular nearby attractions include the majestic Swallow Falls located right on your doorstep, walking trails in Gwydyr Forest, mountain hikes, and outdoor adventures like Zip World and Surf Snowdonia.
For those wanting to tackle Snowdonia’s most iconic peak, Mount Snowdon, the inn is a great base for hiking enthusiasts, or you can take the Snowdon Mountain Railway to enjoy the breathtaking summit views without the climb.
Families and dog owners will also love the local rivers, scenic beaches, and the quaint village of Betws-y-Coed, making The Swallow Falls Inn one of the best bases for hotels near Snowdonia that combine adventure, relaxation, and comfort.

How far is The Swallow Falls Inn from Betws-y-Coed village?
The Swallow Falls Inn is just 2 miles from the village of Betws-y-Coed, making it one of the most convenient hotels in Betws-y-Coed. You can easily explore the village’s shops, cafés, and historic attractions while enjoying the peaceful, forested setting of the inn near the iconic Swallow Falls waterfall.

Do you have any on site bike storage?
We do have bike storage on site. This is charged at £10 per bike, per night, with space for 15 bikes.
Please note E-bikes and e-scooters are permitted on our premises provided the battery has been removed before arrival and is stored at least 10 metres away from the building. E-bikes and e-scooters must not be charged anywhere on our premises.
We are unable to provide storage facilities for e-bike or e-scooter batteries. The bike or scooter may be stored on site without its battery fitted.
