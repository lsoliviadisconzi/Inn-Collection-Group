The Hog’s Head Inn is an award-winning, family-friendly hotel in Alnwick, set on the outskirts of town with free parking, easy access, and dog-friendly rooms. Perfect for families, couples, and four-legged friends, it’s an ideal base for exploring accommodation in Alnwick and the surrounding Northumberland countryside.
Step out to discover Alnwick’s historic market town, quirky shops, and the Duke of Northumberland’s estate. Just minutes away, Alnwick Castle, a famous Harry Potter filming location, offers magical days out, while The Alnwick Garden and Lillidori provide fun for all ages. The hourly town tour bus makes exploring effortless.
Enjoy sandy beaches at Alnmouth or scenic trails in Northumberland National Park, then return to cosy rooms and welcoming hospitality. Practical, comfortable, and full of charm, the Hog’s Head Inn stands out among Alnwick hotels for unforgettable family adventures.

Dog Friendly Hotel in Alnwick
The Hog's Head Inn is a dog friendly hotel in Alnwick, with dog-friendly rooms across the inn where your four-legged friend can join you for adventures in Northumberland. Simply add your dog when booking to make sure you’re allocated a dog-friendly room.
When you book direct, up to two dogs can stay with you for £15 per night. Dogs are also welcome in our designated dog-friendly areas of the pub and restaurant, with water bowls and treats ready and waiting. Please remember that dogs can’t be left unattended in bedrooms during your stay, but can join you every step of the way.
When it’s time to stretch those four legs, the Northumberland countryside is on your doorstep, with woodland walks and quaint town streets waiting to be sniffed out. After a day exploring, head back to the inn for some well-earned food and drinks.
Whether you’re looking for dog friendly places to stay in Alnwick or a dog friendly pub with rooms in Northumberland, make The Hog's Head Inn is your perfect base to explore.

The Hog's Head Inn, Alnwick FAQs
Planning a stay at The Hog’s Head Inn in Alnwick, Northumberland? Our FAQs cover everything from location, parking, accessibility, check-in times and guest amenities you can enjoy during your stay.
Do you have on site parking available?
The Hog’s Head Inn offers free on-site parking for both pub visitors and overnight guests, making it one of the most convenient hotels in Alnwick. Parking is available on a first-come, first-served basis, giving families, friends, and dog owners easy access to the inn’s restaurant, bar, and accommodation. Whether you’re dining in our pub, enjoying a Sunday carvery, or staying overnight, our complimentary parking ensures a stress-free visit in the heart of Alnwick Northumberland.

Check in at The Hog’s Head Inn is from 3pm and check out is 10:30am.

Is there disabled access and accessible rooms at the inn?
The Hog’s Head Inn offers level access from the main entrance, making it easy for all guests to enter the pub and hotel. We have three accessible rooms in Alnwick, designed for comfort and convenience. Two rooms feature a low-level bath, while one includes a walk-in wet room. Please note that the total height of the bed and mattress is approximately 70cm.
Our accessible accommodation is perfect for families, couples, and visitors with mobility needs, ensuring a comfortable stay at one of the top hotels in Alnwick Northumberland. For further details or specific accessibility requirements, please contact our central reservations team who will be more than happy to help.

Are you dog-friendly?
Your dogs are more than welcome at The Hog’s Head Inn! We offer designated dog-friendly areas in the pub and restaurant, complete with water bowls, treats, and our carefully selected range of Sir Woofchester’s dog food. Whether you’re stopping by for a meal or staying overnight, your four-legged friends are part of the family here.
Our pet-friendly hotel rooms in Alnwick are conveniently located on the ground floor, making them easy to access for dogs of all sizes. First-floor rooms are reserved as pet-free, so please let us know at the time of booking if you’ll be bringing a dog to ensure availability.
When you book direct, up to two of your four-legged friend can be added to your booking for £15 per night. Another perk of booking direct with us at The Inn Collection Group!
For more information on our pet-friendly accommodation, please contact our central reservations team on 0191 580 3610.

What facilities are provided in the rooms?
All rooms at The Hog’s Head Inn feature complimentary Wi-Fi, a flat-screen TV, and tea & coffee making facilities, providing everything you need for a comfortable stay at one of the top hotels in Alnwick. What is the cancellation policy?
Our standard cancellation policy is as follows:
You may cancel your booking without any payment to us at any point before 3pm on the day that is two days before the day your booking starts. After this deadline, the whole booking becomes fully non-refundable. If you try to cancel after this deadline (or if you don’t arrive for your booking), you will still be charged the full price for your booking (including any meals or extras).
Our Advance Purchase Rate cancellation policy is as follows: All bookings on an advance purchase rate are non-refundable and cannot be cancelled by you. If you try to cancel a booking on an advance purchase rate (or if you don’t arrive for your booking), you will still be charged the full price for your booking (including any meals or extras).
Our special offers/promotions cancellation policy is as follows: The cancellation rights for bookings offered on a special offer will be stated during the booking process and in your booking confirmation. Where we do not state any cancellation rights for a special offer, the advance purchase rate cancellation terms (above) will apply.

What is there to do in Alnwick?
Alnwick is a historic market town packed with things to see and do for all ages. A must-visit is
Alnwick Castle
, famous as a Harry Potter filming location, where you can explore stunning rooms, beautiful gardens, and family-friendly events throughout the year. Just nearby,
The Alnwick Garden
offers the spectacular Poison Garden, treehouse adventures, and seasonal activities perfect for children.
For outdoor enthusiasts, the Northumberland coastline is just a short drive away, including the sandy beaches at
Alnmouth
and scenic walking trails in
Northumberland National Park
. Families can enjoy local shops, cafés, and the hourly town tour bus, which makes exploring Alnwick easy without worrying about parking.
After a day of sightseeing, relax at The Hog’s Head Inn, with its family-friendly rooms, dog-friendly facilities, hearty meals, and welcoming pub atmosphere—making it one of the top hotels in Alnwick for a memorable stay.
