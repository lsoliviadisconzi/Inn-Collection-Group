Looking for dog friendly pubs in York?
You don't need to stay overnight to enjoy a visit to Dean Court! Our dog-friendly dining area is open to everyone, whether you're visiting us for a refreshment or settling in for a leisurely meal. Dogs can join you in our designated dog-friendly pub and restaurant areas, with water bowls and treats ready and waiting. Feeling peckish? Sir Woofchester’s dog food range means they can tuck into something tasty whilst the humans get stuck into classic pub favourites, all-day dining, Sunday lunch and local cask ales.
With the quaint streets of York just waiting to be sniffed out, you can take your pooch out to wander the historic city before settling in for some food and drinks.
So whether you’re looking for dog friendly pubs in York, somewhere to refuel after a walk or simply a spot where the whole pack can relax, pull up a chair and stay awhile. Good food. Good drinks. Wagging tails. What’s not to like?
