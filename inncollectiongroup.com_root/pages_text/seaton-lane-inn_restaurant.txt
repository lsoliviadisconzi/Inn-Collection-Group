Looking for dog friendly pubs in Seaham?
You don't need to stay overnight to enjoy a visit to The Seaton Lane Inn! Our dog-friendly pub is open to everyone, whether you're visiting us for a refreshment after a walk or settling in for a leisurely meal. Dogs can join you in our designated dog-friendly pub and restaurant areas, with water bowls and treats ready and waiting. Feeling peckish? Sir Woofchester’s dog food range means they can tuck into something tasty whilst the humans get stuck into classic pub favourites, all-day dining, Sunday lunch and local cask ales.
With Seaham’s coastline and local walking routes nearby, you can take your pooch out for a good stretch before settling in for some food and drinks.
So whether you’re looking for dog friendly pubs in Seaham, somewhere to refuel after a walk or simply a spot where the whole pack can relax, pull up a chair and stay awhile. Good food. Good drinks. Wagging tails. What’s not to like?

Our delicious pub food
There are few things better than taking in the sea air at Seaham Beach, or shopping until you drop in Newcastle Town centre and then heading back to our traditional yet modern inn, packed with all those features that make it feel like a home away from home. Put your feet up, order your favourite dish, and let our chefs do the cooking while you and your loved ones chat about the day you’ve just had. Our relaxed bar and spacious dining area make for the ideal venue to spend an afternoon or evening with loved ones. We also have a stunning orangery that is a picture-perfect backdrop for whatever it is you have planned.
When you arrive, whether fresh from your comfortable en-suite accommodation or simply popping in from the town for a quick treat, you can expect the same friendly, northern welcome. Choose to dine in our stone-flagged bar area cosied up with that special someone, enjoying a sandwich and pint of cask ale, or take the whole family into the restaurant space and let them choose their favourites from our scrumptious children’s menu. Whichever eating option you prefer, we have it here at The Seaton Lane Inn.
A brewed recipe for success
The Seaton Lane Inn’s well-stocked cellar brings you wines from around the world. Available by the glass or bottle, there are wines to pair with all dishes and to suit every taste.
We also work with local breweries to offer a selection of cask ales on rotation. We love being a traditional pub, but that doesn't mean we can't enjoy making you a fancy cocktail or two! We have an extensive cocktail list that is handmade by our trained bar staff.
