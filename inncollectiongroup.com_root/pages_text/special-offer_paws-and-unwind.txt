Dog-Friendly Getaway FAQs
What makes your inns ideal for dog-friendly breaks?
With over 30 welcoming pubs with rooms, dog-friendly bars and dining areas, overnight stays, Sir Woofchester's dog dinners and regular Dogs Stay Free offers, we make it easy for you and your dog to enjoy quality time together.
From coast to country, we have inns surrounded by stunning walks and scenic views that you and your four-legged friends and explore.
Discover all our locations here.

What should I bring for my dog's stay?
To help your dog feel right at home, we recommend packing their lead, favourite bed or blanket, toys, food (if you prefer to bring your own), and any medication they may need during your stay. With a few of their favourite things packed, your four-legged friend will have everything they need to feel comfortable, relaxed and right at home at our dog-friendly inns.
Close
Are dogs allowed in our restaurants and bars?
All of our pubs welcome dogs in designated dining and bar areas. Some of our inns have parts of the dining area which they keep pet-free, but if you're pre-booking or walking in, just let our staff know you have a dog and they will be more than happy to find you a table to accommodate you all!
Close
Can I book a dog-friendly break online?
Yes! You can book your dog-friendly stay online by selecting 'Bring Your Furry Friend' on the extras section of the booking process. This means our team can then allocate you to a dog friendly room.
If you need any extra help, our friendly central reservations team are here to help. If required, please call

Do I need to let the team know I am bringing a dog to stay overnight?
Yes, when making your reservation please make our team aware you are bringing a dog so we can make sure you are allocated to a pet-friendly room.
Please let us know by adding a pet in the 'extras' section of the online booking platform, or through our central reservations team on
0191 580 3610.

Do you have any rules for dogs staying with you?
We simply ask that dogs are kept under control, cleaned up after, remain on a lead where appropriate, and are respectful of other guests so everyone can enjoy their visit.
For your dog's safety and comfort, we kindly ask that they aren't left alone in your bedroom during your stay.
