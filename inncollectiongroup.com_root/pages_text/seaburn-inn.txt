Dog Friendly Hotel near Sunderland
The Seaburn Inn is a dog friendly hotel in Seaburn, Sunderland, with pet-friendly rooms across all room categories so your four-legged friend can join you on your stay. Simply add your dog when booking to make sure you’re allocated one of our dog-friendly room options.
When you book direct, up to two dogs can stay with you for £15 per night. Dogs are also welcome in our designated dog-friendly areas of the pub and restaurant, where they’ll find water bowls, treats and Sir Woofchester’s dog food range waiting for them. Please remember that dogs can’t be left unattended in bedrooms during your stay, but they can join you for all your meals with us!
When it’s time to stretch those four legs, Seaburn Beach is right on the doorstep, making it easy to head out for a bracing seaside stroll before settling back in for some well-earned food and drinks.
Whether you're looking for dog friendly places to stay near Sunderland or a dog friendly pub with rooms for a city break, The Seaburn Inn makes it easy to bring the whole family along.

Do you have on site parking available?
We offer complimentary parking at The Seaburn Inn car park to the rear of the inn for guests only.  Space is limited on a first come, first served basis and we do not under any circumstances reserve or guarantee parking spaces.  Complimentary parking in our car park is only valid either when you are staying at our inn and checked in to a room or are a customer in the bar, restaurant or attending a function. Overnight parking is available to guests staying at the hotel in one of our bedrooms only.
You
MUST
register your vehicle registration number into the tablet on reception if you are a bar, restaurant or function customer.  If you are an accommodation guest, then please register your car registration number with reception.
Diners – 2 hours FREE
Overnight Guests – 24 hours FREE for each night of your stay
Charges apply to ALL other car park users – please remember to pay for your parking at the car park pay and display machine, via phone or prebook with RingGo. No overnight camping allowed.

Is there disabled access and accessible rooms at the inn?
There is a lift to all floors and level access into The Seaburn Inn. There are designated accessible rooms onsite with lowered bathtubs.

Are you dog-friendly?
Your dogs are more than welcome at The Seaburn Inn! We have designated dog-friendly areas of the pub and restaurant, with water bowls and treats available – including our selection of Sir Woofchester’s dog food range.
We also offer pet-friendly hotel rooms on the first and second floor, so your dogs can join you for overnight stays. Please let us know whether you will be bringing a pet at the time of booking to ensure availability, and so we can make sure we allocate you to our pet-friendly rooms.
When you book direct, up to two of your four-legged friends can be added to your booking for £15 per night. Another perk of booking direct with us at The Inn Collection Group!
For more information on our pet-friendly accommodation, please contact our central reservations team on 0191 580 3610.

What is the cancellation policy?
Our standard cancellation policy is as follows:
You may cancel your booking without any payment to us at any point before 3pm on the day that is two days before the day your booking starts. After this deadline, the whole booking becomes fully non-refundable. If you try to cancel after this deadline (or if you don’t arrive for your booking), you will still be charged the full price for your booking (including any meals or extras).
Our Advance Purchase Rate cancellation policy is as follows: All bookings on an advance purchase rate are non-refundable and cannot be cancelled by you. If you try to cancel a booking on an advance purchase rate (or if you don’t arrive for your booking), you will still be charged the full price for your booking (including any meals or extras).
Our special offers/promotions cancellation policy is as follows: The cancellation rights for bookings offered on a special offer will be stated during the booking process and in your booking confirmation. Where we do not state any cancellation rights for a special offer, the advance purchase rate cancellation terms (above) will apply.

Do you have any on site bike storage?
There is secure bike storage available on site at The Seaburn Inn.
Please note E-bikes and e-scooters are permitted on our premises provided the battery has been removed before arrival and is stored at least 10 metres away from the building. E-bikes and e-scooters must not be charged anywhere on our premises.
We are unable to provide storage facilities for e-bike or e-scooter batteries. The bike or scooter may be stored on site without its battery fitted.
