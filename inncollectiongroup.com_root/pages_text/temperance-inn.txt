Located in the heart of Ambleside, The Temperance Inn is a historic Cumbria hotel in the Lake District offering cosy accommodation surrounded by mountain views, walking routes and the lively atmosphere of this much-loved Lakeland town. Once a temperance establishment in the 1800s, today it’s the perfect base for walkers, couples, families, dog owners and car-free travellers looking to explore the very best of the Lakes.
Located in the heart of Bustling Ambleside and just a short stroll from the northern shores of Lake Windermere, perfectly placed for scenic lakeside walks, steamer trips and famous fell hikes, The Temperance Inn Ambleside makes exploring the Lake District easy.

Dog Friendly Hotel in Ambleside
Bring your dogs along for a Lake District getaway at the Temperance Inn, a dog friendly hotel in Ambleside with dog-friendly rooms available across all room categories. Just let us know you’re bringing your pooch when you book, and we’ll make sure you’re given a room where they’re welcome too.

Do you have on site parking available?
No on site parking at The Temperance Inn. The nearest car park is located on Rydal Road or Lake Road.

Check in at The Temperance Inn is from 3pm and check out is 10:30am.

Is there disabled access and accessible rooms at the inn?
There is no level access or accessible rooms available at The Temperance Inn. For further accessibility information please call our reservations team.

Does Temperance Inn have live music?
Yes, The Temperance Inn hosts live music in the pub on Friday and Saturday evenings (subject to change). This creates a lively and sociable atmosphere for both guests and visitors, especially at weekends. Walk ins are always welcome, or you can book a table
here
. We recommend checking directly with the hotel for the most up-to-date schedule.
Some rooms may experience sound during performance times, particularly those located closer to the main building. Rooms further away or on higher floors are generally quieter. Guests can request quieter rooms when booking or before arrival. Quiet room requests are subject to availability and are usually allocated to higher floors or cottage rooms where possible.

Are you dog-friendly?
Your dogs are more than welcome at The Temperance Inn! We have designated dog-friendly areas of the pub and restaurant, with water bowls and treats available – including our selection of Sir Woofchester’s dog food range.
We also offer pet-friendly hotel rooms across all rooms in the main inn, we try and keep our cottage rooms in our annex area pet-free. Please let us know whether you will be bringing a pet at the time of booking to ensure availability.
When you book direct, up to two of your four-legged friends can be added to your booking for £15 per night. Another perk of booking direct with us at The Inn Collection Group!
For more information on our pet-friendly accommodation, please contact our central reservations team on 0191 580 3610.

What is the cancellation policy?
Our standard cancellation policy is as follows:
You may cancel your booking without any payment to us at any point before 3pm on the day that is two days before the day your booking starts. After this deadline, the whole booking becomes fully non-refundable. If you try to cancel after this deadline (or if you don’t arrive for your booking), you will still be charged the full price for your booking (including any meals or extras).
Our Advance Purchase Rate cancellation policy is as follows: All bookings on an advance purchase rate are non-refundable and cannot be cancelled by you. If you try to cancel a booking on an advance purchase rate (or if you don’t arrive for your booking), you will still be charged the full price for your booking (including any meals or extras).
Our special offers/promotions cancellation policy is as follows: The cancellation rights for bookings offered on a special offer will be stated during the booking process and in your booking confirmation. Where we do not state any cancellation rights for a special offer, the advance purchase rate cancellation terms (above) will apply.

Do you have any on site bike storage?
There is no bike storage available on site at The Temperance Inn.
Please note E-bikes and e-scooters are permitted on our premises provided the battery has been removed before arrival and is stored at least 10 metres away from the building. E-bikes and e-scooters must not be charged anywhere on our premises.
We are unable to provide storage facilities for e-bike or e-scooter batteries. The bike or scooter may be stored on site without its battery fitted.

Hidden in the heart of ancient Cumbria in North West England lies a National Park known the world over. The Lake District boasts glacial lakes, dramatic fells, historic villages and breathtaking scenery, all waiting to be discovered from the heart of Ambleside.
The Temperance Inn is also ideal for a car-free Lake District break. Travel by train to Windermere station, hop on a direct bus into Ambleside, and enjoy a walkable stay with shops, restaurants, lakeside routes and popular walking trails all close by. With easy access to local bus links across the Lakes, exploring nearby villages, fell walks and attractions without a car couldn’t be simpler.
