Looking for dog friendly pubs in Ambleside?
You don't need to stay overnight to enjoy a visit to The Temperance Inn! Our dog-friendly dining area is open to everyone, whether you're visiting us for a refreshment after a walk or settling in for a leisurely meal. Dogs can join you in our designated dog-friendly pub and restaurant areas, with water bowls and treats ready and waiting. Feeling peckish? Sir Woofchester’s dog food range means they can tuck into something tasty whilst the humans get stuck into classic pub favourites, all-day dining, Sunday lunch and local cask ales.
With the Lake District right on your doorstep, you can spend the day exploring before returning to the inn to put your feet (and paws!) up over some tasty food and drinks.
So whether you’re looking for dog friendly pubs in Ambleside or somewhere where the whole pack can relax, pull up a chair and stay awhile. Good food. Good drinks. Wagging tails. What’s not to love?

Meal at The Temperance Inn
The best food in the Lake District
If you’re looking for exciting places to eat in Ambleside, The Temperance Inn is the perfect spot. Right in the heart of the village, our welcoming venue serves bold, flavour-packed street food in the Lake District in a relaxed and lively setting. From Korean chicken bao buns and loaded tacos to poke bowls, dirty mac & cheese and indulgent loaded hot dogs, our menu is designed for laid-back dining after a day exploring the fells.
Families are always welcome, with a dedicated children’s menu for younger diners, and we’re proudly dog friendly too, making us a popular choice for relaxed dining in Ambleside. Whether you’re stopping by for lunch, street food and cocktails, or an evening meal after exploring the Lakes, The Temperance Inn is one of the top restaurants in Ambleside for great food and atmosphere. Walk-ins are always welcome, or a book a table today!

A brewed recipe for success
Enjoy a G&T or shake things up with one of our signature cocktails. As well as local real ales, guest hand pulls on rotation, perfectly chilled continental lagers and scrumptious ciders, we’ve got a wide range of quirky, characterful bottled craft beers too - you’ll be truly spoilt for choice.
Our well-stocked cellar will bring you wines from around the globe. Sold by the glass or bottle, there are wines to pair with all dishes and to suit every taste.
Enjoy planning your next Lakeland adventure over a pot of tea or aromatic barista coffee. Or why not try something different with our Ringtons speciality loose leaf teas?
