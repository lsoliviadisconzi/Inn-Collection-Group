Looking for dog friendly pubs in Helmsley?
You don't need to stay overnight to enjoy a visit to The Black Swan! Our dog-friendly dining area is open to everyone, whether you're visiting us for a refreshment after a walk or settling in for a leisurely meal. Dogs can join you in our designated dog-friendly pub and restaurant areas, with water bowls and treats ready and waiting. Feeling peckish? Sir Woofchester’s dog food range means they can tuck into something tasty whilst the humans get stuck into classic pub favourites, all-day dining, Sunday lunch and local cask ales.
With quaint market streets on your doorstep and being surrounded by the North York Moors, there's plenty to explore for all activity levels before settling in for some food and drinks.
So whether you’re looking for dog friendly pubs in Helmsley or somewhere to refuel after a walk in the North York Moors, we've got a seat waiting for you!

Our delicious pub food
At The Black Swan, timeless Yorkshire hospitality meets the charm of a historic coaching inn. Our seasonal menus are carefully crafted to celebrate the very best local ingredients. Guests can enjoy everything from comforting pub favourites, rich with tradition, to refined dishes with a modern flair, each one designed to showcase the flavours of the region. Sundays are a true highlight, when the aroma of slow-roasted meats fills the air and beautifully prepared roasts are served with all the trimmings – the perfect way to savour tradition and share time with family and friends in the heart of Helmsley.

A brewed recipe for success
Soak up the sun in our beautiful Walled Garden where you can wile away the time with a glass of something delicious. Choose from local real ales and guest hand pulls on rotation, perfectly chilled continental lagers and scrumptious ciders and a wide range of quirky, characterful bottled craft beers.
A well-stocked cellar brings you wines from around the globe. Sold by the glass or bottle, there are wines to pair with all dishes and to suit every taste.
