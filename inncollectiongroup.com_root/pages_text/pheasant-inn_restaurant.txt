Nothing beats enjoying proper, home-cooked food made with fresh, local ingredients – and at The Pheasant Inn, that’s exactly what you’ll find. Nestled just off the shores of Bassenthwaite Lake, our menu is full of hearty pub favourites and modern dishes, all celebrating seasonal flavours and the very best of the Lake District.
Whether you’re exploring the lakes, visiting the local area, or simply looking for a cosy spot to unwind, you’re welcome to relax in a comfortable corner, start the day with a generous breakfast, enjoy a leisurely lunch, or settle in for an evening meal. From a light bite with friends to a comforting dinner for two, The Pheasant Inn offers great food, drinks, and a warm welcome nestles away just a short walk from Bassenthwaite Lake.

Tucked just off the shores of Bassenthwaite Lake, our traditional inn pairs a stunning Lake District setting with hearty, locally inspired dishes. From comforting pies and stacked burgers to freshly prepared fish, our menu brings you the best of pub food in this beautiful corner of Cumbria. Make sure to check out our changing seasonal menus, making the most of the freshest flavours.
