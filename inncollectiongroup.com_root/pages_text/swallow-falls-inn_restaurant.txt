Dine at The Swallow Falls Inn
+ 4 items
After soaking up the natural beauty of Snowdonia, there’s no better spot to eat, drink, and relax than the Swallow Falls Inn in Betws-y-Coed. Just across from the spectacular Swallow Falls waterfall, our welcoming inn has everything from hearty breakfasts to laid-back lunches and evening meals that really hit the spot.
On sunny days, take in the fresh mountain air with a drink in our spacious beer garden, but when the weather turns cooler, head inside and cosy up – the perfect place to unwind with friends, family, or even the dog.
Whether you’re after a light snack, classic pub favourites, or just stopping by for a pint after exploring the trails, you won’t find a better backdrop in Betws-y-Coed. Serving tasty food and tempting drinks all day, every day, the Swallow Falls Inn is your go-to pub for great food, a proper pint, and unforgettable views of one of Snowdonia’s most famous landmarks.

Looking for a dog friendly pub in Betws-y-Coed?
You don't need to stay overnight to enjoy a visit to The Swallow Falls Inn! Our dog-friendly dining area is open to everyone, whether you're visiting us for a refreshment after a walk through Snowdonia or settling in for a leisurely meal to relax. Dogs can join you in our designated dog-friendly pub and restaurant areas, with water bowls and treats ready and waiting. Feeling peckish? Our delicious pub food
If you’re looking for the best places to eat in Betws-y-Coed, the Swallow Falls Inn is the perfect choice. Set opposite the famous Swallow Falls waterfall, our inn combines breathtaking Snowdonia scenery with hearty, locally inspired dishes. From classic pub favourites and juicy burgers to freshly prepared fish, our menu celebrates the best of traditional Welsh hospitality.
We pride ourselves on using seasonal ingredients and local produce, with menus that adapt throughout the year. Families are always welcome, and younger diners can enjoy our tasty children’s menu. Whether you’re after a relaxed lunch after a walk in the hills, a cosy dinner in the evening, or a refreshing pint in our spacious beer garden, the Swallow Falls Inn is one of the top dining spots in Betws-y-Coed for great food, drinks, and an unforgettable backdrop.

A brewed recipe for success
Enjoy a refreshing gin in our spacious beer garden overlooking the beauty of Snowdonia, or shake things up with one of our signature cocktails. Alongside local real ales, rotating guest hand pulls, crisp continental lagers and delicious ciders, we also stock a wide range of quirky, characterful bottled craft beers – so you’ll always be spoilt for choice.
Our well-stocked cellar brings you wines from around the world, available by the glass or bottle, with the perfect pairing for every dish and every taste.
Planning your next Snowdonia adventure? Settle in with a pot of tea or a rich barista coffee. Or, for something a little different, why not try one of our Ringtons speciality loose leaf teas?
