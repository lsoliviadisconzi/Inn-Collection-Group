Roseberry Topping
Roseberry Topping
Roseberry topping is a distinct and iconic hill that offers views across North Yorkshire and Cleveland. Standing at 1,049 feet high, this local attraction is not the biggest hill but is one of the most distinctive.
Characterised with woodland, grassland, heather moorland and historic quarry, Roseberry Topping is a very fun family day out.
Explore Roseberry Topping
Roseberry Topping is a wonderful attraction to visit if you are looking for a walk filled with exploration. With it’s distinctive shape, caused by a geological fault and mining collapse in 1912, this hill is hard to miss with it’s half-cone summit and jagged cliff. Most visitors visit to conquer the hill, there is so much more to discover like the heather moorland, bluebell woods, the open expanses of Roseberry Common and an abandoned quarry.
Guisborough Forest
Guisborough Forest and Walkway
Guisborough Forest is home to a trim trail, a cycling trail, play areas and fantastic walking trails for relaxing, connecting to nature. This forest is perfect for families, couples or even solo travellers looking to add some adventure to their holiday.
Guisborough Forest Trails
Offering 3 walking trails, Guisborough Forest has something for everyone to discover!
The Green Walk- This course is a circular loop with stunning views over Teesside. Taking approximately 45 Minutes, this trail takes you to the top of the beech wood, passes through a mix of habitats with breathtaking views of Guisborough and beyond.
The Red Walk- A slightly longer trail of 1 hour 30 minutes, this walk takes you on a steep ascent through the beech wood, the route winds its way through a variety of forest habitats with great views over the Tees Valley.
The White Walk- The ultimate walk of Guisborough Forest, this 2 hour 30 minutes circular trail includes a moderate steep climb and takes in forestry and farmland. It then meanders past the Hanging Stone, from which a small detour gives rewarding views over Teesside and the coast. Plus, this trail also passes by the Blue Lake which used to store the water to pump hydraulic machinery at Home Farm.
Middlesborough Golf Club
Middlesborough Golf Club
Founded in 1908 this course is situated 5 miles south of Middlesborough centre, offering	stunning views of Cleveland Hills. This course has many attractive and challenging holes that will test all levels of skill and takes advantage of natural ravines and undulating land.
Alongside the golf course, there is also a modern clubhouse with outstanding hospitality suitable for everyone.
The Course
Middlesborough golf course is an 18 hole course with stunning views of the Cleveland Hills. The course was designed by James Braid, a Scottish professional golfer, who won 5 open championships.
Founded in 1908 Middlesbrough Golf Club was originally based at Saltersgill Farm, until 1938 when they moved to the present location. The course was officially declared open in April 1939, later in 1990 new holes were laid through acquired woodland, giving the present layout.

Guisborough Priory
The ruins of this priory are dominated by the skeleton of of the 14th century church's east end. The priory was founded in about 1119 and endowered by the powerful Norman lord Robert 1 de Brus.
There are guided tours available for Guisborough Priory, the tours are free of charge. They offer a range of different group tours, all tours start at 2pm and last approximately 1 hour.
Discover the history of Guisborough Priory
Mount Grace Priory is the only Carthusian monastery open to the public in England. Discover how the monks lived 600 years ago by exploring the reconstructed monk’s cell. Create the perfect day out with snacks and a picnic whilst taking in the history that surrounds you.
