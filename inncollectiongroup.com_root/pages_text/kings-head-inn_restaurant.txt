Dine at The King’s Head Inn
+ 5 items
After a day exploring the beauty of Roseberry Topping and the surrounding North York Moors, The Kings Head Inn offers the perfect spot to rest, refuel, and enjoy a hearty meal. Our menu is filled with comforting classics, light bites, and seasonal favourites, all freshly prepared to suit every appetite.
Sundays are all about our traditional carvery, a much-loved highlight to indulge in hand-carved meats, fresh, seasonal vegetables, lashings of gravy and of course, a Yorkshire pudding! It’s the ideal way to round off a weekend of fresh air and adventure with all the trimmings.
Dining at The Kings Head Inn is all about flavour and comfort. Whether you’re walking the moors, climbing Roseberry Topping, or simply looking for a cosy inn to unwind, you’ll find a warm welcome, generous portions, and a menu designed to please the whole family in a truly memorable setting.

Our delicious pub food
If you’re looking for the best places to eat near Roseberry Topping, The Kings Head Inn is the perfect choice. Set at the foot of this iconic North York Moors landmark, our pub pairs stunning countryside views with hearty, freshly prepared dishes. From classic pies and juicy burgers to traditional fish and chips, our menu celebrates the very best of pub food in a welcoming village inn setting.
Families are always welcome, with a children’s menu full of tasty favourites for younger diners. Whether you’re stopping by for a relaxed lunch after a walk on the moors, a cosy dinner by the fire, or a pint in our beer garden with Roseberry Topping as your backdrop, The Kings Head Inn offers the ideal place to eat, drink, and unwind. Walk-ins are always welcome.
