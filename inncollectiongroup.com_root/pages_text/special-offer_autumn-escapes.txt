Autumn Escapes FAQs
Are Autumn Escapes available for families?
Yes, our Autumn Escapes are ideal for family staycations in the UK. With quieter destinations after the summer rush, it is easier to explore at a relaxed pace with far less stress and fewer crowds. Families can enjoy coastal walks, countryside adventures and local attractions without peak season busyness.
Many of our inns offer spacious family friendly accommodation, hearty meals and relaxed spaces to unwind together after a day of exploring. Additional children can be added to your booking at the standard rate (dinner is not included).
All of our inns also offer a dedicated children’s menu, suitable for even the pickiest eaters, whether you have spent the day by the seaside or exploring castles and countryside trails. Family rooms often include flexible sleeping arrangements such as sofa beds or bunk beds, depending on the inn. Please check individual inn pages for full room details and availability.

Are Autumn Escapes dog friendly?
Yes, our Autumn Escapes are fully dog friendly. Your four-legged friend is welcome to join you, so no one gets left behind. With walking routes right on the doorstep, from lakeside strolls to coastal paths and countryside trails, autumn is the perfect season for a dog-friendly getaway in the UK. All of our inns are dog friendly and also offer a doogy menu, Sir Woofchesters, meaning no one gets to miss out on any of the fun!
If you're planning on bringing your dog with you, simply add this option on during the booking process. Book direct and enjoy the added perk of including two dogs per night for just £15.

What makes autumn a good time for a UK staycation?
Autumn is one of the best times for a UK staycation thanks to fewer crowds, off peak prices, and some of the most scenic landscapes of the year as countryside, coastlines and national parks transform with autumn colours. It is ideal for cosy weekend breaks, autumn walking holidays, countryside retreats and relaxed coastal escapes across popular destinations such as the Lake District, Lancashire, Northumberland, Yorkshire, North Wales and the North East coast.
It is also a great season for last minute UK breaks and short staycations, with better availability and more choice of rooms compared to peak summer months. Whether you are planning a relaxing autumn getaway, a walking holiday with pub stops, or a cosy break by the coast, autumn offers the perfect balance of value, comfort and seasonal scenery. What's more, with our Autumn Breaks you can enjoy a 2 night stay and save 20% too*, as well as enjoy dinner on the first evening, making it a great value getaway for everyone.
