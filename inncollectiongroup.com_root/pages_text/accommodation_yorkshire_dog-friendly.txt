The Amble Inn - Dog-Friendly
Stroll around York with Your Furry Best Friend
Talk about an Inn with a view! If you want to soak up the magic of York’s historical charm, you’re in the right place. Dean Court is just a stone’s throw from the magnificent York Minster.
Book a room with a view and you can even wake up to it when you open the curtains. What better way to start a day that beckons cultural delights and enchanting sights with every step?
You’ll be mere minutes away from the attractions that make York the cultural haven that it is. The iconic Shambles Market is just a five-minute walk away. Here you can experience a virtually untouched medieval market with quirky old buildings and cute independent shops tucked behind the ancient shopfronts. Browse the boutiques, sweet shops and curiosity shops.
Have a railway enthusiast with you?
The National Railway Museum
is a mere five-minute walk from Dean Court and is packed with locomotive history. History buffs will be just as happy with the
Yorkshire Museum
and the
JORVIK Viking Centre
close by.
When you’ve had your fill of culture, pretty streets and independent shops treat yourself to a cream cake at a traditional Yorkshire tea shop on the way home. After all, you’ve earned it.
Just don’t forget to save room for dinner from our notoriously delicious dinner menu.
