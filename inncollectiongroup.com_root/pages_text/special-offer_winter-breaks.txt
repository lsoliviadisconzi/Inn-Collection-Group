Winter Breaks FAQs
Are Winter Breaks dog friendly?
Yes, our Winter Breaks are fully dog friendly, making it easy to get away without leaving your four legged friend behind. Winter is a great time for a dog friendly UK break, with quieter beaches, less crowded walking routes and plenty of open space to explore together. After a windswept coastal walk or countryside adventure, you can head back to a cosy inn and warm up by the fire.
All of our inns are dog friendly and offer Sir Woofchesters, our dedicated dog menu, so your pup can enjoy the stay too. If you are bringing your dog, simply add this during the booking process. When you book direct, you can include up to two dogs per night for just £15.

Can I stay longer than 1 night?
Yes, our Winter Breaks are perfect for longer UK staycations as well as short winter getaways. While the offer is based on a one night dinner, bed and breakfast stay, many guests choose to extend their break to enjoy more time exploring the countryside, coast and nearby attractions.
Whether you are planning a relaxing midweek escape, a winter walking holiday or a cosy weekend away, extra nights can be added during the booking process, subject to availability and pricing. Longer stays are a great way to make the most of the UK’s scenic winter landscapes and slower off season pace.
