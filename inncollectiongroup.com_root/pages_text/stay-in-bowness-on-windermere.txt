The Angel Inn, Cumbria FAQs
Planning a stay at The Angel Inn? Our FAQs cover everything from location, parking, accessibility, check-in times and guest amenities you can enjoy during your stay.
Do you have on site parking available?
We offer complimentary parking in The Angel Inn car park for accommodation guests staying overnight only.  Space is limited on a first come, first served basis and we do not under any circumstances reserve or guarantee parking spaces.  Complimentary parking in our car park is only valid when you are staying at our inn and checked in to a room. Overnight parking is available to guests staying at the hotel in one of our bedrooms only.
Please remember enter your vehicle registration on the tablet at check-in. Guests can enjoy 24 hours free for each night of your stay.
Charges apply to ALL other car park users – please remember to pay for your parking at the car park pay and display machine, via phone or prebook with RingGo. No overnight camping allowed.

Is there disabled access and accessible rooms at the inn?
The Angel Inn has level disabled access available to the right of the main building via the conservatory. There are no disabled rooms available at The Angel Inn. For further accessibilty information please call our reservations team.
Close
Are you dog-friendly?
Your dogs are more than welcome at The Angel Inn! We have designated dog-friendly areas of the pub and restaurant, including beer garden with spectacular views across Bowness-On-Windermere and the Lake beyond! We have water bowls and treats available – including our selection of Sir Woofchester’s dog food range.
We also offer pet-friendly hotel rooms in our Oakbank House annex rooms, located just across the road from the main inn. We try and keep accommodation rooms in the main inn pet-free. Please let us know whether you will be bringing a pet at the time of booking to ensure availability.
When you book direct, up to two of your four-legged friend can be added to your booking for £15 per night. Another perk of booking direct with us at The Inn Collection Group!
For more information on our pet-friendly accommodation, please contact our central reservations team on 0191 580 3610.

How far is The Angel Inn from the centre of Bowness-on-Windermere?
The Angel Inn is just a short walk from the centre of Bowness-on-Windermere.
Perfectly located in the heart of the Lake District, the inn is only a few minutes on foot from the village’s shops, restaurants, and the shores of Lake Windermere. This makes it an ideal base for visitors looking for
hotels in Bowness-on-Windermere
that combine convenience with stunning lake and fell views.

Do you have any on site bike storage?
There is no on site bike storage available at The Angel Inn.
Please note E-bikes and e-scooters are permitted on our premises provided the battery has been removed before arrival and is stored at least 10 metres away from the building. E-bikes and e-scooters must not be charged anywhere on our premises.
We are unable to provide storage facilities for e-bike or e-scooter batteries. The bike or scooter may be stored on site without its battery fitted.
