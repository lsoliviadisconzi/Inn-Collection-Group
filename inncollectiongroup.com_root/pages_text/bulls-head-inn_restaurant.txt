Looking for dog friendly pubs in Anglesey?
You don't need to stay overnight to enjoy a visit to The Bull's Head Inn! Our dog-friendly dining area is open to everyone, whether you're visiting us for a refreshment after a walk or settling in for a leisurely meal. Dogs can join you in our designated dog-friendly pub and restaurant areas, with water bowls and treats ready and waiting. Feeling peckish? Sir Woofchester’s dog food range means they can tuck into something tasty whilst the humans get stuck into classic pub favourites, all-day dining, Sunday lunch and local cask ales.
With the stunning Anglesey coastline on your doorstep and the Welsh countryside just waiting to be explored, you can take your pooch out for a good stretch before settling in for some food and drinks.
So whether you’re looking for dog friendly pubs in Anglesey, somewhere to refuel after a walk or simply a spot where the whole pack can relax, pull up a chair and stay awhile. Good food. Good drinks. Wagging tails. What’s not to like?

Our delicious pub food
If you’re looking for one of the best places to eat in Beaumaris, The Bulls Head Inn is a must-visit. Set in the heart of this historic coastal town, our welcoming pub combines warm Welsh hospitality with hearty, locally inspired dishes. From classic pub favourites and juicy burgers to freshly prepared seafood, our menu celebrates the best of Anglesey’s produce.
Whether you’re after a relaxed lunch, a cosy dinner by the fire, or a pint enjoyed just steps away from Beaumaris’ seafront, The Bulls Head Inn is the perfect place to eat, drink, and unwind. Walk-ins are always welcome.
