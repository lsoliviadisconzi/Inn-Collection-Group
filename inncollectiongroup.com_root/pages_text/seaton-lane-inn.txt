Nestled in the quiet village of Seaham, The Seaton Lane Inn is a traditional pub offering great food, tempting drinks, and a truly outstanding place to stay. This inn is an excellent choice for anyone searching for a hotel in Seaham that combines character with comfort.
Brimming with style and quirky charm, The Seaton Lane Inn is open all day, every day, offering laid-back dining with delicious homemade fare, a welcoming bar, and cosy boutique-style en-suite accommodation. It’s a standout Seaham hotel, well known for its friendly atmosphere and thoughtful touches.
Perfectly located to explore the stunning Durham Heritage Coast and the nearby port of Seaham – famous for its beaches scattered with multicoloured sea glass deposits that attract collectors from around the world – this charming pub with rooms offers the best of both seaside and countryside.
Conveniently close to the cities of Durham and Sunderland, The Seaton Lane Inn shines as one of the best places to stay in Seaham. With warm hospitality and an unbeatable location, it’s a place where you’ll fit right inn and be made to feel like an East Durham local.

Dog Friendly Hotel in Seaham
The Seaton Lane Inn is a dog friendly hotel in Seaham, with a selection of dog-friendly rooms where your four-legged friend can join you.
Dogs stay for £15 per night, with up to two dogs welcome. Just add them to your booking when you reserve your room. Dogs can also join you in our designated dog-friendly pub and restaurant areas, with water bowls and treats available.

Is free parking available at The Seaton Lane Inn?
Yes, there is free on-site car parking available at The Seaton Lane Inn. Parking is first-come, first-served.

Is there disabled access and accessible rooms at the inn?
There is disabled access into The Seaton Lane Inn from the ramp located in the car park. There is one accessible room available at The Seaton Lane.
Please note the total height of the bed and the mattress approximately 70cm.
For further accessibility information please call our reservations team.

What is the cancellation policy?
Our standard cancellation policy is as follows:
You may cancel your booking without any payment to us at any point before 3pm on the day that is two days before the day your booking starts. After this deadline, the whole booking becomes fully non-refundable. If you try to cancel after this deadline (or if you don’t arrive for your booking), you will still be charged the full price for your booking (including any meals or extras).
Our Advance Purchase Rate cancellation policy is as follows: All bookings on an advance purchase rate are non-refundable and cannot be cancelled by you. If you try to cancel a booking on an advance purchase rate (or if you don’t arrive for your booking), you will still be charged the full price for your booking (including any meals or extras).
Our special offers/promotions cancellation policy is as follows: The cancellation rights for bookings offered on a special offer will be stated during the booking process and in your booking confirmation. Where we do not state any cancellation rights for a special offer, the advance purchase rate cancellation terms (above) will apply.

Do you have any on site bike storage?
There is very limited bike storage available on site at The Seaton Lane Inn.
Please note E-bikes and e-scooters are permitted on our premises provided the battery has been removed before arrival and is stored at least 10 metres away from the building. E-bikes and e-scooters must not be charged anywhere on our premises.
We are unable to provide storage facilities for e-bike or e-scooter batteries. The bike or scooter may be stored on site without its battery fitted.
