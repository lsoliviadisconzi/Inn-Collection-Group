Lake District refurbishments underway at The Inn Collection Group
Three iconic Lake District Inns are now under refurbishment as The Inn Collection Group works to breathe new life into
The Wateredge Inn
,
The Waterhead Inn
and
The Angel Inn
.
The Newcastle-based pubs with rooms operator began the third of these sympathetic revamps on Monday as contractors moved into The Waterhead Inn, with national architects GSSArchitecture appointed as Architects and Principal Designers on the schemes.
GSSArchitecture
are leading the redevelopment plans on the three sites, with The Wateredge Inn and The Angel Inn grade II listed buildings in the World Heritage Site.
All three sites will benefit from careful refurbishment with new bar and restaurants, improved accessibility, and higher quality guest accommodation. The Wateredge Inn and The Waterhead Inn buildings are situated on a prominent plot of land, providing guests with beautiful views of Lake Windermere.
Works will retain the character of the Inns and be sympathetic to their surroundings, providing these historic buildings with a sustainable long-term future whilst keeping and reinstating their original features.
In addition to the Lake District Inns, the Park Hotel in Tynemouth also recently started on site, with main contractors STP Construction working on the refurbishment of the much-loved, well-known hotel.
Plans for the North Tyneside property include the construction of a two-storey extension in order to add an additional 20 bedrooms at the site. Additionally, a single storey extension will house a new fish and chip takeaway, ice cream parlour and bar area, whilst the external facades, seating and landscaping will undergo comprehensive refurbishment to improve the overall appearance and experience for guests of the Inn.
Louise Stewart, Property Director at The Inn Collection Group said, “We are delighted to be working with GSSArchitecture and bringing their expertise to the refurbishment of these much-loved Inns. They are iconic sites that we are excited to have brought into group and we are confident guests will love them once they are ready to welcome people back through their doors.
“It is important to us that our renovations bring our own identity but at the same time are respectful of these iconic properties’ heritage and working with GSSArchitecture will help achieve that at these Inns.”
Simon Rennison-Rae, Partner at GSSArchitecture, said, “It’s a pleasure to be working so closely with The Inn Collection Group on multiple projects in the Lake District and beyond. We are excited to see how these much-loved buildings can enhance the communities and we look forward to visiting each one once they’ve been renovated!”
GSSArchitecture have extensive experience in the design and delivery of similar hospitality and commercial projects, as well as high-profile projects in a variety of other sectors across the North East and further afield.
