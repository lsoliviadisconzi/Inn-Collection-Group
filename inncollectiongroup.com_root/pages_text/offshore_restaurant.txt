Set right on the St Annes-on-Sea seafront, Offshore is the perfect spot to enjoy delicious home-made food and a wide choice of drinks with a view. Whether you’re calling in for a hearty meal, a relaxed drink with friends, or simply to soak up the coastal atmosphere, our welcoming pub and restaurant are at the heart of this vibrant seaside town.
On sunny days, grab a seat outside and breathe in the fresh sea air as you take in sweeping views across the beach. From crisp pints to coffee catch-ups or long, lazy lunches, there’s no better place to relax and watch the world go by on the coast.

Looking for dog-friendly pubs in Lytham St Annes?
You don't need to stay overnight to enjoy a visit to Offshore! Our dog-friendly dining area is open to everyone, whether you're visiting us for a refreshment after a walk or settling in for a leisurely meal. Dogs can join you in our designated dog-friendly pub and restaurant areas, with water bowls and treats ready and waiting. Feeling peckish? Sir Woofchester’s dog food range means they can tuck into something tasty whilst the humans get stuck into classic pub favourites, all-day dining, Sunday lunch and local cask ales.
With the Fylde Coast at your fingertips and local walking routes on the doorstep, you can take your pooch out for a good stretch before settling in for some food and drinks.
So whether you’re looking for dog friendly pubs in Lytham St Annes, somewhere to refuel after a walk or simply a spot where the whole pack can relax, pull up a chair and stay awhile. Good food. Good drinks. Wagging tails. What’s not to like?

Our delicious pub food
At Offshore, we believe great food starts with fresh ingredients and plenty of flavour. From Great British pub classics to dishes with a modern twist, our menus are filled with seasonal favourites, all handmade and freshly cooked to order. Whether you’re after a comforting classic, a lighter bite, or something a little different, there’s always something to tempt you.
Set against the backdrop of the Lancashire coast, dining at Offshore is about more than just good food – it’s about the experience. Using local produce wherever possible, we serve hearty meals, tasty light options, and indulgent desserts, all best enjoyed with a drink in hand and those unbeatable seaside views.

A brewed recipe for success
Take in the fresh sea air with a gin in hand out in the beer garden, or shake things up with one of our signature cocktails. Alongside local real ales and rotating guest hand pulls, you’ll also find perfectly chilled continental lagers, refreshing ciders, and a wide choice of bottled craft beers full of character – plenty to keep you coming back to try something new.
Our well-stocked bar also offers wines from around the world, available by the glass or bottle, with something to suit every taste and dish. And if you’re in the mood for something softer, settle in with a pot of tea, a smooth barista coffee, or one of our Ringtons speciality loose-leaf teas – perfect for unwinding by the coast.
