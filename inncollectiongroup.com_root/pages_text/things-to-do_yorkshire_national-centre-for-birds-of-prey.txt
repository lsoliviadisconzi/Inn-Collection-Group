National Centre for Birds of Prey
Look up to the sky when you visit the National Centre for Birds of Prey and watch spectacular flying demonstrations! From the tiny terrestrial Burrowing Owl to the mighty and majestic Steller’s Sea Eagle the National Centre for Birds of Prey houses the largest collection of birds of prey in the north of England.
