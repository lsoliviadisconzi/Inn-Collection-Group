Fairhaven Lake & Gardens
Fairhaven Lake and Gardens is a beautiful coastal park between Lytham and St Annes, featuring a large boating lake, scenic walking paths, landscaped gardens, and plenty of wildlife.
Looking for something to do in Lancashire?
Fairhaven Lake and Gardens, nestled between Lytham and St Annes, is a stunning coastal park perfect for all ages. Enjoy peaceful walks around the boating lake, explore beautifully landscaped gardens, and spot local wildlife along the Ribble Estuary. You can hire a rowing boat or paddleboard, play a game of tennis or mini golf, or simply relax with coffee and cake at the lakeside café while taking in the sea views.
It’s an ideal place to unwind, explore nature, and soak up the charm of the Fylde coast.
