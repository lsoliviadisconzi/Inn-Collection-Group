Visit Northumberland
Explore the Northumberland Coast Area of Outstanding Natural Beauty, which stretches for mile upon glorious mile, north of Amble. Famed for its magnificent beaches, castle-studded coastline, unique islands with their diverse wildlife and an area steeped in history spanning thousands of years.
Northumberland Coast
Amble stands at the southern gateway of the Northumberland Coast Area of Outstanding Natural Beauty, a designated stretch of the coast which spans the coast and surrounding areas to Berwick-upon-Tweed.
