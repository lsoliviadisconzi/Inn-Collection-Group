Standing proudly above the River Nidd, The Knaresborough Inn is one of the most welcoming places to stay in Knaresborough. Offering a peaceful stay with beautiful grounds, free on-site parking, and a blend of historic character and modern convenience.
Ideal for both short breaks and longer stays, our inn provides relaxed accommodation just moments away from the town centre in Knaresborough.
Knaresborough is a town bursting with character, known for its ancient walkways, cobbled alleys, and secret passageways that wind through centuries of history. Independent shops, boutiques, and a traditional Wednesday market give it a warm, welcoming feel, while much-loved events such as the famous Bed Race add colour and excitement throughout the year.
From The Knaresborough Inn, you’re ideally placed to explore North Yorkshire’s most loved attractions, with Mother Shipton’s Cave and Knaresborough Castle both just a 10-minute walk away, as well as excellent transport links for easy day trips to Harrogate, Leeds and York.

The Knaresborough Inn FAQs
Make the most of your visit to The Knaresborough Inn in the heart of North Yorkshire. Explore our FAQs for details on location, parking, accessibility, check-in times and our on-site facilities.
Do you have on site parking available?
We have a small car park to the front of the building and a larger one to the rear. All of our car parking is free to guests on a first come first serve basis. We also have 2 EV- Chargers in our rear car park: please be aware that these are owned and operated by a 3rd party.

What time is Check in and Check out?
Check in at The Knaresborough Inn is from 3pm till 11pm. Please be aware that as we are not a 24 hour site, if you do check in after this time you may be subject to a £25 late check in fee. Please contact us directly on
01423 863 302 if you are expecting to arrive after 11pm. Our check out time every morning is 10:30am.
Late check in/check out times can be arranged through our central reservations team on
0191 580 3610.

Is there disabled access and accessible rooms at the inn?
There is level access available throughout The Knaresborough Inn. There is one accessible room available at The Knaresborough Inn; which is to the rear of the building near our rear car park and the accessible refuge. Please note the total height of the bed and the mattress approximately 70cm. All of our rooms have walk-in showers. Please be aware that we do not have any lifts available. For further accessibility information please call our reservations team.

Are you dog-friendly?
Your dogs are more than welcome at The Knaresborough Inn! We have designated dog-friendly areas of the pub and restaurant, with water bowls and treats available – including our selection of Sir Woofchester’s dog food range.
We offer pet-friendly hotel rooms, so your four-legged friends can join you on your adventures! Please let us know whether you will be bringing a pet at the time of booking to ensure availability. What is the cancellation policy?
Our standard cancellation policy is as follows:
You may cancel your booking without any payment to us at any point before 3pm on the day that is two days before the day your booking starts. After this deadline, the whole booking becomes fully non-refundable. If you try to cancel after this deadline (or if you don’t arrive for your booking), you will still be charged the full price for your booking (including any meals or extras).
Our Advance Purchase Rate cancellation policy is as follows: All bookings on an advance purchase rate are non-refundable and cannot be cancelled by you. If you try to cancel a booking on an advance purchase rate (or if you don’t arrive for your booking), you will still be charged the full price for your booking (including any meals or extras).
Our special offers/promotions cancellation policy is as follows: The cancellation rights for bookings offered on a special offer will be stated during the booking process and in your booking confirmation. Where we do not state any cancellation rights for a special offer, the advance purchase rate cancellation terms (above) will apply.

Do you have any on site bike storage?
There is no on site bike storage available at The Knaresborough Inn.
Please note E-bikes and e-scooters are permitted on our premises provided the battery has been removed before arrival and is stored at least 10 metres away from the building. E-bikes and e-scooters must not be charged anywhere on our premises.
We are unable to provide storage facilities for e-bike or e-scooter batteries. The bike or scooter may be stored on site without its battery fitted.
