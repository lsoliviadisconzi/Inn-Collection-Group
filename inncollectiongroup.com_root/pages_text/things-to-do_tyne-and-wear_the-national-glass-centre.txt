National Glass Centre
Sitting on the bank of the Wear, the National Glass Centre is based in a unique building, constructed of glass and steel. Rotating exhibitions make the museum a brilliant place to visit over and over again, with new exhibits to explore! The museum is also the home of numerous family friendly events and activities.
