Looking for dog friendly pubs in Whitby?
You don’t have to be staying at The Stables to bring your pooch along! Our dog-friendly areas are open to everyone, whether you’re popping in after a walk around Whitby or settling down for a relaxed meal. Your four-legged friend can join you in our designated dog-friendly pub and restaurant areas, with water bowls and treats ready and waiting. Feeling peckish? Sir Woofchester’s dog food range means they can enjoy something tasty too, while the humans tuck into classic pub favourites, all-day dining, Sunday lunch and something refreshing from the bar.
With Whitby’s coastline, harbour and plenty of local walking spots nearby, it’s easy to stretch those paws before heading back to The Stables for some well-earned food and drinks.
Whether you’re looking for dog friendly pubs in Whitby, somewhere to refuel after a seaside stroll or a relaxed spot where the whole family can settle in, pull up a chair and stay awhile. Good food, good drinks and happy hounds – sounds like a pretty good pit stop to us.

Our delicious pub food
If you’re looking for one of the best places to eat in Whitby, The Stables is a fantastic choice. Set just outside the town with sweeping views across the countryside and coast, our welcoming pub combines rustic charm with hearty, locally sourced dishes. From classic pies and juicy burgers to freshly prepared seafood, our menu showcases the very best of pub food in North Yorkshire.
