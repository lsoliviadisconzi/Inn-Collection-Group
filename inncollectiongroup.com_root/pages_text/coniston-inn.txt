Located on the shores of Coniston Water with breathtaking lake and fell views, The Coniston Inn is the perfect escape for anyone looking to unwind in the heart of the Lake District. This handsome, traditional Lakeland slate building sits within its own spacious grounds, offering a warm Cumbrian welcome that makes you feel right at home.
Make the most of the stunning surroundings with scenic walks around our private grounds and the surrounding fells – perfect for families, couples, and dog walkers. We offer dog-friendly accommodation in designated rooms, so you won’t have to leave your four-legged friend behind.
After a day exploring, enjoy our delicious pub food, locally sourced dishes, and refreshing drinks in our welcoming restaurant and bar, all while taking in the views of Coniston Water and the Old Man of Coniston. Book your stay at The Coniston Inn today and experience the perfect Lakeland getaway.

Planning a stay at The Coniston Inn? Our FAQs cover everything from location, parking, accessibility, check-in times and guest amenities you can enjoy during your stay.
Do you have on site parking available?
Yes, The Coniston Inn offers free on-site parking for guests, making it a convenient choice if you’re travelling by car. With easy access to Coniston village, Coniston Water, and nearby Lake District walking routes, parking is hassle-free throughout your stay.

Is there disabled access and accessible rooms at the inn?
There is level access into The Coniston Inn via the front door. There are two accessible rooms available at The Coniston Inn.

Are you dog-friendly?
Your dogs are more than welcome at The Coniston Inn! We have designated dog-friendly areas of the pub and restaurant, with water bowls and treats available – including our selection of Sir Woofchester’s dog food range.
We also offer pet-friendly hotel rooms across a select number of rooms at The Coniston Inn. Please let us know whether you will be bringing a pet at the time of booking to ensure availability and so we can allocate your room accordingly.
When you book direct, up to two of your four-legged friend can be added to your booking for £15 per night. Another perk of booking direct with us at The Inn Collection Group!
For more information on our pet-friendly accommodation, please contact our central reservations team on 0191 580 3610.

What is the cancellation policy?
Our standard cancellation policy is as follows:
You may cancel your booking without any payment to us at any point before 3pm on the day that is two days before the day your booking starts. After this deadline, the whole booking becomes fully non-refundable. If you try to cancel after this deadline (or if you don’t arrive for your booking), you will still be charged the full price for your booking (including any meals or extras).
Our Advance Purchase Rate cancellation policy is as follows: All bookings on an advance purchase rate are non-refundable and cannot be cancelled by you. If you try to cancel a booking on an advance purchase rate (or if you don’t arrive for your booking), you will still be charged the full price for your booking (including any meals or extras).
Our special offers/promotions cancellation policy is as follows: The cancellation rights for bookings offered on a special offer will be stated during the booking process and in your booking confirmation. Where we do not state any cancellation rights for a special offer, the advance purchase rate cancellation terms (above) will apply.

Do you have any on site bike storage?
There is purpose-built steel bike storage at The Coniston Inn. Our bike storage offers 12 lock hoops and electric charging points, including a Paxton Compact Fob lock to keep your bike safe whilst you enjoy a relaxing overnight stay. If you require this please add this option to you booking during the booking process.
Please note E-bikes and e-scooters are permitted on our premises provided the battery has been removed before arrival and is stored at least 10 metres away from the building. E-bikes and e-scooters must not be charged anywhere on our premises.
We are unable to provide storage facilities for e-bike or e-scooter batteries. The bike or scooter may be stored on site without its battery fitted.

Are you family friendly?
The Coniston Inn is a welcoming choice for families, offering comfortable family rooms and a delicious children’s menu designed to keep little ones happy after a day of exploring. Our relaxed pub atmosphere makes family dining easy, with hearty meals for adults and child-friendly favourites for younger guests.
Coniston is an ideal destination for family breaks in the Lake District, with plenty to enjoy nearby. Families can spend time by Coniston Water, enjoy gentle lakeside walks, take a boat trip on the lake, or head to Tarn Hows for an easy, scenic walk suitable for all ages. With outdoor adventures, fresh air, and welcoming accommodation, The Coniston Inn is a great base for a memorable family getaway.

What are the best things to do near Coniston?
Coniston is a fantastic base for exploring the Lake District, with plenty to see and do nearby. Popular activities include walking or cycling around Coniston Water, taking a scenic boat trip on the lake, or hiking the iconic Old Man of Coniston for panoramic views across the fells. Just a short drive away, Tarn Hows offers gentle circular walks and some of the most photographed scenery in the Lakes. Visitors can also explore Coniston village, home to independent shops, cafés, and local heritage attractions, making it ideal for couples, families, and outdoor enthusiasts enjoying a Lake District getaway.

Explore what's near The Coniston Inn
Surrounded by the Lake District’s stunning scenery, The Coniston Inn is an ideal base for outdoor adventures. Coniston village, just over a mile away, offers shops, cafés, and local heritage, while Coniston Water is moments from the inn for lakeside walks, boating, or paddleboarding. Around 4 miles away, Tarn Hows provides circular trails with panoramic fell and woodland views. Classic Lake District walking routes start nearby, including climbs up the Old Man of Coniston, and quiet lanes and off-road trails make the area perfect for cycling. With so much on your doorstep, exploring the best of Cumbria has never been easier.
