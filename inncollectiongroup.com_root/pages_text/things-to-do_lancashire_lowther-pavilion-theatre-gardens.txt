Lowther-Pavilion
Looking for a mix of entertainment and natural beauty in Lancashire?
Lowther Pavilion Theatre & Gardens in Lytham combines live theatre, music, comedy, and film with stunning historic gardens filled with colourful flowerbeds, open lawns, and a charming café.
Visitors can catch a show, enjoy a leisurely walk through the gardens, play tennis or crazy golf, or simply relax with coffee in hand while soaking up the peaceful coastal atmosphere, making it a perfect day or evening out for all ages.
