Dog Friendly at The King's Head Inn
The North York Moors are the perfect place to go exploring with your dog and our pub, situated right at the foot of Roseberry Topping, is surrounded by many dog-friendly walks and trails. That’s why we’re more than happy to accommodate for you and your dog overnight or for the day.
The Kingslodge Inn - double bedrooms
Dog friendly rooms
At The King’s Head Inn, we offer superb dog-friendly accommodation. We welcome well-behaved dogs and pets and ask for a £15 per night supplement fee if you do choose one of these suites. This covers the charge of a special clean that these rooms have to undergo, so you can expect to be welcomed with a clean and comfortable room for you to enjoy.

The King's Head Inn - Bar Area
Dog friendly pub
We’re happy to host your dog in the gardens and in the bar area of our inn too, so you can relax together. There is a pet-friendly area in our pub, where your dog can enjoy a fresh drink of water and complimentary treats.
We’re also open to other pets too, Simply get in touch if you have any queries about this. Please bear in mind that any damage that’s not consistent with general wear and tear will be chargeable to the person responsible for the pet.
