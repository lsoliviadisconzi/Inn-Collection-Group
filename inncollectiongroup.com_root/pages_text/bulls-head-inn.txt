Dating back to the 15th century, The Bull’s Head Inn is a Grade II listed landmark full of character and heritage in the heart of Beaumaris. A welcoming Anglesey inn, it offers charming Beaumaris accommodation perfectly placed for exploring the island’s coastline, historic streets and nearby Snowdonia views.
Just moments from Beaumaris Castle and the Menai Strait, the inn is an ideal base for coastal walks, boat trips to Puffin Island, and discovering the best of North Wales.
A proud regional Pub & Bar of the Year award winner, it’s a standout choice for great food, quality drinks and a traditional Welsh pub atmosphere. With comfortable rooms, locally sourced food and a warm welcome throughout, it’s ideal for dog-friendly stays and relaxed coastal breaks in Anglesey.

Dog Friendly Hotel in Beaumaris
The Bull’s Head Inn is a dog friendly hotel in Beaumaris, with dog-friendly rooms where your four-legged friend can join you for a stay on Anglesey. Simply add your dog when booking to make sure you’re allocated a dog-friendly room.
When you book direct, up to two dogs can stay with you for £15 per night. Dogs are also welcome in our designated dog-friendly areas of the pub and restaurant, with water bowls and treats ready and waiting. Please remember that dogs can’t be left unattended in bedrooms during your stay, but are welcome in our dog-friendly dining areas so can join you every step of the way.
When it’s time to stretch those four legs, Beaumaris seafront is close by, making it easy to head out for some fresh sea air before returning to the inn for some well-earned food and drinks. Whether you’re looking for dog friendly places to stay in Beaumaris or a dog friendly pub with rooms in Beaumaris, The Bull’s Head Inn makes it easy to bring the whole family along.

What is the cancellation policy?
Our standard cancellation policy is as follows:
You may cancel your booking without any payment to us at any point before 3pm on the day that is two days before the day your booking starts. After this deadline, the whole booking becomes fully non-refundable. If you try to cancel after this deadline (or if you don’t arrive for your booking), you will still be charged the full price for your booking (including any meals or extras).
Our Advance Purchase Rate cancellation policy is as follows: All bookings on an advance purchase rate are non-refundable and cannot be cancelled by you. If you try to cancel a booking on an advance purchase rate (or if you don’t arrive for your booking), you will still be charged the full price for your booking (including any meals or extras).
Our special offers/promotions cancellation policy is as follows: The cancellation rights for bookings offered on a special offer will be stated during the booking process and in your booking confirmation. Where we do not state any cancellation rights for a special offer, the advance purchase rate cancellation terms (above) will apply.

Do you have any on site bike storage?
There is no on site bike storage available at The Bull’s Head Inn.

We are unable to provide storage facilities for e-bike or e-scooter batteries. The bike or scooter may be stored on site without its battery fitted.

Is The Bull's Head Inn family friendly?
Yes – The Bull’s Head Inn is a family-friendly hotel in Beaumaris, welcoming guests of all ages for relaxed coastal stays on Anglesey!
We offer comfortable family rooms in Beaumaris, providing plenty of space for parents and children to enjoy a fun and convenient base by the sea. Our children’s menu features a range of tasty favourites, making dining easy and enjoyable for younger guests.
Perfectly located for family days out, there’s plenty nearby to explore including Beaumaris Castle, the Victorian pier, and boat trips to Puffin Island. Families can also enjoy nearby beaches at Red Wharf Bay, coastal walks along the Anglesey Coast Path, and wildlife spotting at Penmon Point lighthouse—all within easy reach of the inn.
Close
How close is Snowdonia National Park to The Bull's Head Inn?
Snowdonia National Park (Eryri) is within easy reach of The Bull’s Head Inn in Beaumaris, making it ideal for combining a coastal and mountain break in one stay.
From the inn, you can reach the edge of the national park in around 30–45 minutes by car, with scenic routes taking you across Anglesey and over the Menai Bridge into mainland North Wales. This gives you easy access to some of the UK’s most iconic landscapes, including Snowdon (Yr Wyddfa), Llanberis Pass, and a network of mountain walking trails.
It’s the perfect base if you want to enjoy both the Anglesey coastline and Snowdonia’s dramatic peaks during your stay.
Close
Are there coastal walks nearby?
Yes, The Bull’s Head Inn is perfectly placed for coastal walks in Beaumaris and across Anglesey, making it an ideal base for walking and beach holidays in North Wales.
From the inn, you can join the Anglesey Coastal Path, which offers miles of scenic walking with dramatic sea views, hidden coves, and wildlife spotting along the way. Popular nearby routes include walks to Penmon Point and its historic lighthouse, where you’ll enjoy views across to Puffin Island, and gentle coastal strolls around Red Wharf Bay, known for its wide sandy shoreline and peaceful scenery.
Beaumaris itself is also great for relaxed walks, with its Victorian pier, castle grounds, and waterfront all within easy reach of the inn.

Explore Beaumaris and Anglesey
Boasting dramatic coastlines, rolling countryside and historic charm, North Wales is one of the UK’s most rewarding destinations for a coastal escape. From things to do in Beaumaris to exploring wider Anglesey attractions, the region offers a mix of natural beauty and rich heritage.
In Beaumaris itself, discover the iconic Beaumaris Castle, stroll the Victorian pier, or explore the town’s independent shops and seafront. Just a short distance away, you’ll find highlights such as Penmon Point lighthouse, Red Wharf Bay, and scenic walking routes along the Anglesey Coastal Path.
