Why dine with The Inn Collection Group?
Great food is about more than what’s on the plate – it’s about the whole experience. Whether you’re joining us after a countryside walk, a day of exploring, or simply catching up with friends and family, you’ll always find a warm welcome waiting.
Our menus celebrate freshly prepared dishes made with seasonal ingredients, from comforting pub classics to lighter bites and indulgent desserts. Pair your meal with a carefully chosen wine, a local ale or your favourite tipple, then settle into our cosy pub surroundings or soak up the atmosphere in our outdoor beer garden or terrace, offering stunning views of some of the UK’s most picturesque locations.
And because no adventure is complete without your four-legged companion, dogs are welcome in our designated dog-friendly dining areas too. With friendly service, delicious food and a relaxed atmosphere, every visit is one to savour.
