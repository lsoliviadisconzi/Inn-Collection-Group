There is something truly special about a waterside stay in the Lake District, and The Wateredge Inn and Windermere Rooms in Ambleside offers one of the most iconic lakeside locations on Lake Windermere. Set right on the shoreline, our welcoming Lake District pub with rooms combines spectacular lake views, relaxed dining and comfortable accommodation, making it the perfect base for exploring the Lake District.
Take a seat on our waterfront terrace and enjoy panoramic views across Windermere, whether you’re stopping by for a drink, a meal, or a memorable lakeside stay. With a warm and traditional pub atmosphere, delicious home-cooked food served all day, every day, and a friendly welcome for both guests and their four-legged companions, The Wateredge Inn is a favourite destination for visitors to Ambleside.
Our dog-friendly accommodation offers everything you’d expect from a relaxing Lake District pub stay, including comfortable rooms, essential amenities, a full cooked breakfast, and convenient on-site parking. Whether you’re returning after a day’s hiking in the fells, exploring the nearby villages, or simply taking in the breathtaking scenery of Lake Windermere, you’ll feel right at home at The Wateredge Inn.

Dog Friendly Hotel near Lake Windermere
Bring your dogs along for a Lake District getaway at the Wateredge Inn, with dog-friendly rooms to make your stay easy for everyone, including our garden rooms, giving you and your pooch more room to relax. Just add your dog when booking and we’ll make sure you’re allocated a dog-friendly room.

The Wateredge Inn FAQs
Heading to The Wateredge Inn for a break in the Lake District? Our FAQs provide helpful details on location, parking, accessibility, check-in times, and the facilities you can enjoy during your visit.
Do you have on site parking available?
We offer complimentary parking in The Wateredge Inn car park for accommodation guests only.  Space is limited on a first come, first served basis and we do not under any circumstances reserve or guarantee parking spaces.  Complimentary parking in our car park is only valid when you are staying at our inn and checked in to a room. Overnight parking is available to guests staying at the hotel in one of our bedrooms only.
Please remember enter your vehicle registration on the tablet at check-in. Guests can enjoy 24 hours free for each night of your stay.
Charges apply to ALL other car park users – please remember to pay for your parking at the car park pay and display machine, via phone or prebook with RingGo. No overnight camping allowed.

Are you dog-friendly?
Your dogs are more than welcome at The Wateredge Inn! We have designated dog-friendly areas of the pub and restaurant, with water bowls and treats available – including our selection of Sir Woofchester’s dog food range.
We also offer a number of pet-friendly rooms, meaning you can enjoy the ultimate getaway with your dogs. Please let us know whether you will be bringing a pet at the time of booking to ensure availability.
When you book direct, up to two of your four-legged friends can be added to your booking for £15 per night. Another perk of booking direct with us at The Inn Collection Group!
For more information on our pet-friendly accommodation, please contact our central reservations team on 0191 580 3610.

What is the cancellation policy?
Our standard cancellation policy is as follows:
You may cancel your booking without any payment to us at any point before 3pm on the day that is two days before the day your booking starts. After this deadline, the whole booking becomes fully non-refundable. If you try to cancel after this deadline (or if you don’t arrive for your booking), you will still be charged the full price for your booking (including any meals or extras).
Our Advance Purchase Rate cancellation policy is as follows: All bookings on an advance purchase rate are non-refundable and cannot be cancelled by you. If you try to cancel a booking on an advance purchase rate (or if you don’t arrive for your booking), you will still be charged the full price for your booking (including any meals or extras).
Our special offers/promotions cancellation policy is as follows: The cancellation rights for bookings offered on a special offer will be stated during the booking process and in your booking confirmation. Where we do not state any cancellation rights for a special offer, the advance purchase rate cancellation terms (above) will apply.

Do you have any on site bike storage?
There is limited storage available on site at The Wateredge Inn and this is subject to availability.
Please note E-bikes and e-scooters are permitted on our premises provided the battery has been removed before arrival and is stored at least 10 metres away from the building. E-bikes and e-scooters must not be charged anywhere on our premises.
We are unable to provide storage facilities for e-bike or e-scooter batteries. The bike or scooter may be stored on site without its battery fitted.
