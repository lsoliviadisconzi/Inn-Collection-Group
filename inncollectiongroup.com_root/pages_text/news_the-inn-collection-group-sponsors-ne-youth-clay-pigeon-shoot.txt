The Inn Collection Group sponsors NE Youth clay pigeon shoot
North East businesses raise money with a bang for NE Youth.
NEYOUTH SHOOTING JULY2022 -145
At the start of July, The Inn Collection Group were proudly amongst a number of North East Businesses to sponsor and support the NE Youth annual clay pigeon shoot.
The weekend, held at the beautiful Hule Priory in Alnwick, saw both a corporate shoot day as well as a John Shallcross memorial shoot day. The weekend raised a whopping £62,000 for NE Youth, a charity which supports disadvantaged young people across the region.
The Inn Collection Group also supplied goody bags to all of the guests, highlighting the range of fantastic offers that are available across the North, as well as some food and drinks offers available specifically in the North East.
