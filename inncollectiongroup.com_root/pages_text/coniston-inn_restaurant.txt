Looking for dog friendly pubs in Coniston?
You don't need to stay overnight to enjoy a visit to The Coniston Inn! Our dog-friendly dining area is open to everyone, whether you're visiting us for a refreshment after a walk or settling in for a leisurely meal. Dogs can join you in our designated dog-friendly pub and restaurant areas, with water bowls and treats ready and waiting. Feeling peckish? Sir Woofchester’s dog food range means they can tuck into something tasty whilst the humans get stuck into classic pub favourites, all-day dining, Sunday lunch and local cask ales.
Coniston is a great place to explore on four paws, with Coniston Waters on your doorstep and plenty of walking routes nearby. Once you’ve worked up an appetite, come back to the inn and put your feet – and paws – up.
So whether you’re looking for dog friendly pubs in Coniston, somewhere to refuel after a walk or simply a spot where the whole pack can relax, pull up a chair and stay awhile. Good food. Good drinks. Wagging tails. All that we're waiting for is you!
