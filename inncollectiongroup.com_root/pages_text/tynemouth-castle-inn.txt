A Tyneside icon for more than 80 years, The Tynemouth Castle Inn sits in a commanding position overlooking the sprawling Longsands Beach of this pretty North Tyneside town.
The Tynemouth Castle Inn blends its heritage setting with modern comfort, offering a relaxed hotel in Tynemouth where sea views, coastal walks and easygoing pub hospitality come together. Wake to the sound of the North Sea, stroll straight onto the beach or explore the charming streets of Tynemouth village before returning to a hearty pub meal and a well-poured drink overlooking the waves.
With comfortable rooms, dog-friendly stays and an unbeatable location on the coastline, The Tynemouth Castle Inn is the perfect base for Tynemouth accommodation, seaside staycations and coastal walking breaks.
Open all day, every day, you’ll fit right inn, enjoying delicious pub food and well-kept drinks from the stunningly grand bar in a sumptuous, cosy interior, or the outdoor terrace where you can enjoy spectacular sea views.

Dog Friendly Hotel in Tynemouth
The Tynemouth Castle Inn is the perfect spot for dog friendly accommodation in Tynemouth. We have plenty of pet-friendly rooms available, including garden rooms offering private outdoor space to give your four-legged friend more space to feel right at home.
When you book direct, up to two dogs can stay with you for £15 per night. Dogs are also welcome in our designated dog-friendly areas of the pub and restaurant, where they’ll find water bowls, treats and Sir Woofchester’s dog food range waiting for them. Please remember that dogs can’t be left unattended in bedrooms during your stay, but can join you for your meals!
And when it’s time to stretch those legs, you won’t have to go far. The Tynemouth Castle Inn is located right on the shores of Longsands Beach, with sweeping sands to explore and only a short coastal walk to Cullercoats and Whitley Bay. Whether you're looking for dog friendly places to stay in Tynemouth or a dog friendly pub with rooms for a coastal break, The Tynemouth Castle Inn makes it easy to bring the whole family along.

The Tynemouth Castle Inn FAQs
Planning a visit to The Tynemouth Castle Inn on the stunning North Tyneside coast? Our FAQs will provide you with quick details on location, parking, accessibility, check-in times and all the facilities available during your stay.
Do you have on site parking available?
We offer complimentary parking at The Tynemouth Castle Inn car park to the rear of the inn for guests only.  Space is limited on a first come, first served basis and we do not under any circumstances reserve or guarantee parking spaces.  Complimentary parking in our car park is only valid either when you are staying at our inn and checked in to a room or are a customer in the bar, restaurant or attending a function. Overnight parking is available to guests staying at the hotel in one of our bedrooms only.
You
MUST
register your vehicle registration number into the tablet on reception if you are a bar, restaurant or function customer.  If you are an accommodation guest, then please register your car registration number with reception.
Diners – 2 hours FREE
Overnight Guests – 24 hours FREE for each night of your stay
Charges apply to ALL other car park users – please remember to pay for your parking at the car park pay and display machine, via phone or prebook with RingGo. No overnight camping allowed.

click here
to visit

website for more info, to pay or how to appeal.

Is The Tynemouth Castle Inn dog-friendly?
Yes! The Tynemouth Castle Inn is a dog friendly pub with rooms in Tynemouth, welcoming four-legged guests throughout the inn. Dogs are welcome in designated areas of the pub and restaurant, with water bowls, treats, and our selection of Sir Woofchester’s dog food range available.
We also offer pet-friendly accommodation across a variety of room types, including terrace rooms with direct outdoor access—perfect for taking your dog out for a stroll. To ensure availability, please let us know when booking if you’ll be bringing a pet.
When you book direct, up to two of your four-legged friends can be added to your booking for £15 per night. Another perk of booking direct with us at The Inn Collection Group!
Simply add your dog on during the online booking process, or for more details about our dog friendly pub with rooms in Tynemouth, contact our central reservations team on 0191 580 3610.

What is the cancellation policy?
Our standard cancellation policy is as follows:
You may cancel your booking without any payment to us at any point before 3pm on the day that is two days before the day your booking starts. After this deadline, the whole booking becomes fully non-refundable. If you try to cancel after this deadline (or if you don’t arrive for your booking), you will still be charged the full price for your booking (including any meals or extras).
Our Advance Purchase Rate cancellation policy is as follows: All bookings on an advance purchase rate are non-refundable and cannot be cancelled by you. If you try to cancel a booking on an advance purchase rate (or if you don’t arrive for your booking), you will still be charged the full price for your booking (including any meals or extras).
Our special offers/promotions cancellation policy is as follows: The cancellation rights for bookings offered on a special offer will be stated during the booking process and in your booking confirmation. Where we do not state any cancellation rights for a special offer, the advance purchase rate cancellation terms (above) will apply.

Do you have any on site bike storage?
There is secure bike storage available at The Tynemouth Castle Inn in a lockable shed.
Please note E-bikes and e-scooters are permitted on our premises provided the battery has been removed before arrival and is stored at least 10 metres away from the building. E-bikes and e-scooters must not be charged anywhere on our premises.
We are unable to provide storage facilities for e-bike or e-scooter batteries. The bike or scooter may be stored on site without its battery fitted.

Is The Tynemouth Castle Inn suitable for walking holidays or coastal breaks?
Absolutely! The Tynemouth Castle Inn is the ideal base for coastal breaks and walking holidays on the North East coast. Step out from the inn straight onto Longsands Beach or explore scenic coastal paths that stretch along the North Sea, perfect for leisurely strolls or longer hikes.
The inn’s clifftop location also puts you close to historic landmarks, such as Tynemouth Priory and Castle, and the charming village with its cafés, boutiques, and weekend markets. For families, there are nearby attractions like Tynemouth Aquarium and plenty of spots for sandcastle-building or ice cream at Cones Ice Cream Parlour.
Whether you’re a couple, a group of friends, or travelling with your dog, the inn provides comfortable dog friendly accommodation in Tynemouth and easy access to the coast, making it a perfect choice for seaside walking holidays.
