So, if you’re looking for country pubs in Berwick-upon-Tweed, look no further than The Lindisfarne Inn. We’re located just 14 minutes from Berwick by car, and only 11 minutes from the famous Holy Island.
If you’ve been exploring Northumberland National Park and visiting places such as Wooler Common or Yeavering Bell Hill Fort, then don’t worry, we’re just a swift 25 minute or so drive from that area. Wherever you are in this area of the UK, you’ll be enjoying food that’s rustic, modern, and great value for money in no time.
Serving homemade food all day, every day, sit down to a great range of dishes that use quality, fresh ingredients from trusted suppliers at The Lindisfarne Inn.  Our chefs have crafted a menu packed with excellent food that is perfect for a spot of lunch or  tucking in to a relaxing evening meal, with picks for the whole family. Operating as one of the many Berwick-upon-Tweed restaurants, we know what visitors to this beautiful, northern part of the world enjoy. Just grab a table, relax, and enjoy!

Looking for dog friendly pubs near Lindisfarne?
You don't need to stay overnight to enjoy a visit to The Lindisfarne Inn! Our dog-friendly dining area is open to everyone, whether you're visiting us for a refreshment after a walk or settling in for a leisurely meal. Dogs can join you in our designated dog-friendly pub and restaurant areas, with water bowls and treats ready and waiting. Feeling peckish? Sir Woofchester’s dog food range means they can tuck into something tasty whilst the humans get stuck into classic pub favourites, all-day dining, Sunday lunch and local cask ales.
With the Northumberland coast and Holy Island nearby, there’s plenty of fresh sea air and open space for your pooch to explore before heading back to the inn for some well-earned food and drinks.

Our delicious pub food
Our food menu is created by our head chef and our team of cooks who have years of experience between them. Changing seasonally, we always make use of the freshest flavours and produce on our menus. Eat in the cosy bar area with its stone-flagged floors and copper bar, or soak up the sun in the beer garden while you dine al fresco
Before you get comfy at your table, check out our daily specials board which rotates regularly and is always packed with flavourful dishes made from local, ethical produce. Families are welcome in the inn’s dining area, where a wide range of family favourites and fantastic children’s dishes will please even the fussiest of eaters.
Sundays are for cosy pubs, and a proper roast. That’s why we have made it a priority to delight guests with cosy settings and delicious roast dinners too. We have generous helpings of hand-carved, juicy meats with all the trimmings, as well as vegetarian and plant-based alternatives.

A brewed recipe for success
As anyone in the beautiful north of England will know, sometimes a good brew is all you need to feel better about the world. As proud northerners ourselves, we've taken this sentiment and crafted it to perfection. To ensure that every hot drink you buy hits the spot, we’ve brewed a recipe for success with our wide range of speciality loose leaf teas and aromatic coffees from the renowned local company Ringtons.
Our well-stocked cellar will bring you wines from around the globe. Sold by the glass or bottle, there are wines to pair with all dishes and to suit every taste. Our rotating selection of cask ales from renowned local breweries are kept in perfect cellar conditions too, and are waiting to be poured and enjoyed by you.

Four-legged fun
Our restaurant is proud to be dog-friendly, and your pets are always welcome. We know they’re part of the family, so there’s no need to leave them at home or arrange a sitter while you dine. Instead, bring them along to relax by your side and soak up the same warm, friendly atmosphere you do. After all, great food and good company are even better when your four-legged friends can be part of the experience.
With our selection of Sir Woofchester's on offer, your dogs can tuck in too - a well deserved treat after joining you on your explorations of Northumberland!
