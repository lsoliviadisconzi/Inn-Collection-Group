Locked In Live Escape Games
Locked In offers live escape team bonding activities, where you can gather friends, family, or colleagues to try and escape out of a locked room by solving various puzzles, riddles, and all sorts of codes and hints.
Locked In Escape Rooms
Locked In have escape rooms at two different locations in North Wales – Towyn and Abergele, within a 10-minute radius of each other.  Escape games are the latest craze taking the nation by storm – a thrilling, mind-bending adventure! Once you and your team of up to 6 people enter the room, the door locks behind you and the countdown begins- 60 minutes to escape! Together, you’ll need to uncover hidden clues and solve tricky puzzles to find your way out.
