Bring your pooch!
Looking for dog friendly pubs in Tynemouth?
You don't need to stay overnight to enjoy a visit to The Tynemouth Castle Inn! Our dog-friendly dining area is open to everyone, whether you're visiting us for a refreshment after a walk or settling in for a leisurely meal. Dogs can join you in our designated dog-friendly pub and restaurant areas, with water bowls and treats ready and waiting. Feeling peckish? Sir Woofchester’s dog food range means they can tuck into something tasty whilst the humans get stuck into classic pub favourites, all-day dining, Sunday lunch and local cask ales.
With views of Longsands Beach and coastal walking routes on the doorstep, you can take your pooch out for a good stretch before settling in for some food and drinks in stunning scenery.
So whether you’re looking for dog friendly pubs in Tynemouth, somewhere to refuel after a walk or simply a spot where the whole pack can relax, pull up a chair and stay awhile. Good food. Good drinks. Wagging tails. What’s not to like?

Our delicious pub food
If you’re looking for the best places to eat in North Tyneside, The Tynemouth Castle Inn is the perfect spot. Perched on the seafront with stunning views across the North Sea, our pub combines coastal charm with hearty, locally sourced dishes. From classic fish and chips and juicy burgers to seasonal seafood specials, our menu showcases the very best of pub food on the North East coast.
Families are always welcome, with a delicious children’s menu designed for our younger guests. Whether you’re after a relaxed lunch with sea views, a cosy dinner, or a pint enjoyed in the fresh coastal air, The Tynemouth Castle Inn is one of the top places to eat and drink in North Tyneside.
