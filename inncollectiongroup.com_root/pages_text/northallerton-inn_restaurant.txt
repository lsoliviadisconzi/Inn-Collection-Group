Dine at The Northallerton Inn
+ 7 items
Relax in the heart of Herriot countryside in North Yorkshire’s welcoming new inn, The Northallerton Inn. Sitting in the centre of the thriving market town of Northallerton, The Northallerton Inn is the newest place to eat, drink, sleep and explore Yorkshire from. Freshly prepared food is served all day so you can catch up with family, friends or colleagues over brunch, lunch and afternoon tea or make a night of it over a splendid evening meal, with food served until 9.00pm.
Serving a wide range of dishes from morning until night, look forward to tucking into delicious, homemade dishes in our relaxed bar and dining areas where there’s plenty of space for everyone to stretch out. You’ll be spoilt for choice with a menu bursting with traditional pub classics alongside modern dishes including plenty of local ingredients and seasonal flavours at every mouthful.
Whether you’re staying with us or not, you’re welcome to pop in and enjoy the fresh flavours and hearty Yorkshire food done right!

Looking for dog friendly pubs in Northallerton?
You don't need to stay overnight to enjoy a visit to The Northallerton Inn! Our dog-friendly dining area is open to everyone, whether you're visiting us for a refreshment after a walk or settling in for a leisurely meal. Dogs can join you in our designated dog-friendly pub and restaurant areas, with water bowls and treats ready and waiting. Feeling peckish? Sir Woofchester’s dog food range means they can tuck into something tasty whilst the humans get stuck into classic pub favourites, all-day dining, Sunday lunch and local cask ales.
With the Yorkshire Dales  and North York Moors close by, you can head out for a walk, take in the views and sniff out some new adventures before refueling in a cosy corner.
So if you’re looking for dog friendly pubs in Northallerton, we have the perfect spot where the whole pack can relax.

We've brewed a recipe for success
Open all day, every day, The Northallerton Inn offers a wide range of cask ales, lagers, ciders, speciality gins and cocktails as well as quality teas, coffees and delicious, creamy hot chocolate – just the ticket after a day’s exploring.
Our well-stocked cellar brings you wines from around the globe. Sold by the glass or bottle, there are wines to pair with all our dishes and to suit every taste.
Or why not chat over a pot of tea or aromatic barista coffee, or our deliciously different Ringtons speciality loose leaf tea? All in all there’s something for everyone at The Northallerton Inn.
