Once a main stopping point for weary stagecoach travellers, The Black Swan is one of the most characterful hotels in Helmsley, with a history dating back to the 15th century. This much-loved inn has welcomed guests for centuries and remains a standout choice among Helmsley hotels for those seeking comfort, charm, and a true sense of place.
Made up of an Elizabethan building at one end, a Tudor structure at the other, and a Georgian house in between, The Black Swan is delightfully higgledy-piggledy, full of original features and historic detail. As one of the most distinctive pubs with rooms in Helmsley, it blends its rich heritage with thoughtful modern touches throughout.
Inside, inviting open fires, cosy armchairs, and a warm welcome set the tone, while our wonderfully refurbished rooms offer the perfect place to bed down after a day exploring Helmsley and the surrounding countryside. Comfortable, characterful, and designed for a restful night’s sleep, they provide an ideal base for visiting Helmsley Castle, Rievaulx Abbey, and Helmsley Walled Garden, before returning to relax and unwind at The Black Swan.

Dog Friendly Hotel in Helmsley
The Black Swan makes it easy to enjoy a getaway with your dog. As a dog friendly hotel in Helmsley, we have dog-friendly rooms where your pooch can settle in after a day of exploring. Just add them to your booking when you reserve your room.
When you book direct, up to two dogs can stay for £15 per night. They’re also welcome in our designated dog-friendly pub and restaurant areas, with water bowls and treats ready and waiting. Dogs can’t be left unattended in bedrooms during your stay.
From wandering Helmsley's market square to discovering nearby parks and country walks, there’s plenty for you and your pooch to explore before heading back for some well-earned food and drinks.
Whether you’re staying for a night or making a weekend of it, The Black Swan is a welcoming base for dog friendly accommodation in Helmsley.

Are you dog-friendly?
Your dogs are more than welcome at The Black Swan! We have designated dog-friendly areas of the pub and restaurant, with water bowls and treats available – including our selection of Sir Woofchester’s dog food range.
We also offer pet-friendly hotel rooms in a variety of room categories across our inn. Please let us know whether you will be bringing a pet at the time of booking to ensure availability and that you are located in a pet friendly room, as we try and keep a limited number of accommodation rooms pet-free.
When you book direct, up to two of your four-legged friend can be added to your booking for £15 per night. Another perk of booking direct with us at The Inn Collection Group!
For more information on our pet-friendly accommodation, please contact our central reservations team on 0191 580 3610.

What is the cancellation policy?
Our standard cancellation policy is as follows:
You may cancel your booking without any payment to us at any point before 3pm on the day that is two days before the day your booking starts. After this deadline, the whole booking becomes fully non-refundable. If you try to cancel after this deadline (or if you don’t arrive for your booking), you will still be charged the full price for your booking (including any meals or extras).
Our Advance Purchase Rate cancellation policy is as follows: All bookings on an advance purchase rate are non-refundable and cannot be cancelled by you. If you try to cancel a booking on an advance purchase rate (or if you don’t arrive for your booking), you will still be charged the full price for your booking (including any meals or extras).
Our special offers/promotions cancellation policy is as follows: The cancellation rights for bookings offered on a special offer will be stated during the booking process and in your booking confirmation. Where we do not state any cancellation rights for a special offer, the advance purchase rate cancellation terms (above) will apply.

Do you have any on site bike storage?
There is a space to store bikes on the ground floor at The Black Swan.
Please note E-bikes and e-scooters are permitted on our premises provided the battery has been removed before arrival and is stored at least 10 metres away from the building. E-bikes and e-scooters must not be charged anywhere on our premises.
We are unable to provide storage facilities for e-bike or e-scooter batteries. The bike or scooter may be stored on site without its battery fitted.

How old is The Black Swan in Helmsley?
The Black Swan in Helmsley dates back to the 15th century, making it one of the town’s most historic and characterful hotels in Helmsley, North Yorkshire. Originally a stop for weary stagecoach travellers, the inn has welcomed guests for centuries, blending its rich heritage with modern comforts. Today, visitors can enjoy cosy rooms, hearty meals in the on-site pub and restaurant, and a warm Yorkshire welcome, all while soaking up the unique history and charm that have made The Black Swan a landmark in Helmsley.

What is Helmsley famous for?
Helmsley is famous for its rich history, charming market town atmosphere, and stunning surrounding landscapes in North Yorkshire. Visitors flock to explore Helmsley Castle, the historic ruins set in landscaped gardens, and the nearby Rievaulx Abbey, one of England’s most impressive Cistercian monasteries. The town is also renowned for its independent shops, cafés, and local food scene, making it a favourite destination for both day-trippers and those seeking longer breaks. With walking trails, scenic countryside, and a vibrant town centre, Helmsley offers a perfect mix of culture, heritage, and outdoor adventure.
