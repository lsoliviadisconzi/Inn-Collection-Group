Blackpool Zoo
A multi-award winning zoo with over 1000 animals and 37 beautiful acres of land, this zoo is a fun visit for the everyone!
Blackpool zoo 2
You can enjoy close encounters with over 1000 animals including many favourites! From aardvarks to zebras, cockroaches to camels- they're all at Blackpool Zoo!
The zoo stands out in the resort, as being an attraction that the whole family can visit together. If you're planning a visit the zoo has everything you could ever need, from cafes for food and drink to the gift shop upon exit where you will find a selection of cuddly animal toys, books, models and plenty of souvenirs to remember your day by.
