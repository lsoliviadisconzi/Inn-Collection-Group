The Wordsworth has reopened following an exciting refurbishment
, and we can’t wait to welcome you to our beautifully refreshed restaurant and bar. Start your day with a hearty Full English breakfast, tuck into elevated pub classics crafted with seasonal ingredients, and finish on a sweet note with one of our indulgent desserts.

Our delicious pub food
At The Wordsworth, our menus celebrate the very best of seasonal ingredients, bringing together comforting pub classics with elevated dishes, all thoughtfully prepared by our talented chefs.
Sink into a cosy corner by the fire, enjoy a leisurely meal in our stylish dining spaces or make the most of our inviting outdoor terrace when the Cumbrian weather allows. Whether you're joining us for a relaxed lunch, an evening with friends or a special family occasion, you'll find delicious food, carefully selected drinks and friendly hospitality at the heart of every visit.
From guests staying with us to those calling in after a day on the fells, everyone can expect a warm welcome from the moment they arrive. Whether it's a light bite and a perfectly poured pint or a memorable evening meal, The Wordsworth offers a relaxed yet refined dining experience in the heart of Grasmere.

A brewed recipe for success
The Wordsworth’s well-stocked cellar brings you wines from around the world. Available by the glass or bottle, there are wines to pair with all dishes and to suit every taste.
