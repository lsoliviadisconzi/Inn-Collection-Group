Our delicious pub food
A visit to The Seaburn Inn wouldn’t be complete without great food to match the sea views. Our menu brings together comforting pub classics and modern favourites, all freshly prepared to satisfy every appetite. From hearty pies and fish & chips to lighter seasonal dishes, there’s something for everyone to enjoy. We’re happy to cater for dietary requirements too – just let our friendly team know, and we’ll make sure your meal is both suitable and delicious.
Whether you’re joining us for a relaxed lunch, a family dinner, or a pint and a bite after a walk along the seafront, The Seaburn Inn is the perfect place to eat, drink, and unwind on the North East coast.
