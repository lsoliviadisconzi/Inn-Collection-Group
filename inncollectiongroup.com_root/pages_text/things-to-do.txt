Explore Northumberland
Only in Northumberland will you find more castles than any other county in England, experience unrivalled wildlife opportunities hopping aboard a boat to the Farne Islands. Skim across the causeway to Holy Island and have endless stretches of beach and vast landscapes almost to yourself. There’s world class attractions including The Alnwick Garden and Bamburgh Castle to visit all year round, while bustling market towns like Amble and historic Berwick-upon-Tweed are a shopper’s paradise.

Visit Durham
Explore County Durham
Tucked on a loop of the River Wear, Durham’s magnificent cathedral and castle form a World Heritage site. Wander Durham’s cobbled streets or stretch your legs further with a hike through Durham Dales, home to spectacular Highforce Waterfall. Head seawards for a clifftop hike along Durham’s rugged Heritage Coast. Step back in time at must-visit Beamish Open Air Museum and see history brought to life at annual outdoor extravaganza, Kynren. Just three hours by train from Kings Cross, Durham is easy to reach.

Explore Yorkshire
Renowned for its beautiful landscapes including the famous Yorkshire Dales and North York Moors, breathtaking coastline with famous seaside resorts including Whitby, Staithes and Scarborough. There are charming market towns to discover and historic and cultural sites just waiting to be delved into. Birthplace of the famous Yorkshire Pudding and home of Yorkshire Tea and Wensleydale Cheese, there are so many reasons why Yorkshire is a brilliant place to explore.

Tyne and Wear
Explore Tyne and Wear
Known as one of the friendliest places in England, Tyne and Wear is filled with award-winning beaches, vibrant cities, fantastic attractions and so much more. With the best of both worlds, enjoy the cultural quayside city of Newcastle upon Tyne and the sandy beaches at Tynemouth where the surf is almost always up. Brimming with historical spots to discover too, the warmest of welcomes is just the beginning.
