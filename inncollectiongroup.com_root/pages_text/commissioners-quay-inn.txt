Taking prime position on Blyth’s historic quay area, step inside The Commissioners Quay Inn for fantastic home-made food, a wide range of drinks, fabulous accommodation and a very warm welcome every time.

Overlooking the River Blyth and out towards the sea, this vibrant and welcoming pub with rooms has all day, every day laid-back dining, delicious drinks, and 40 contemporary en-suite bedrooms.
Boasting a modern seaside style with a hint of Miami art-deco The Commissioners Quay Inn is where you’ll fit right inn when staying in South Northumberland.

Dog Friendly Hotel in Blyth
The Commissioners Quay Inn is a dog friendly hotel in Blyth, with dog-friendly rooms across all room categories where your four-legged friend can join you for an adventure in Northumberland. Simply add your dog when booking to make sure you’re allocated a dog-friendly room.
When you book direct, up to two dogs can stay with you for £15 per night. Dogs are also welcome in our designated dog-friendly areas of the pub and restaurant, with water bowls and treats ready and waiting. Please remember that dogs can’t be left unattended in bedrooms during your stay, but can join you every step of the way.
When it’s time to stretch those four legs, you’re already got the sea on your doorstep for harbor strolls or a trip to discover more of the Northumberland coast. After a day exploring, head back to the inn for some well-earned food and drinks.
Whether you’re looking for dog friendly places to stay in Blyth or a dog friendly pub with rooms in Northumberland, The Commissioners Quay Inn is a great base for exploring Blyth and beyond.

The Commissioners Quay Inn FAQs
Make the most of your visit to The Commissioners Quay Inn in Blyth, Northumberland. Explore our FAQs for details on location, parking, accessibility, check-in times and guest amenities.
Does The Commissioners Quay Inn have parking?
There is free on site car parking available at The Commissioners Quay Inn. Parking is first come first served.
Close
What are check-in and check-out times, and is there a late check-in fee?
Check in at The Commissioner’s Quay Inn is from 3pm, and check out is 10:30am.
If you wish to check in after 11pm there will be an automatic late check in fee of £25. Please contact us if you will be arriving after this time.
Close
Is there disabled access and accessible rooms at the inn?
The Commisioner’s Quay Inn is a purpose built inn with lift access to all rooms and level access to the bar and restaurant. There are three accessible rooms available at The Commissioner’s Quay Inn.
Please note the total height of the bed and the mattress approximately 70cm.
For further accessibility information please call our reservations team.

Is The Commissioners Quay Inn dog-friendly?
Your dogs are more than welcome at The Commissioners Quay Inn! We have designated dog-friendly areas of the pub and restaurant, with water bowls and treats available – including our selection of Sir Woofchester’s dog food range. Soak up the sun from our beer garden, or get cosy inside with your pets.
We offer pet-friendly hotel rooms across all room categories at The Commissioners Quay Inn. Please let us know whether you will be bringing a pet at the time of booking to ensure availability and so our team members are aware before your visit. When you book direct, up to two of your four-legged friend can be added to your booking for £15 per night. Another perk of booking direct with us at The Inn Collection Group!
For more information on our pet-friendly accommodation, please contact our central reservations team on 0191 580 3610.

What is the cancellation policy?
Our standard cancellation policy is as follows:
You may cancel your booking without any payment to us at any point before 3pm on the day that is two days before the day your booking starts. After this deadline, the whole booking becomes fully non-refundable. If you try to cancel after this deadline (or if you don’t arrive for your booking), you will still be charged the full price for your booking (including any meals or extras).
Our Advance Purchase Rate cancellation policy is as follows: All bookings on an advance purchase rate are non-refundable and cannot be cancelled by you. If you try to cancel a booking on an advance purchase rate (or if you don’t arrive for your booking), you will still be charged the full price for your booking (including any meals or extras).
Our special offers/promotions cancellation policy is as follows: The cancellation rights for bookings offered on a special offer will be stated during the booking process and in your booking confirmation. Where we do not state any cancellation rights for a special offer, the advance purchase rate cancellation terms (above) will apply.

Do you have any on site bike storage?
There is no official indoor bike storage available at The Commissioner’s Quay Inn.
Please note E-bikes and e-scooters are permitted on our premises provided the battery has been removed before arrival and is stored at least 10 metres away from the building. E-bikes and e-scooters must not be charged anywhere on our premises.
We are unable to provide storage facilities for e-bike or e-scooter batteries. The bike or scooter may be stored on site without its battery fitted.
