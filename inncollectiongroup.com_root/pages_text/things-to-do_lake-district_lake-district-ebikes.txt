E-Bikes Rental
E-Bike Safaris provide Electric Bikes for hire, delivered directly to customers accommodation in the South and Central Lakes.
E-Bikes parked next to a lake
E Bike safaris provide high spec Electric Hybrid Bicycles (new and larger fleet size for 2024) and Tricycles as well as children’s bike seats, tag-alongs and child trailers. Each E-Bike comes with a set of panniers so customers can take spare clothing, shop on route and/or take packed lunches. Phone mounts and a navigation device with pre-loaded tailor-made routes are also provided on the handlebars for easy navigation along the country lanes and trails. So customers can enjoy the Lake District by cycling from their accommodation, visit local attractions and participate in other leisure activities along the way.
