Number 2: Bamburgh, Northumberland
It’s easy to see why Bamburgh has come in at a strong second place in the UK’s top staycation destinations for 2026, only behind St. Ives in Cornwall. The award-winning North-East coastline stretches for miles, with Bamburgh Castle sitting proudly at the top. With seasonal displays and
events guide
, a trip to the castle is definitely not one to miss for history fanatics and families this summer.
If you are a wildlife enthusiast, then the
Farne Islands
are just a short boat trip away off the Northumberland coast. Here, visitors can spot breeding seabird colonies, grey seals and, on rare occasions, even orcas during the summer months.

Number 3: Ambleside, Lake District
The bustling Lake District town of Ambleside comes in at number three of the top staycation locations to visit for 2026. This charming town is one of the Lake District’s most popular destinations, thanks to its stunning scenery and vibrant atmosphere. From winding cobbled streets filled with local delicacies, to the towering fells overlooking the town, the dramatic scenery and cosy atmosphere makes trips here unforgettable!
Windermere
Only a short 15 minute walk from the town center will take you right to the shores of Lake Windermere – the biggest in the Lake District!
Here you can take one of the many hikes around the lake and see the water from new angles, try one of the many water sports available during the summer months, or just sit back and take in the scenic views with a drink in hand.

Number 4: Grasmere, Lake District
Another Lake District gem features among the highest-ranked staycation destinations for 2026. This time, we’re heading just north of Ambleside, in the town of Grasmere. This quiet village has become popular thanks to its rich history, scenic views and friendly charm.
One of the notable attractions to see in Grasmere is the history of the poet Wordsworth, where his grave and former house,
Dove Cottage
, are able to be visited, as well as the many daffodils in the gardens. Other key attractions include the famous
Sarah Nelson’s gingerbread store
, proudly serving the ‘world’s best gingerbread’, hikes around Grasmere Lake, and Rydal Cave’s stepping stones, which is just a small walk away.
Where to stay in Grasmere
For accommodation in Grasmere, situated just a short walk from the village centre is The Swan, Grasmere. This charming coaching inn combines traditional character with modern comforts, making it the ideal base for exploring Grasmere and the surrounding fells.

Number 5: Robin Hood’s Bay, North Yorkshire
Yorkshire makes an appearance at number five on the top staycation spots, with Robin Hood’s Bay in North Yorkshire. This charming fishing village offers seaside views and dramatic cliffs on the Heritage Coast of the North York Moors. The narrow, cobbled alleyways make you feel like you’re going back in time. With its dramatic coastline, winding streets and famously warm Yorkshire welcome, it’s no surprise Robin Hood’s Bay ranks among the UK’s favourite staycation destinations.
For those who love the great outdoors, Robin Hood’s Bay is packed with opportunities for adventure. Wander along the sandy beach in search of fossils, or lace up your walking boots and explore stretches of the
Cleveland Way,
which offers breathtaking views of the Heritage Coast. The village is also the traditional finishing point of Wainwright’s iconic Coast to Coast Walk, making it a must-visit destination for keen walkers and nature lovers alike.
Where to Stay Near Robin Hood’s Bay
Located just a 15-minute drive away is the historic town of Whitby, located just north of Robin Hood’s Bay, and makes the perfect makes the perfect base for your trip, where you can also explore the history of Whitby. Located in one of Yorkshire’s most iconic seaside towns, the inn offers a comfortable base from which to discover the area’s rich history, stunning beaches and scenic walking routes. You’ll be perfectly placed to experience the very best of the Yorkshire coast.
