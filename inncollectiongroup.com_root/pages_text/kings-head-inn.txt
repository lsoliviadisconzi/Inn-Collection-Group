A quintessential country pub with rooms nestled under picture-postcard Roseberry Topping, our Great Ayton hotel The King’s Head Inn is your gateway to Captain Cook Country and superb walks around the North York Moors National Park and renowned Cleveland Way.

With a cosy pub offering delicious food, well-kept drinks, and the warm welcome of a traditional North Yorkshire inn, you’ll feel right at home from the moment you step inside this village pub. Making the most of its tranquil setting, the outdoor terraces provide the perfect place to unwind and relax.

Do you have on site parking available?
We offer complimentary parking at The King’s Head Inn car park to the rear of the inn for guests only.  Space is limited on a first come, first served basis and we do not under any circumstances reserve or guarantee parking spaces.  Complimentary parking in our car park is only valid either when you are staying at our inn and checked in to a room or are a customer in the bar, restaurant or attending a function. Overnight parking is available to guests staying at the hotel in one of our bedrooms only.

What is the cancellation policy?
Our standard cancellation policy is as follows:
You may cancel your booking without any payment to us at any point before 3pm on the day that is two days before the day your booking starts. After this deadline, the whole booking becomes fully non-refundable. If you try to cancel after this deadline (or if you don’t arrive for your booking), you will still be charged the full price for your booking (including any meals or extras).
Our Advance Purchase Rate cancellation policy is as follows: All bookings on an advance purchase rate are non-refundable and cannot be cancelled by you. If you try to cancel a booking on an advance purchase rate (or if you don’t arrive for your booking), you will still be charged the full price for your booking (including any meals or extras).
Our special offers/promotions cancellation policy is as follows: The cancellation rights for bookings offered on a special offer will be stated during the booking process and in your booking confirmation. Where we do not state any cancellation rights for a special offer, the advance purchase rate cancellation terms (above) will apply.

What is there to do in Great Ayton?
Great Ayton is a charming North Yorkshire village with plenty to discover. History enthusiasts can visit the Captain Cook Schoolroom Museum to learn about the famous explorer’s early life, while High Green offers a picturesque spot to relax in the heart of the village. Enjoy a stroll alongside the River Leven, browse the independent shops and cafés, or head out on foot to climb the iconic Roseberry Topping for spectacular views across the surrounding countryside.
Close
Can I get to Great Ayton by train?
Yes. Great Ayton has its own railway station, making it easy to visit without a car. Great Ayton railway station is served by the Esk Valley Line, which runs between Middlesbrough and Whitby, connecting the village with both the coast and nearby towns. From the station, the King’s Head Inn is just a short walk away.
Close
Is Great Ayton on the Cleveland Way?
While the Cleveland Way doesn’t pass directly through the centre of Great Ayton, it runs within easy reach of the village, making it an excellent base for walkers exploring this famous National Trail. The King’s Head Inn is a convenient place to stay before or after tackling sections of the route, offering comfortable accommodation, hearty food and a warm Yorkshire welcome.

Visit Great Ayton by Train
Leave the car behind and enjoy a stress-free escape to the North York Moors. Great Ayton railway station is served by the Esk Valley Line, with regular connections to Middlesbrough and Whitby, where you can connect to services from destinations across the UK, including London, York, Newcastle and Edinburgh.
The station is just a short five-minute walk from The King’s Head Inn. Simply leave the station, head along Station Road towards the village centre, and you’ll soon arrive at our welcoming inn, ready to check in, enjoy a bite to eat or set off to explore Great Ayton and nearby Roseberry Topping on foot.
