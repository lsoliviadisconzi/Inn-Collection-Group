Looking for dog friendly pubs in Ambleside?
You don't need to stay overnight to enjoy a visit to The Ambleside Inn! Our dog-friendly dining area is open to everyone, whether you're visiting us for a refreshment after a walk or settling in for a leisurely meal. Dogs can join you in our designated dog-friendly pub and restaurant areas, with water bowls and treats ready and waiting. Feeling peckish? Sir Woofchester’s dog food range means they can tuck into something tasty whilst the humans get stuck into classic pub favourites, all-day dining, Sunday lunch and local cask ales.
With Ambleside’s quaint streets and Lake District trails close by, there’s plenty of opportunity for a good walk before heading back to the inn for some well-earned food and drinks.
So whether you’re looking for dog friendly pubs in Ambleside, somewhere to refuel after a walk or simply a spot where the whole pack can relax, pull up a chair and stay awhile. Good food. Good drinks. Wagging tails. All that's waiting is for you to tuck-in!
