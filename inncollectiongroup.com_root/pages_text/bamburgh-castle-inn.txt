When it comes to seaside escapes, The Bamburgh Castle Inn is one of the most popular hotels in Seahouses. Perched on the harbourside in this charming fishing village, the inn offers stunning views of Bamburgh Castle and the Farne Islands, making it the perfect base for exploring the Northumberland Coast.
A previous gold award-winner with the North East England Tourism Awards and a finalist in the Great British Pub Awards, The Bamburgh Castle Inn is celebrated for its historic charm, welcoming atmosphere, and relaxed seaside vibe. Known for hearty home-cooked pub food and serving a proper pint of Northumberland’s finest, it remains a firm favourite with both visitors and locals.
As one of the most inviting dog-friendly hotels in Seahouses, the inn combines cosy rooms with a welcoming pub and restaurant, offering excellent value for money and a more personal experience than self-catering.
Please note: There is no parking on site, there is a pay and display car park available opposite The Bamburgh Castle Inn.

Dog Friendly Hotel in Seahouses
The Bamburgh Castle Inn is the paw-fect pick for a dog friendly hotel in Seahouses, with dog-friendly rooms across all room categories so the whole family can enjoy an adventure. Simply add your dog when booking to make sure you’re allocated a dog-friendly room.
When you book direct, up to two dogs can stay with you for £15 per night. Please remember that dogs can’t be left unattended in bedrooms during your stay, but can join you every step of the way.
When it’s time to stretch those four legs, you’re already in the heart of the Northumberland coast, with the lively streets of Seahouses to wander, as well as the impressive coastal walk to Bamburgh Castle. For dog friendly accommodation near Bamburgh, look no further!

Do you have on site parking available?
There is no parking on site, there is a pay and display car park available opposite The Bamburgh Castle Inn.

Is there disabled access and accessible rooms at the inn?
The Bamburgh Castle Inn has disabled access into the pub via the ramp on left handside of the main building. There are two designated accessible rooms available at The Bamburgh Castle Inn.
Please note the total height of the bed and the mattress approximately 70cm.
For further accessibility information please call our reservations team.

Are you dog-friendly?
Your dogs are more than welcome at The Bamburgh Castle Inn! We have designated dog-friendly areas of the pub and restaurant, with water bowls and treats available – including our selection of Sir Woofchester’s dog food range.
We offer pet-friendly hotel rooms in a variety of room categories across our inn. Please let us know whether you will be bringing a pet at the time of booking to ensure availability. When you book direct, up to two of your four-legged friend can be added to your booking for £15 per night. Another perk of booking direct with us at The Inn Collection Group!
For more information on our pet-friendly accommodation, please contact our central reservations team on 0191 580 3610.

What is the cancellation policy?
Our standard cancellation policy is as follows:
You may cancel your booking without any payment to us at any point before 3pm on the day that is two days before the day your booking starts. After this deadline, the whole booking becomes fully non-refundable. If you try to cancel after this deadline (or if you don’t arrive for your booking), you will still be charged the full price for your booking (including any meals or extras).
Our Advance Purchase Rate cancellation policy is as follows: All bookings on an advance purchase rate are non-refundable and cannot be cancelled by you. If you try to cancel a booking on an advance purchase rate (or if you don’t arrive for your booking), you will still be charged the full price for your booking (including any meals or extras).
Our special offers/promotions cancellation policy is as follows: The cancellation rights for bookings offered on a special offer will be stated during the booking process and in your booking confirmation. Where we do not state any cancellation rights for a special offer, the advance purchase rate cancellation terms (above) will apply.

Do you have any on site bike storage?
We have a small bike rack outside our annex building. We don’t have any lockable storage for bikes available.
Please note E-bikes and e-scooters are permitted on our premises provided the battery has been removed before arrival and is stored at least 10 metres away from the building. E-bikes and e-scooters must not be charged anywhere on our premises.
We are unable to provide storage facilities for e-bike or e-scooter batteries. The bike or scooter may be stored on site without its battery fitted.

Are you family friendly?
The Bamburgh Castle Inn is a welcoming choice for families, offering comfortable family rooms and a tasty Kids’ Menu to keep little ones happy. With cosy dining spaces and a relaxed pub atmosphere, mealtimes are easy and enjoyable for all ages.
Seahouses itself is perfect for a family getaway, with plenty to explore nearby, including Bamburgh Castle, Seahouses Beach, scenic coastal walks, and boat trips to the Farne Islands to spot seals and puffins. Whether you’re enjoying outdoor adventures, discovering local history, or simply relaxing by the sea, The Bamburgh Castle Inn provides the ideal base for an unforgettable family break on the Northumberland coast.

Is there much to do in Seahouses and Bamburgh?
Absolutely! Seahouses and Bamburgh are brimming with activities for all kinds of visitors, making them ideal destinations on the Northumberland coast. From exploring the iconic Bamburgh Castle and strolling along Seahouses Beach to discovering the history at the RNLI Grace Darling Museum, there’s plenty to keep families, couples, and solo travellers entertained. Outdoor enthusiasts can enjoy coastal walks, cycling routes, and boat trips to the Farne Islands, home to seals, puffins, and other wildlife. With a wealth of shops, pubs, and restaurants in Bamburgh and Seahouses, plus scenic views at every turn, there’s something for everyone to enjoy.

Nestled in the harbourside village of Seahouses, The Bamburgh Castle Inn is perfectly positioned for exploring the best of the Northumberland coast. Visitors can enjoy the iconic Bamburgh Castle, wander along Seahouses Beach, or take in the fresh sea air on one of the many scenic coastal walking routes.
The surrounding area offers something for everyone, from families looking for a fun day out to walkers, cyclists, and history enthusiasts eager to explore landmarks like the RNLI Grace Darling Museum or the charming streets of Bamburgh village. Whether you’re after cultural heritage, outdoor adventure, or simply a peaceful seaside retreat, the area around The Bamburgh Castle Inn provides the perfect base for an unforgettable Northumberland getaway.
