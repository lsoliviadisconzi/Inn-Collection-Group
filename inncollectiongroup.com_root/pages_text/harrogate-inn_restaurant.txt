Looking for dog friendly pubs in Harrogate?
You don't need to stay overnight to enjoy a visit to The Harrogate Inn! Our dog-friendly dining area is open to everyone, whether you're visiting us for a refreshment after a walk or settling in for a leisurely meal. Dogs can join you in our designated dog-friendly pub and restaurant areas, with water bowls and treats ready and waiting. Feeling peckish? Sir Woofchester’s dog food range means they can tuck into something tasty whilst the humans get stuck into classic pub favourites, all-day dining, Sunday lunch and local cask ales.
Whether you fancy a leisurely stroll through town or a longer wander through the surrounding countryside, there are plenty of ways to get those paws moving before refueling back at the inn.
So if you’re looking for dog friendly pubs in Harrogate, pull up a chair and stay awhile. Good food. Good drinks. Wagging tails. All that's waiting is you!

Our delicious pub food
If you’re looking for one of the best places to eat in Harrogate, The Harrogate Inn is the perfect spot. Located in the heart of this elegant spa town, our welcoming pub combines relaxed Yorkshire charm with hearty, locally sourced dishes. From classic pies and juicy burgers to freshly prepared seasonal specials, our menu showcases the very best of pub food in Yorkshire.
We’re passionate about fresh, local produce, with menus that change throughout the year to reflect the seasons. Families are always welcome, with a delicious children’s menu designed for younger guests. Whether you’re after a leisurely lunch, a cosy dinner in our inviting restaurant, or a drink with friends in our beer garden, The Harrogate Inn is the ideal place.
