The Baltic
Sitting on the South bank of the river Tyne, The Baltic is a contemporary art gallery, located in a converted traditional flour mill.
BALTIC Centre for Contemporary Art
The Baltic houses a wide range of exhibitions throughout the magnificent building. The exhibitions are frequently changing, allowing a variety of international artists to be displayed. Numerous events are also hosted at the Baltic, as well as interactive activities for families.
BALTIC Centre for Contemporary Art
On the 4th level of The Baltic, you will find a viewing platform, looking over the River Tyne. The platform provides a view of the iconic Newcastle bridges, as well as a glimpse over the city itself.
