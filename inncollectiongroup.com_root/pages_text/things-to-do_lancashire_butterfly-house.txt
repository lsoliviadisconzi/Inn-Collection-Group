Williamson Park
The butterfly house is located in Williamson Park, it is newly renovated and displays mini-beasts, a bird enclosure and a small mammal section.
Here to fascinate the whole family and add an exotic touch to your visit to Lancaster. Whatever the weather you can be sure of a warm welcome as you walk amongst some of the world's most stunning butterfly species. They have everything from the blue morpho, emerald swallowtails to cleverly camouflaged owl butterflies.
Learn about the diet and lifecycle of a butterfly as you wander around the stunning grounds of Williamson Park.
