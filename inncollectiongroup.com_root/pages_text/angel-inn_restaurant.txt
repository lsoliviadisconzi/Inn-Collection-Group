Taking full advantage of Cumbria’s rich source of local produce, The Angel Inn at Bowness-on-Windermere proudly serves up delicious, wholesome pub food all day, every day, cooked freshly for you upon ordering. Crowd pleasing favourites sit alongside Cumbrian classics. Look out for flavoursome modern twists on the menu, too.
Start the day with a fine breakfast that will see you through a day’s climbing or taking in the sights. Pop by for brunches and lunches with family or colleagues, while delicious evening meals always pack a punch, as do our irresistibly moreish homemade puddings.
A firm favourite among families, kids love The Angel Inn’s tasty Children’s Menu with plenty of choices for little ones plus there’s plenty of space for everyone to spread out.

Looking for a dog friendly pub in Bowness-on-Windermere?
You don't need to stay overnight to enjoy a visit to The Angel Inn! Our dog-friendly dining area is open to everyone, whether you're visiting us for a refreshment after a walk or settling in for a leisurely meal. Dogs can join you in our designated dog-friendly pub and restaurant areas, with water bowls and treats ready and waiting. Feeling peckish? Sir Woofchester’s dog food range means they can tuck into something tasty whilst the humans get stuck into classic pub favourites, all-day dining, Sunday lunch and local cask ales.
Fancy a wander to work up an appetite? Bowness and the shores of Windermere are yours to explore, giving your pooch plenty of opportunities to stretch their legs before it’s time for food and drinks.
So whether you’re looking for dog friendly pubs in Bowness, somewhere to refuel after a walk or simply a spot where the whole pack can relax, pull up a chair and stay awhile. Good food. Good drinks. Wagging tails. What’s not to love?
