Dine at The Kingslodge Inn

Escape, relax, and let us take care of you at The Kingslodge Inn. With cosy log fires, comfy sofas, high-back chairs, and stone-clad floors, The Kingslodge Inn just on the edge of Durham city centre is the perfect dining escape.
Good food done right is hard to beat – and that’s exactly what we aim for at The Kingslodge Inn. Our menu is packed with tasty dishes made from quality, seasonal ingredients, perfect whether you’re starting your day in Durham, grabbing lunch, or settling in for a relaxed evening meal.
Our location makes dining here extra special. Tucked beside the peaceful Flass Vale Nature Reserve, you can enjoy food and drinks in our beer garden when the sun’s out, or head indoors to our cosy bar and restaurant when it’s not.
Whether you’re staying with us or just popping by, The Kingslodge Inn is all about great food, a warm welcome, and a laid-back atmosphere that keeps guests coming back.

Looking for dog friendly pubs in Durham?
Your four-legged friend is welcome at The Kingslodge Inn whether you’re staying overnight or just popping in for a bite, a drink or a well-earned pit stop after a walk.
Dogs can join you in our designated dog-friendly pub and restaurant areas, with water bowls and treats ready and waiting. Feeling peckish? Sir Woofchester’s dog food range means they can tuck into something tasty while the humans get stuck into classic pub favourites, Sunday lunches, or local cask ales.
Then there’s the handy location to consider. With Flass Vale right alongside the inn, you can take your pooch for a woodland wander before settling in for some food and drinks.
So whether you’re looking for dog friendly pubs in Durham, somewhere to refuel after a walk or simply a spot where the whole pack can relax, pull up a chair and stay awhile. Good food. Good drinks. Wagging tails. What’s not to like?

Explore pub favourites in Durham
If you’re looking for one of the best places to eat in Durham, The Kingslodge Inn is a great choice. Just a short walk from the city centre yet tucked beside the peaceful greenery of Flass Vale, our pub combines a relaxed setting with hearty, freshly prepared dishes. From comforting pies and stacked burgers to traditional fish and chips, our menu celebrates proper pub food made with care.
Families are always welcome, with a children’s menu full of tasty favourites for younger diners. Whether you’re after a leisurely lunch, a cosy dinner by the fire, or drinks in our garden surrounded by nature, The Kingslodge Inn is the perfect place in Durham to eat, drink, and unwind - with walk-ins always welcome.

A brewed recipe for success
There is a great deal to do in Durham and, after a busy day taking in the sights and sounds of the city centre, a cup of tea or artisan coffee is the only thing that's going to hit the spot. And, as northerners, we know the importance of a good hot drink! As a result, we've worked hard to offer a wide range of hot drinks from flat whites and cappuccinos to good old-fashioned cups of tea provided by Ringtons.
Our well-stocked cellar brings an assortment of drinks, from wines spanning around the world, to popular, hand-pulled cask ales that have been lovingly brewed by local breweries right here in our home city of Durham. Just pull up a chair and enjoy!
