When will my points hit my account after I make a reservation?
Points linked to a reservation booking will be added to your account after you check out.  Your points will be applied overnight and applied to your account the next day.
Points linked to food and drink will be applied in around 20 minutes.  If your points move, you into a new tier then this will happen overnight, and you will be moved into the new tier the next day.
