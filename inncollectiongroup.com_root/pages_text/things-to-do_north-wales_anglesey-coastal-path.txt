Isle of Anglesey Coastal Path
Explore the Isle of Anglesey, filled with wild and unspolit landscapes.
Anglesey
Anglesey
For a breath of fresh air and some of the most stunning scenery Wales has to offer, the Anglesey Coastal Path is a trail worth adding to your list. This route spans 200km in total, covering the coastline of Wales' largest island. From lighthouses to historic ruins, as well as some local puffins you may pass along the way, this path has it all.
The route is clearly waymarked and divided into manageable sections, so you can explore at your own pace - whether you're tackling the full island circuit or just enjoying a relaxed coastal stroll for the day.
Along the way, you’ll encounter some of Anglesey’s most dramatic scenery, from towering cliffs to sweeping bays. Stop off at iconic landmarks, soak in the views, or simply pause to breathe in the fresh sea air. Whether you're after a solo hike, a weekend adventure, or a quiet moment of calm, the Anglesey Coastal Path is ready when you are!
