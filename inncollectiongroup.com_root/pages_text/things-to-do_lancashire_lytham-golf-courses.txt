Royal Lytham & St Annes Golf Club
Royal Lytham & St Annes is one of the premier links courses in the world. The course has hosted eleven Open Championships and two Ryder Cups, to name a few.
The 18 hole course is surrounded by a railway line and suburban housing, and only a short while away from the sea.

Fairhaven Golf Club
Originally a 12-hole course close by to Fairhaven Lake in 1892 and founded as a club in 1895. The club then moved half a mile to its current location in 1924.
Fairhaven Golf Club is not only is it a beautiful place to play golf, but lives up to its reputation as a championship course.

Lytham Green Drive Golf Course
Situated on the beautiful Fylde Coast, the Lytham Green Drive Club was founded in 1913. It is described as an easy walking pheasant parkland course with no strenuous hills.
The course has a reputation for being the friendliest, most sociable golf club on the Flyde. Rest assured you will receive a warm welcome and friendly atmosphere for your perfect game of golf.

St. Annes Old Links Golf Club
Founded in 1901 it is one of the fine championship courses of England’s north-west coast.
They hosted Final Qualifying for The Open on several occasions most recently from 2018 to 2022, as well as qualifying for the Women’s British Open and the Senior Open.
