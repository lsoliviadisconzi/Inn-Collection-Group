National White Water Rafting Centre
The National White-Water Centre, located in Snowdonia National Park, has been providing some of the best white water rafting experiences in the UK for over 40 years.
White Water Rafting
Offering a range of activities, including rafting, rapid sessions, canyoning, and safari adventures, the centre also hosts numerous competitions throughout the year. Thanks to the controlled flow of the Tryweryn River, water levels remain excellent throughout the year, so you can be assured that rain won’t disrupt your adventure.
