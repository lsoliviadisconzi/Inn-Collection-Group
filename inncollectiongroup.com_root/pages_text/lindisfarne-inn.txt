The nearest mainland pub to Holy Island, the ward-winning Lindisfarne Inn is perfectly placed for exploring Northumberland and for forays into Scotland. With great facilities for walkers and cyclists, guests can enjoy a warm welcome and superb home-cooked meals at The Lindisfarne Inn, conveniently located just off the A1, south of Berwick-upon-Tweed and close to the Scottish Border.
Open all day, every day, this traditional inn is an ideal place to pause and enjoy excellent pub food, refreshing drinks, or a relaxing night’s sleep. Whether your stay is fleeting or part of a longer pilgrimage, you’ll fit right inn thanks to the cosy atmosphere of the pub, while our comfortable rooms offer all the amenities you’d expect, including en-suite bathrooms and flatscreen TVs.
You’ll also enjoy a true taste of Northumberland at The Lindisfarne Inn, with local flavours running throughout the menus and drinks list – including the world-famous Lindisfarne mead and a selection of local ales.

Dog Friendly Hotel near Lindisfarne
The Lindisfarne Inn is a dog friendly hotel near Holy Island, with dog-friendly rooms where your four-legged friend can join you for a Northumberland getaway. Just add your dog to your booking and we’ll make sure you’re allocated one of our dog-friendly rooms.
When you book direct, up to two dogs can stay with you for £15 per night. Dogs are also welcome in our designated dog-friendly areas of the pub and restaurant, with water bowls and treats ready and waiting. Please remember that dogs can’t be left unattended in bedrooms during your stay, but can join you every step of the way.
When it’s time to get those paws moving, you’re just moments from the Holy Island causeway and Northumberland coast, with plenty of fresh sea air and coastal scenery to enjoy together. After a day exploring, head back to the inn for some well-earned food and drinks.
Whether you’re looking for dog friendly places to stay near Lindisfarne or a dog friendly pub with rooms near Holy Island, The Lindisfarne Inn is a handy base for exploring the Northumberland coast together.

The Lindisfarne Inn FAQs
Make your trip to The Lindisfarne Inn, Berwick, smooth and stress-free by exploring our FAQs. You’ll find helpful information about location, parking options, accessibility, check-in times and guest amenities.
Do you have on site parking available?
There is free on site car paking available at The Lindisfarne Inn. Parking is first come first served.
Close
What time is Check in and Check out?
Check in at The Lindisfarne Inn is from 3pm and check out is 10:30am. If you wish to check in after 11pm there will be an automatic late check in fee of £25.
Close
Is there disabled access and accessible rooms at the inn?
There is an accessible ramp available via the main door to The Lindisfarne Inn. There is one ground floor accessible room available at The Lindisfarne Inn.
Please note the total height of the bed and the mattress approximately 70cm.
For further information please call our reservations team.

Are you dog-friendly?
Your dogs are more than welcome at The Lindisfarne Inn! We have designated dog-friendly areas of the pub and restaurant, with water bowls and treats available – including our selection of Sir Woofchester’s dog food range.
We also offer pet-friendly hotel rooms across all room categories at The Lindisfarne Inn.
Please let us know whether you will be bringing a pet at the time of booking to ensure availability. When you book direct, up to two of your four-legged friend can be added to your booking for £15 per night. Another perk of booking direct with us at The Inn Collection Group!
For more information on our pet-friendly accommodation, please contact our central reservations team on 0191 580 3610.

What is the cancellation policy?
Our standard cancellation policy is as follows:
You may cancel your booking without any payment to us at any point before 3pm on the day that is two days before the day your booking starts. After this deadline, the whole booking becomes fully non-refundable. If you try to cancel after this deadline (or if you don’t arrive for your booking), you will still be charged the full price for your booking (including any meals or extras).
Our Advance Purchase Rate cancellation policy is as follows: All bookings on an advance purchase rate are non-refundable and cannot be cancelled by you. If you try to cancel a booking on an advance purchase rate (or if you don’t arrive for your booking), you will still be charged the full price for your booking (including any meals or extras).
Our special offers/promotions cancellation policy is as follows: The cancellation rights for bookings offered on a special offer will be stated during the booking process and in your booking confirmation. Where we do not state any cancellation rights for a special offer, the advance purchase rate cancellation terms (above) will apply.

Do you have any on site bike storage?
There is on site bike storage and bike tool kit/cleaning station available at The Lindisfarne Inn.
Please note E-bikes and e-scooters are permitted on our premises provided the battery has been removed before arrival and is stored at least 10 metres away from the building. E-bikes and e-scooters must not be charged anywhere on our premises.
We are unable to provide storage facilities for e-bike or e-scooter batteries. The bike or scooter may be stored on site without its battery fitted.
