Alnwick Markets
On market days you can buy local produce, find a bargain and enjoy the genial atmosphere in the Market Place. It is a way of life for many local people who come each week and for visitors it can be an enjoyable part of your day out in Alnwick.
Taking place each Friday and Saturday from 9am until 4pm in the heart of the town there are a wide range of stalls selling quirky gifts, plants, local produce and clothing. A Farmer’s Market takes place on the last Friday of the month.
