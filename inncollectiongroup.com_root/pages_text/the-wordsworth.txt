The Wordsworth is NOW OPEN!
Set in the very heart of Grasmere, one of the most sought-after villages in the Lake District, The Wordsworth is being thoughtfully reimagined following a multi-million-pound refurbishment. Surrounded by iconic fells, peaceful lakes and some of the most photographed landscapes in Cumbria, this much-loved hotel in Grasmere returns as a standout destination for those looking to truly switch off and soak up the beauty of the national park.
Perfectly placed for exploring everything the Lake District has to offer, The Wordsworth puts you just moments from scenic walking routes, traditional tearooms and the shores of Grasmere itself. Whether you’re planning a romantic Lake District getaway, a walking holiday filled with fell-top views, or a relaxed UK staycation, this is a location that consistently ranks among the best places to stay in the Lakes.
From cosy corners to refuel after a day on the fells, to stylish bedrooms ideal for longer stays, The Wordsworth will offer a welcoming base for couples, families and those searching for dog-friendly hotels in Grasmere and the wider Lake District.

What is the cancellation policy?
Our standard cancellation policy is as follows:
You may cancel your booking without any payment to us at any point before 3pm on the day that is two days before the day your booking starts. After this deadline, the whole booking becomes fully non-refundable. If you try to cancel after this deadline (or if you don’t arrive for your booking), you will still be charged the full price for your booking (including any meals or extras).
Our Advance Purchase Rate cancellation policy is as follows: All bookings on an advance purchase rate are non-refundable and cannot be cancelled by you. If you try to cancel a booking on an advance purchase rate (or if you don’t arrive for your booking), you will still be charged the full price for your booking (including any meals or extras).
Our special offers/promotions cancellation policy is as follows: The cancellation rights for bookings offered on a special offer will be stated during the booking process and in your booking confirmation. Where we do not state any cancellation rights for a special offer, the advance purchase rate cancellation terms (above) will apply.

Do you have any on site bike storage?
There is no bike storage available on site at The Wordsworth.
Please note E-bikes and e-scooters are permitted on our premises provided the battery has been removed before arrival and is stored at least 10 metres away from the building. E-bikes and e-scooters must not be charged anywhere on our premises.
We are unable to provide storage facilities for e-bike or e-scooter batteries. The bike or scooter may be stored on site without its battery fitted.

Explore what's near The Wordsworth, Grasmere
Picturesque Grasmere sits in the heart of the Lake District and it’s every bit as impressive as you’d expect. Think dramatic fells, glassy lakes, and walking routes that range from gentle lakeside loops to proper leg burners with views worth every step. Add in stone-built villages, cosy pubs and a deep literary history, and you’ve got one of the UK’s most loved places to escape to.
Staying at The Wordsworth puts you in a strong spot for exploring it all. You’re located in the heart of Grasmere village, the famous lake itself is on your doorstep, as are some of the most popular things to do in Grasmere, whether that’s wandering independent shops, visiting local attractions, or heading out on nearby hiking routes straight into the fells. It’s also a great base for ticking off wider Lake District highlights without having to spend your break in the car.
From here, it’s easy to keep things simple - slow mornings, big views, and proper time in the heart of the Lake District National Park.
