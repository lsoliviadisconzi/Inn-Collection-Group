Occupying a prime position on the St Annes on Sea beach, Offshore is a new-look destination, where our hotel in Lytham St Annes has an unbeatable seafront location on the south promenade. Look forward to fantastic home-made food, a wide range of drinks, fabulous accommodation and a very warm welcome in the vibrant coastal town.
Born out of a total refurbishment and remodelling of the former Carlton and Lindum Hotels, Offshore’s 98 bedrooms (which include premium sea-view rooms, dog-friendly rooms and accessible accommodation) are perfectly placed to serve guests wishing to enjoy this special part of Lancashire.
The completely re-imagined bar area and large outdoor seating terrace offer laid-back dining and delicious drinks and are where you’ll fit right inn as you unwind and enjoy the views across the Fylde coast.
Please note there is very limited on site parking. Free on street parking is available as well as pay and display car parks close by, or the Blackpool Tramway runs through St Annes for car-free guests.

Do you have on site parking available at Offshore?
There is very limited on site parking. Free on street parking is available as well as pay and display car parks close by.

Is there disabled access and accessible rooms at the inn?
There is disabled access into Offshore from the ramp at the front side entrance of the inn. There are three ground floor accessible rooms available at Offshore, all rooms have wet-rooms in the bathroom.
Please note the total height of the bed and the mattress approximately 70cm.
For further accessibility information please call our reservations team.

Is Offshore dog-friendly?
Your dogs are more than welcome at Offshore! We have designated dog-friendly areas of the pub and restaurant, with water bowls and treats available – including our selection of Sir Woofchester’s dog food range.
We offer pet-friendly hotel rooms, so your pets can join you for overnight stays with us. Please let us know whether you will be bringing a pet at the time of booking to ensure availability and so we can allocate you accordingly to our pet-friendly rooms.
When you book direct, up to two of your four-legged friends can be added to your booking for £15 per night. Another perk of booking direct with us at The Inn Collection Group!
For more information on our pet-friendly accommodation, please contact our central reservations team on 0191 580 3610.

What is the cancellation policy?
Our standard cancellation policy is as follows:
You may cancel your booking without any payment to us at any point before 3pm on the day that is two days before the day your booking starts. After this deadline, the whole booking becomes fully non-refundable. If you try to cancel after this deadline (or if you don’t arrive for your booking), you will still be charged the full price for your booking (including any meals or extras).
Our Advance Purchase Rate cancellation policy is as follows: All bookings on an advance purchase rate are non-refundable and cannot be cancelled by you. If you try to cancel a booking on an advance purchase rate (or if you don’t arrive for your booking), you will still be charged the full price for your booking (including any meals or extras).
Our special offers/promotions cancellation policy is as follows: The cancellation rights for bookings offered on a special offer will be stated during the booking process and in your booking confirmation. Where we do not state any cancellation rights for a special offer, the advance purchase rate cancellation terms (above) will apply.

Do you have any on site bike storage?
There is no bike storage available on site at Offshore.
Please note E-bikes and e-scooters are permitted on our premises provided the battery has been removed before arrival and is stored at least 10 metres away from the building. E-bikes and e-scooters must not be charged anywhere on our premises.
We are unable to provide storage facilities for e-bike or e-scooter batteries. The bike or scooter may be stored on site without its battery fitted.

Can I get to St Annes by tram from Blackpool?
Yes. The Blackpool Tramway runs between Blackpool and Starr Gate, where you can connect to St Annes by bus, taxi or a walk which would take around 20-30 minutes. It’s a convenient way to visit both resorts during your stay, allowing you to experience Blackpool’s lively promenade before unwinding back in St Annes.
Close
Is Lytham St Annes near Blackpool?
Lytham St Annes is located just south of Blackpool, with the two destinations around 15-20 minutes apart by car. It’s easy to travel between them by train, bus or the famous Blackpool tram, making it simple to enjoy Blackpool’s attractions before returning to the quieter coastal charm of St Annes.
Close
Is St Annes Pier near Offshore?
Offshore is just a short stroll from St Annes Pier, making it an ideal base for exploring the seafront. In around 5 minutes on foot, you can reach the Victorian pier, sandy beach, amusements, cafés and scenic promenade.
