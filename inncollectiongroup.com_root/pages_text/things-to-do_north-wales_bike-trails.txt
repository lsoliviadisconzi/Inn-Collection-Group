Bike Trails
Take your exploration round North Wales on wheels!
Bike trail
Ready to see North Wales like never before? Hop on your bike and explore a region made for cycling - where every turn offers a new view, and every stop is filled with charm and a warm Welsh welcome. With rich history, breath-taking landscapes, and a vibrant culture, there’s no better way to discover it all than on two wheels.
Looking for a family-friendly ride? Try the Lon Las Cefni Path, a traffic-free route through nature reserves and along the coastline. Or, for something a bit more challenging, tackle the Copper Trail on the North Anglesey Cycle Route, set in stunning forests with a dash of mountain adventure.
wales bike trails
wales bike trails
No matter which route you choose, be sure to stop and explore historic towns, ancient castles, cosy cafes, and hidden viewpoints that make every ride unforgettable. From flat, scenic paths to off-the-beaten-track forest trails, there’s something for every cyclist.
Grab your bike and start your adventure in North Wales, where there's beauty around every bend!
