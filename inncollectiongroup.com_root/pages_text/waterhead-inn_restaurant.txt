Looking for the best restaurant in Ambleside with unbeatable Lake Windermere views? The Waterhead Inn serves freshly prepared Cumbrian and British pub classics, with every dish packed full of local provenance and flavour. From hearty pies and fresh fish to seasonal favourites, our menu celebrates the very best of pub food in the Lake District.
Start the day with a full Cumbrian breakfast, whether you’re staying with us or just popping in to enjoy breakfast with a view. Brunches and lunches are perfect for relaxing by the lake, and our evening meals – complete with the most indulgent puddings – are not to be missed as the sun sets over Windermere.
With a welcoming beer garden and cosy indoor seating, The Waterhead Inn is the ideal spot to enjoy great food, drinks, and lakeside charm. Walk-ins are always welcome, so simply find your perfect table and soak in one of the most stunning settings in Ambleside.

Looking for dog friendly pubs near Lake Windermere?
You don’t have to be staying at the Waterhead Inn to enjoy a meal with your four-legged friend. Our dog-friendly areas are open to everyone, whether you’re dropping in after a lakeside walk or settling down for a leisurely meal. Your dog can join you with water bowls and treats on hand, while Sir Woofchester’s dog food range means they can enjoy a tasty treat too. For the humans, there’s plenty to tuck into, from classic pub favourites and all-day dining to Sunday lunch and something refreshing from the bar.
With the shores of Lake Windermere and some of the Lake District’s best-loved walking routes close by, it’s easy to work up an appetite together before settling in for food and drinks.
So, whether you’re looking for dog friendly pubs in Ambleside, somewhere to refuel after a walk or a relaxed spot where the whole family can settle in, pull up a chair and make yourselves at home. Good food, great company and happy hounds - sounds like a pretty good pit stop to us.

Our delicious pub food
At The Waterhead Inn, we keep things simple, hearty, and full of flavour, serving classic pub food in the Lake District that everyone loves. From pies and juicy burgers to freshly caught fish from local fishermen, every dish uses the freshest, locally sourced ingredients. Seasonal vegetables feature throughout our menus, which are updated regularly to make the most of what’s fresh and in season.
Our seasonal menu is at the heart of what we do, offering a wide range of dishes that showcase the best of Cumbrian produce and traditional flavours, perfect for lunch or dinner with Lake Windermere views. Families are always welcome, and our thoughtfully crafted children’s menu ensures that younger guests have plenty of tasty, fun options that balance flavour with nutrition.
