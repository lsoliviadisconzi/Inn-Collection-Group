New family rooms unveiled
Transforming a peaceful adjacent cottage alongside the main building into six, spacious family rooms, The Seaton Lane Inn offers quality yet affordable accommodation for families looking to visit Seaton, Seaham and Durham’s striking Heritage Coastline.
Just the place to return to after a day’s exploring Durham’s Heritage Coastline, a trip to the football or visiting family and friends, The Seaton Lane Inn’s comfortable family rooms have king size beds that can be twinned, bunk beds and an en suite bathroom.
General manager Debbie Brown said: “There is a real shortage of family rooms in the area for people whether they are holidaying here, perhaps going to a wedding at nearby Seaham Hall, visiting friends or family or going to the football. As a mum myself, I know how it makes all the difference having somewhere comfortable and secure where you can all stay together in as a family.”
The Seaton Lane Inn’s new family rooms are available to book
online
or by calling the booking hotline 0191 5803610, seven days a week.
