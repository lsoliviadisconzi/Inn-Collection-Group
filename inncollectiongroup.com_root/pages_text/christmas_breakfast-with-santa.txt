Start Times
All Breakfast with Santa events begin at 10am, except The Commissioners Quay Inn at 9.30am and The Amble Inn at 10.30am.

Full payment required upon booking.
Allergies & Special Guests
Please inform us of any allergies at the time of booking. Santa and special guests will be present for approximately one hour during the event.
Event Details & Availability
Events may differ by site, and there is limited availability per event. Book early to secure your space.

Buffet breakfast is provided instead of a plated breakfast at select inns marked with **, and the menu may differ slightly between inns. Please contact your chosen inn for full menu details and allergen information.
