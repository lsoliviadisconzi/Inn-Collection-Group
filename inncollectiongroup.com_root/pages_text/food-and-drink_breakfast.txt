What's included with our Full English breakfast?
What’s in our Full English? All the good stuff, of course! Our freshly cooked fry-up comes loaded with back bacon, pork sausages, eggs your way, baked beans, grilled tomato, mushrooms, crispy hash browns and black pudding. Everything is cooked fresh to order, using quality ingredients sourced as locally as possible.
And we’re big on knowing where our food comes from. Our in-house butchery at The Links gives us genuine provenance for our meat, helping bring quality from the butcher’s block to your breakfast plate. So if you’re looking for a full English breakfast near me, you can expect a proper, freshly cooked breakfast worth getting out of bed for.

Looking for a vegetarian breakfast? Our vegetarian Full English is packed with all the breakfast favourites, just without the meat. Tuck into Vegan sausage, your choice of egg (fried, poached or scrambled), baked beans, hash browns, mushrooms and grilled tomato, all freshly cooked to order.
