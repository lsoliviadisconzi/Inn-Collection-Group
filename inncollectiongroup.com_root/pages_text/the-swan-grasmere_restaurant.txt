Whether you’re lingering over a lazy breakfast or lunch, stopping off from sightseeing, or feasting on a hearty plate of delicious fare after scaling a summit, The Swan is ideally placed to drop by for flavour-packed Cumbria pub classics all day, every day.

Looking for dog friendly pubs in Grasmere?
You don't need to stay overnight to enjoy a visit to The Swan! Our dog-friendly dining area is open to everyone, whether you're visiting us for a refreshment after a walk or settling in for a leisurely meal. Dogs can join you in our designated dog-friendly pub and restaurant areas, with water bowls and treats ready and waiting. Feeling peckish? Sir Woofchester’s dog food range means they can tuck into something tasty whilst the humans get stuck into classic pub favourites, all-day dining, Sunday lunch and local cask ales.
With Grasmere just a short walk away, you can head out in the fresh air, take in the views and sniff out some new adventures before settling in for food and drinks.
So whether you’re looking for dog friendly pubs in Grasmere, somewhere to refuel after a walk or simply a spot where the whole pack can relax, pull up a chair and stay awhile. Good food. Good drinks. Wagging tails. What’s not to like?

The best pub food in the Lake District
If you’re looking for the best places to eat in Grasmere, The Swan at Grasmere is the perfect choice. Set in the heart of the Lake District, our historic coaching inn combines charming countryside views with delicious, locally inspired dishes. From comforting pub classics and hearty burgers to freshly prepared seasonal specials, our menu celebrates the best of Cumbrian produce.
We’re proud to use seasonal ingredients and support local suppliers, with menus that change throughout the year. Families are warmly welcomed too, with a tasty children’s menu designed for our younger guests. Whether you’re after a relaxed lunch, a cosy dinner by the fire, or a pint enjoyed in our beautiful garden with views of the fells, The Swan at Grasmere is one of the top places to eat and drink in Grasmere. Walk-ins are always welcome.
