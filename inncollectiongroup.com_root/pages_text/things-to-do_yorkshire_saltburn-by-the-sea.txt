Saltburn-by-the-Sea, commonly referred to as Saltburn, is a seaside town in Redcar and Cleveland in North Yorkshire. Home to the oldest water balanced cliff lift in the country. Opened in June 1884, Saltburn Cliff Lift goes up 37 meters to connect the pier and seafront to the town, while at the same time offering extensive views over the coastline and the North Sea.
saltburn 3
Saltburn beach is a sand and shingle affair backed by a promenade and with a wide range of facilities close by. The beach is also home to one of the best surfing spots along this stretch of North Sea coast.
