Conwy Valley Railway Museum
The Conwy Valley Railway Museum is a small but charming railway attraction combining a museum, working miniature railway, and hobby shop. This attraction is ideal for families and train enthusiasts exploring North Wales.
Conwy Valley Railway Museum 2
If you're exploring Snowdonia National Park or staying in the charming village of Betws-y-coed, make sure to add the Conwy Valley Railway Museum to your itinerary. Tucked beside the village's historic railway station , this unique attraction offers a fun and fascinating experience for families, railway enthusiasts, and anyone looking for something a little different during their North Wales getaway.
Conwy Valley Railway Museum
Things you can do whilst you visit:
- Ride the miniature railway
- Take a trip on the electric tram
- Step into railway history
- On-site model railway shop
