Ashton Gardens
Ashton Gardens is a 10 acre park, it welcomes guests and visitors to enjoy the beautiful land that is filled with plants, trees and flowers.
Located in the heart of St Annes, this park is situated within a lovely town centre and not too far back from the seaside. Providing the perfect walk about for your visit to St Annes.
The park is home to many featured attractions, such as The Pavilion tea rooms and restaurant, superb bowling greens and a galleon climbing frame perfect for a pitstop with the children.
