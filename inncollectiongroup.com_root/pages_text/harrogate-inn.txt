For the perfect place to enjoy a break away with friends or family, or business trip in Harrogate look no further than The Harrogate Inn.
Winner of the regional Pub & Bar of the Year award, this inn really hits the spot for those who enjoy great food and drink in stunning surroundings.
Set on famous Parliament Hill, The Harrogate Inn is surrounded by Yorkshire gems including Betty’s tearooms, the Royal Hall and Turkish Baths while Harrogate Convention Centre is on the doorstep.
Not only can you enjoy the buzz of Harrogate’s vibrant shops and cafes including the exclusive Montpellier Quarter, this classic inn is just a short drive from the remote beauty of the Yorkshire Moors and some of the most breathtaking countryside in the country.
Our onsite pub and restaurant is the perfect spot to relax and enjoy traditional pub classics with an elevated twist. Grab a cosy corner and indulge!
With Harrogate’s train station nearby and well served with bus routes, The Harrogate Inn is also ideal for a car-free break away.

Dog Friendly Hotel in Harrogate
The Harrogate Inn is a dog friendly hotel where you can explore the town of Harrogate with all the family! We have dog-friendly rooms across all room categories so you can settle in together. Just add your dog when booking and we’ll take care of the rest.
When you book direct, up to two dogs can stay for £15 per night. They’re also welcome in our designated dog-friendly pub and restaurant areas, with water bowls and treats ready and waiting. Dogs can’t be left unattended in bedrooms during your stay, but can join you every step of the way!
You can spend your getaway with your dog, exploring the town, taking in the local sights and finding a few new walking spots before settling in for some well-earned refreshments.
Wherever the day takes you, The Harrogate Inn gives you and your pooch a welcoming place to relax, refuel and put your feet (and paws!) up.

The Harrogate Inn FAQs
Visiting The Harrogate Inn, just steps from Harrogate’s shops and attractions? Our FAQs cover everything you need to know about getting here, parking, accessibility, check-in times and the facilities available throughout your stay.
Do you have on site parking available?
There is chargeable on site car parking available at The Harrogate Inn – £10 per night. Parking is limited and cannot be reserved so operates on first come first served basis.
Close
What time is Check in and Check out?
Check in at The Harrogate Inn is from 3pm and check out is 10:30am.
Close
Is there disabled access and accessible rooms at the inn?
There is disabled access available into The Harrogate Inn via the car park and the ground floor is fully accessible. There is one accessible room available at The Harrogate Inn.
Please note the total height of the bed and the mattress approximately 70cm.
There are three accessible parking spaces charged at £10 per night. Please note parking cannot be pre-booked. For further accessibility information please call our reservations team.

Are you dog-friendly?
Your dogs are more than welcome at The Harrogate Inn! We have designated dog-friendly areas of the pub and restaurant, with water bowls and treats available – including our selection of Sir Woofchester’s dog food range.
We also offer a select number of pet-friendly hotel rooms, so you can bring your dogs for overnight stays. Please let us know whether you will be bringing a pet at the time of booking to ensure availability and so we can allocate you accordingly.
When you book direct, up to two of your four-legged friend can be added to your booking for £15 per night. Another perk of booking direct with us at The Inn Collection Group!
For more information on our pet-friendly accommodation, please contact our central reservations team on 0191 580 3610.

What is the cancellation policy?
Our standard cancellation policy is as follows:
You may cancel your booking without any payment to us at any point before 3pm on the day that is two days before the day your booking starts. After this deadline, the whole booking becomes fully non-refundable. If you try to cancel after this deadline (or if you don’t arrive for your booking), you will still be charged the full price for your booking (including any meals or extras).
Our Advance Purchase Rate cancellation policy is as follows: All bookings on an advance purchase rate are non-refundable and cannot be cancelled by you. If you try to cancel a booking on an advance purchase rate (or if you don’t arrive for your booking), you will still be charged the full price for your booking (including any meals or extras).
Our special offers/promotions cancellation policy is as follows: The cancellation rights for bookings offered on a special offer will be stated during the booking process and in your booking confirmation. Where we do not state any cancellation rights for a special offer, the advance purchase rate cancellation terms (above) will apply.

Do you have any on site bike storage?
There is no on site bike storage available at The Harrogate Inn.
Please note E-bikes and e-scooters are permitted on our premises provided the battery has been removed before arrival and is stored at least 10 metres away from the building. E-bikes and e-scooters must not be charged anywhere on our premises.
We are unable to provide storage facilities for e-bike or e-scooter batteries. The bike or scooter may be stored on site without its battery fitted.

Explore what's near Harrogate, North Yorkshire
From stunning gardens and elegant architecture to boutique shopping, cosy cafés, and historic attractions, Harrogate truly has something for everyone. Whether you’re planning a weekend getaway, a family break, or a romantic stay in this charming North Yorkshire spa town, you’ll find endless ways to make the most of your time here. Stroll through the beautiful Valley Gardens, relax with an indulgent spa experience at the Turkish Baths Harrogate, or explore the independent shops and bustling markets that give the town its unique character. Food lovers will be spoiled for choice too, with everything from award-winning restaurants to traditional Yorkshire tearooms.
