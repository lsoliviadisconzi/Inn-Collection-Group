Valley Gardens
An English Heritage Grade II Listed gardens situated in regal Low Harrogate.
ebun-oluwole-KB-B7WJmvfw-unsplash
The Valley Gardens Harrogate, a charming English Heritage Grade II Listed site in elegant Low Harrogate, cover 17 acres along with woodland known as The Pinewoods. Discover an abundance of mineral springs unparalleled elsewhere at The Valley Gardens Harrogate – explore Bogs Field where 36 unique mineral wells were found.
Constant restoration led by Friends of Valley Gardens with Harrogate Borough Council keeps the park amazing. Open year-round, these gardens offer diverse styles, perfect for a relaxing exploration. Enjoy Sunday afternoon band concerts in summer for music lovers. For outdoor fun, there's tennis, pitch & putt golf, crazy golf, boating and paddling pools, plus a children's play area.
