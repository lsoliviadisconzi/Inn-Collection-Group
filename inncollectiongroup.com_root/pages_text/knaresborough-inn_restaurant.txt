Dine at The Knaresborough Inn
+ 5 items
Nothing beats tucking into proper, home-cooked food made with fresh, local ingredients – and at The Knaresborough Inn, that’s exactly what you’ll find. Nestled in the quaint town of Knaresborough, our menu is packed with hearty pub favourites and modern dishes, all showcasing seasonal flavours and a true taste of Yorkshire.
Whether you’re a visitor or a local, you’ll always find a cosy corner to relax in, whether start the day with a generous breakfast, enjoy a leisurely lunch, or unwind over an evening meal served until 9pm. From light-bites with friends to catch up to a comforting dinner for two, The Knaresborough Inn is the perfect spot to enjoy great food, good company, and the charm of this picturesque market town.

Looking for dog friendly pubs in Knaresborough?
You don't need to stay overnight to enjoy a visit to The Knaresborough Inn! Our dog-friendly dining area is open to everyone, whether you're visiting us for a refreshment after a walk or settling in for a leisurely meal. Dogs can join you in our designated dog-friendly pub and restaurant areas, with water bowls and treats ready and waiting. Feeling peckish? Sir Woofchester’s dog food range means they can tuck into something tasty whilst the humans get stuck into classic pub favourites, all-day dining, Sunday lunch and local cask ales.
Knaresborough is a great place to explore on four paws, with the river and plenty of walking routes nearby. Once you’ve worked up an appetite, come back to the inn and put your feet (and paws!) up and relax in style.
So if you’re looking for dog friendly pubs in Knaresborough, pull up a chair, tuck in, and stay awhile. Good food. Good drinks. Wagging tails. All we're waiting for is you!
