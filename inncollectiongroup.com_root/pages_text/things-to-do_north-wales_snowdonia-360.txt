What is Snowdonia 360?
Snowdonia 360 is a 360 mile circular route taking you through scenic trails and some of North Wales' best spots.
You'll head through well-known areas including Snowdonia, Anglesey, Llŷn Peninsula and the North Wales coastline, where you can experience stunning hikes, mountain landscapes as you drive through, and can stop off along the way to take in the vast Welsh history and culture.
Snowdonia
Best stops on the Snowdonia 360 roadtrip
The 360 mile route is brimming with attractions just waiting to be explored! Some of the key areas the route passes through include:
- North Wales Coast: view the cliffs of Great Orme at Llandudno, discover the fortress of Conwy castle, and browse the many Victorian seaside towns.
- Anglesey - Stop in Beaumaris steeped in history, view the iconic South Stack Lighthouse, and take a dip in the refreshing Anglesey beaches.
- Llŷn Peninsula - a former AONB, this 30-mile stretch of coast is brimming full of beaches, hikes and wildlife, perfect for outdoor enthusiasts.
- North Snowdonia - Discover Betws-y-Coed, known as the 'gateaway to Snowdonia', as you begin to take on hikes and bike trails in this stunning national park.
- South Snowdonia - Immerse yourselves in the Welsh scenery as you become surounded by waterfalls, valleys and estuaries.

How to plan your Snowdonia 360 route
Whether you'll be taking your car, experiencing the trip with more space in a campervan, or battling the elements by bike, the Snowdonia 360 is waiting for you!
The different areas of Welsh scenery the route passes all offer something different, and the route can be customised depending on your preferences and time available.
We would recommend viewing the Snowdonia 360 website - which has a useful customisable route planner to help start your journey!
So, what are you waiting for? Get planning your North Wales road trip today!
