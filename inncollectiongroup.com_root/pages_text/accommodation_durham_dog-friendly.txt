The City at Your Fingertips
When you stay at The Kingslodge Inn you’re in a unique position, as there aren’t many dog-friendly hotels in Durham city. That means you can enjoy all of the historical landmarks and heritage, with quick access to the stunning surroundings. All with your beloved dog in tow.
So what should you and your pooch get up to in Durham? Here are some of the top sights to see.
Durham Cathedral
has to make an appearance during your visit to the city. After all, it’s over a thousand years old and a protected
UNESCO World Heritage site
. How often do you get a chance to see a sight like that in a lifetime?
Want to dig deeper into Durham’s rich history? Take a guided tour of Durham Castle to find out all about how this incredible building dates back to the 11th Century and was originally built as a Norman fortress.
Top off your weekend with an
evening cruise
on the Prince Bishop River Cruiser to see how Durham twinkles from the water. Sip a drink as you take in this ancient city and all it has to offer.
And don’t worry, we’ve already checked - dogs are as welcome on board as they are in our dog-friendly accommodation in Durham.
