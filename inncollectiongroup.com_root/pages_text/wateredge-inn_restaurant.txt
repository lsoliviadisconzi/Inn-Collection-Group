Dine at The Wateredge Inn
+ 8 items
After soaking up the Lake District’s stunning scenery, there’s no better spot to eat and drink in Ambleside than The Wateredge Inn. Right on the shores of Lake Windermere, our pub’s got everything from hearty breakfasts to laid-back lunches and evening meals that really hit the spot.
When the sun’s out, pull up a chair in our spacious beer garden by the lake, but when the weather turns cooler, head inside and warm up by the fire in our cosy pub – the perfect place to unwind with friends, family, or even the dog.
Whether you’re grabbing a light snack, tucking into classic pub favourites, or just stopping by for a pint with a view, you won’t find a better backdrop in Ambleside. Serving tasty food and tempting drinks all day, every day, The Wateredge Inn is the go-to pub in Ambleside when you want great food, a proper pint, and those unbeatable lakeside views.

Looking for dog friendly pubs near Lake Windermere?
You don't need to stay overnight to enjoy a visit to The Wateredge Inn! Our dog-friendly dining area is open to everyone, whether you're visiting us for a refreshment after a walk or settling in for a leisurely meal. Dogs can join you in our designated dog-friendly pub and restaurant areas, with water bowls and treats ready and waiting. Feeling peckish? Sir Woofchester’s dog food range means they can tuck into something tasty whilst the humans get stuck into classic pub favourites, all-day dining, Sunday lunch and local cask ales.
With the banks of Lake Windermere straight outside our suntrap beer garden and plenty of trails to sniff out, you'll have plenty to explore before settling in for some food and drinks.
So whether you’re looking for dog friendly pubs near Lake Windermere or somewhere to refuel after a walk, pull up a chair and stay awhile. Good food. Good drinks. Wagging tails. What’s not to love?

Our delicious pub food
If you’re looking for the best places to eat in Ambleside, The Wateredge Inn is the perfect spot. Nestled on the shores of Lake Windermere, our pub combines stunning lakeside views with hearty, locally sourced dishes. From classic pies and juicy burgers to freshly prepared fish, our menu showcases the best of pub food in the Lake District.
We focus on seasonal ingredients and local produce, with menus that change throughout the year. Families are welcome too, with a fun, tasty children’s menu designed for young diners. Whether you’re after a relaxed lunch, a cosy dinner by the fire, or a pint in our beer garden in Ambleside, The Wateredge Inn is one of the top restaurants in Ambleside for food, drinks, and lakeside charm. Walk-ins are always welcome.
