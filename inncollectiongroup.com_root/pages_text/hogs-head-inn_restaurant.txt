Dine at The Hog’s Head Inn
+ 5 items
After a day discovering the history and charm of Alnwick, The Hog’s Head Inn is the perfect place to relax and refuel. Take a seat in our cosy pub area, or make the most of our terraced outdoor space – perfect for soaking up the sun. Our menu caters for every appetite, from light bites and comforting classics to hearty main courses, all freshly prepared with quality ingredients.
Sundays are extra special here, when our carvery has become a favourite with locals and visitors alike. Enjoy generous servings of hand-carved British beef, pork, and poultry, or a delicious meat-free option, all served with the traditional trimmings that make Sunday dining such a treat.
To keep things fresh, our changing seasonal menus and chef’s specials highlight the best seasonal produce Northumberland has to offer, from game sourced from the Northumberland Estates to seafood and shellfish from nearby shores. Whether you’re gathering with family, meeting friends, or simply looking for a cosy spot to unwind, dining at The Hog’s Head Inn is an experience rooted in great food and a warm welcome.

Looking for dog friendly pubs in Alnwick?
You don't need to stay overnight to enjoy a visit to The Hog's Head Inn! Our dog-friendly dining area is open to everyone, whether you're visiting us for a refreshment after a walk or settling in for a leisurely meal. Dogs can join you in our designated dog-friendly pub and restaurant areas, with water bowls and treats ready and waiting. Feeling peckish? Sir Woofchester’s dog food range means they can tuck into something tasty whilst the humans get stuck into classic pub favourites, all-day dining, Sunday lunch and local cask ales.
From Alnwick’s historic town centre to the open countryside beyond, there’s plenty for curious paws to sniff out. Make a day of exploring before heading back to the inn to put your feet (and paws!) up over some tasty food and drinks.
Whether you’re looking for dog friendly pubs in Alnwick orsomewhere to refuel after a walk, pull up a chair and stay awhile. Good food. Good drinks. Wagging tails. All that's waiting is for you to tuck-in!
