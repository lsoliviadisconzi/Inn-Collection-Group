Dine at The Ripon Inn

Following a complete renovation that includes an enhanced outdoor terrace, The Ripon Inn is the newest place in the city to enjoy fabulous food and well-kept drinks. Sitting in six acres of secluded gardens, The Ripon Inn is the perfect place to sit back and unwind or to call into whilst exploring this historic part of North Yorkshire.
You’ll be spoilt for choice with a menu bursting with traditional pub classics alongside modern dishes including plenty of local ingredients and seasonal flavours in every mouthful. Whether you’re staying with us or not, you’re welcome to pop in and start the day with one of The Ripon Inn’s outstanding full Yorkshire breakfasts.
Freshly prepared food is served all day so you can catch up with with loved ones over brunch, lunch or make a night of it over a splendid evening meal, with food served until 9.00pm and drinks at our bar served until 11.00pm. Simply join us, pick your perfect table, and indulge.

Looking for dog friendly pubs in Ripon?
You don't need to stay overnight to enjoy a visit to The Ripon Inn! Our dog-friendly dining area is open to everyone, whether you're visiting us for a refreshment after a walk or settling in for a leisurely meal. Dogs can join you in our designated dog-friendly pub and restaurant areas, with water bowls and treats ready and waiting. Feeling peckish? Sir Woofchester’s dog food range means they can tuck into something tasty whilst the humans get stuck into classic pub favourites, all-day dining, Sunday lunch and local cask ales.
With the North York Moors and local walking routes on the doorstep, you can take your pooch out for a good stretch before settling in for some food and drinks.
So whether you’re looking for dog friendly pubs in Ripon, somewhere to refuel after a walk or simply a spot where the whole pack can relax, pull up a chair and stay awhile. We've got a seat waiting for you!
