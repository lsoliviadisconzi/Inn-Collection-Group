Combine the best of city living with the relaxed atmosphere of the countryside at this cosy gem of an inn in Durham. Surrounded by the peaceful woodland of Flass Vale, it’s hard to believe The Kingslodge Inn is a hotel in Durham city centre, just a ten-minute stroll from the historic Durham Cathedral and its adjoining castle.
Voted Best Venue in County Durham at the National Pub & Bar Awards in 2019 and named by The Times as one of the ‘20 best places to stay in the UK with a dog’, The Kingslodge Inn stands out among places to stay in Durham city, offering all the benefits of a central location but with a laid-back, restful vibe.

Dog Friendly Hotel in Durham
The Kingslodge Inn offers dog friendly accommodation in Durham, with a selection of dog-friendly rooms where your four-legged friend can join you.

Is The Kingslodge Inn in Durham city centre?

Do you have on site parking available?
If you’re searching for hotels in Durham city centre with parking, you’ll be pleased to know The Kingslodge Inn offers free parking for guests. Our car park is located at the rear of the inn. Space is limited, so availability is on a first-come, first-served basis. Please note that we can’t reserve or guarantee spaces, and overnight parking is only available for hotel guests.
Whether you’re visiting our bar, or restaurant, attending a function, or staying with us overnight, you
MUST
register your vehicle at reception.
Free parking allowances:
Diners – 2 hours FREE
Overnight Guests – 24 hours FREE for each night of your stay
Charges apply to ALL other car park users. Please remember to pay for your parking at the car park pay-and-display machine, via phone or prebook with RingGo. Overnight camping is not permitted.

Are you dog-friendly?
Your dogs are more than welcome at The Kingslodge Inn! We have designated dog-friendly areas of the pub and restaurant, with water bowls and treats available – including our selection of Sir Woofchester’s dog food range.
We also offer a limited number of pet-friendly hotel rooms, so you can bring your dogs along with you for overnight stays. Please let us know whether you will be bringing a pet at the time of booking to ensure availability and so we can allocate you accordingly.
When you book direct, up to two of your four-legged friend can be added to your booking for £15 per night. Another perk of booking direct with us at The Inn Collection Group!
For more information on our pet-friendly accommodation, please contact our central reservations team on 0191 580 3610.

What is the cancellation policy?
Our standard cancellation policy is as follows:
You may cancel your booking without any payment to us at any point before 3pm on the day that is two days before the day your booking starts. After this deadline, the whole booking becomes fully non-refundable. If you try to cancel after this deadline (or if you don’t arrive for your booking), you will still be charged the full price for your booking (including any meals or extras).
Our Advance Purchase Rate cancellation policy is as follows: All bookings on an advance purchase rate are non-refundable and cannot be cancelled by you. If you try to cancel a booking on an advance purchase rate (or if you don’t arrive for your booking), you will still be charged the full price for your booking (including any meals or extras).
Our special offers/promotions cancellation policy is as follows: The cancellation rights for bookings offered on a special offer will be stated during the booking process and in your booking confirmation. Where we do not state any cancellation rights for a special offer, the advance purchase rate cancellation terms (above) will apply.

Do you have any on site bike storage?
There is no covered on site bike storage at The Kingslodge Inn.
Please note E-bikes and e-scooters are permitted on our premises provided the battery has been removed before arrival and is stored at least 10 metres away from the building. E-bikes and e-scooters must not be charged anywhere on our premises.
We are unable to provide storage facilities for e-bike or e-scooter batteries. The bike or scooter may be stored on site without its battery fitted.
